# Anchor fidelity and controlled encounters

The anchor-return round tests a separation of reference roles: original drawing/world benchmark, compact new cast-and-equipment sheet, and editable pose/contact/geography layout. Preserve each direction's actual medium; do not import Nightglass's palette or rendering into other styles. Original worlds and drawing qualities are references, not automatically selected cast or canon.

Inspect cast sheets before using them: generated sheets can reverse anatomical markings, add alternate weapons or show contradictory front/back construction. A sheet or vector control is not proof of successful generated anatomy. Controls must vary actual scale/framing, not merely label identical side views with different camera names.

Give each scene only its current visible state. Keep later damage and outcomes out of earlier prompts. Track anatomical weapon ownership, source/contact/target, support, force direction, and persistent damage separately from excitement and appeal. Magic rules need visible limitations and consequences; captions cannot establish something absent from the artwork.

During a source-only texture finish, protect story damage, contact deformation, equipment construction and matte ground material as well as faces and silhouettes. Preserve medium-defining brush or engraving marks selectively. Reject or qualify a quieter finish that removes a meaningful effect or changes material. Keep all attempts and actual reference hashes. Independent review and owner choices remain separate from author display defaults.

Read `research/anchor-return/PIPELINE.md` for the reusable sequence and `research/anchor-return/RESULTS.md` for observed failure modes and source-preserving finishing tradeoffs. Use those examples to guide inspection, not to pre-fill the owner's preferences.
