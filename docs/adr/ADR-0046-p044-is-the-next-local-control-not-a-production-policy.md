# ADR-0046: P044 is the next local control, not a production policy

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-01

## Context

P019, P026, and P044 are explicit causal panels without local mechanics policies. P036 has exercised concavity and a 42-pixel thin reach, but not a bounded feature narrower than twice its fixed 16-pixel inward boundary. Choosing the next experiment by story appeal or motion mode would not identify the highest-information mechanics gap.

## Decision

Select P044 for one deterministic abstract topology control because its hard assertion explicitly requires hands, blade, and taut twine. Blade/twine contact can test a bounded sub-32-pixel feature under the current compositor without introducing a new renderer or diffuse-effect mechanism.

Do not create a production policy or mask from this selection. P019's separated hand-signal blocking and P026's smoke/heat boundary remain future questions. In particular, do not smuggle P026's diffuse/translucent effect into the opaque inward compositor.

## Consequences

- P044 control construction is authorized locally with abstract geometry only.
- The experiment may confirm or reject fixed 16-pixel compatibility; it cannot approve a panel input.
- P019 and P026 are deferred, not ranked as narrative panels or rejected.
- Eight/ eight inventory, order, mechanism, dimension, selection, and authority mutations fail.
