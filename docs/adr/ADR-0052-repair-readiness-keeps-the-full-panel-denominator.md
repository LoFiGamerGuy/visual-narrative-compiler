# ADR-0052: repair readiness keeps the full panel denominator

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-01

## Context

The boundary selector and v2.1 outcome contract cover only panels with exact artifacts. Reporting only P036 and P044 would hide the other 48 approved ComicPanelPlans and could make two local topology controls look like chapter production coverage.

## Decision

Compile every one of the 50 CH05 ComicPanelPlans into a repair-evidence readiness row. A gate is present only from an exact panel/revision-bound artifact. Do not infer profiles, policies, masks, authority, budget, visual results, reviews, or outcomes from motion mode, adjacent panels, proxy controls, G07 capacity, or synthetic fixtures.

## Consequences

- The chapter denominator is 50: four explicit repair candidates, two selector profiles/topology controls, and one panel-specific policy.
- Exact approved bases, masks, authorities, reservations, visual-boundary results, seam reviews, v2.1 RenderRecords, candidates, accepted panels, and provider activity are all zero; human minutes remain null.
- P036 is the most advanced local repair row but is not production ready. P044 has topology only and no policy. P019/P026 have neither profile nor policy.
- Thirteen/thirteen denominator, inference, width, input, result, review, and medium-boundary mutations fail.
