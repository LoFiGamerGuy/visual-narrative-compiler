# ADR-0081: closeout achieves engineering scope without closing human gates

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-01

## Context

The requested outcome is an evidence-backed pipeline selection followed by the smallest high-information hardening toward chapter-scale production. It is not authorization to invent human review, approve CH05 inputs, reuse the G07 cap, or execute production.

## Decision

Mark the research/engineering objective achieved when all 12 operating requirements are bound to current evidence and reproducible validation. Keep the selected route explicitly engineering-only, leave the 20-decision review and four CH05 authority roots open, and request no approval in the closeout record.

## Consequences

- Twelve/twelve objective requirements are evidence-bound; 22/22 closeout mutations fail.
- OpenAI remains the measured engineering hardening route; G07 remains 16 candidates/$1.057377/0 of 20 human decisions.
- Local hardening records three topology controls/two profiles, 28 reproducible artifacts, a 50-panel denominator, and fail-closed P036 finalization.
- Integrity binds 74/74 release checks, 11 reproducers, 32 lineage records, safe-source/frozen targets, and 48 zero-cost milestones.
- Human judgment, exact inputs, upload authority, CH05 cap/reservation, provider outcome, and acceptance remain unresolved by design.
