# ADR-0084: owner visual approval advances direction, not invented timing or exact authority

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-01

## Context

The owner reviewed the prepared material, reported that it was all great, approved continued evidence-backed progress, conditionally approved transparent lettering overlap that does not block people or faces, and described the current art as potentially eligible for an exact production base. The owner also requested broader style, density, quality, action, and panel-size exploration.

The G07 protocol still requires a hash-chained timed session with 20 subject-specific decisions. CH05 production still requires an exact base hash, exact mask hash, exact input-package upload authority, and distinct cap/reservation.

## Decision

Record the owner's qualitative approval as real visual-direction authority and issue ComicStyleDirection r2. Do not synthesize G07 event timing, per-subject assertions, minutes, deblinding, exact CH05 input selection, upload authority, or budget. Run new style exploration from text-only ComicPanelPlan constraints so no existing art or likeness is uploaded.

## Consequences

- Lettering may overlap only when transparency preserves faces, people, hands, props, silhouettes, and phone-size readability.
- Chapter design explicitly explores wide action anchors, tall causal-action panels, medium character beats, and small story-object inserts.
- Four new text-only local candidates cover three panel-format roles and four style/prompt conditions.
- The first P036 result remains a documented semantic miss; the corrected prompt is a stronger candidate but not an accepted base.
- Formal G07 remains 0/20 with null minutes; CH05 exact inputs, upload authority, cap, RenderRecords, and accepted panels remain absent.
