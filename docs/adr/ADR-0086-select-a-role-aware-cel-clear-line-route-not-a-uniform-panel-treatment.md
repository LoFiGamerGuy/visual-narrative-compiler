# ADR-0086: select a role-aware cel/clear-line route, not a uniform panel treatment

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-01

## Context

The 20-candidate CH05 overnight batch covers 14 ComicPanelPlans and four styles. Provisional engineering review reports 12 candidates passing all six declared dimensions, three warn-only, and five with failures. The cel-painted arm is 4/4 but covers only medium character and tall practical-action roles. Clear-line watercolor covers the broadest role set with three passes, two warnings, and one failure. Clean graphic and limited ink contain useful successes but also demonstrate direction, safe-zone, and density failures.

Selecting one uniform surface treatment from unequal role coverage would overstate the evidence. Surface polish also cannot override role order, causal action, lettering clearance, or phone readability.

## Decision

Adopt a provisional role-aware route: a premium cel-painted finish with clear-line contour hierarchy is the primary character/causal-action direction, while clear-line watercolor composition remains the measured wide/transition fallback. Small inserts use a genuinely simplified limited-ink treatment. Complete the missing cel-painted P050 wide-action comparison before standardizing the primary finish across the chapter.

Use panel size and detail as narrative cadence. Wide/tall anchors, medium deductions, transitions, and object inserts remain ComicPanelPlan decisions. Reference conditioning uses P050/P040 identity plus explicit hair/wardrobe invariants; text-only controls remain necessary where reference dominance can reverse orientation.

## Consequences

- The next experiment is small and discriminating: one cel-painted P050 wide candidate plus targeted safe-zone/causal corrections for failure classes.
- Causal failures and lettering collisions cannot be promoted through aesthetic preference.
- c001 and c011 remain valuable diagnostics; c003, c007, and c009 remain layout failures.
- No candidate becomes accepted, commercially cleared, reproducible, or an exact production base.
- No new provider, paid API, BFL upload, cloud resource, large model, AnimationShotPlan, or E-Conte record is introduced.
