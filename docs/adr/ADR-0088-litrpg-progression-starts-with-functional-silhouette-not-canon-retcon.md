# ADR-0088: LitRPG progression starts with functional silhouette, not canon retcon

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-01

## Context

The owner requested examples of improved clothing, armor, weapons, monsters, and a more exciting LitRPG direction. CH05 currently requires Soren's oatmeal coat and Sigrid's plaid wrap. Changing those chapter designs or inventing classes/monsters inside CH05 would silently alter canon and ComicPanelPlans.

Three separate concepts test a safer progression language: Soren as a work-derived warden-engineer, Sigrid as a plaid-preserving pathfinder, and a coordinated fight against a peat/root/slate Mireback. The action panel preserves broad identity motifs, but exact kit replication is untested because the new kit images were not authorized as additional uploads.

## Decision

Treat the three images as non-canon visual-development concepts only. Favor functional silhouette, equipment purpose, material-causal monsters, and team tactics before UI text, rarity glow, class naming, or spectacle. Preserve oatmeal and plaid as identity bridges in future exploration.

Any promotion requires a future explicit canon/ComicPanelPlan decision. Do not upload l001/l002 as references without exact new authority.

## Consequences

- CH05 wardrobe and story remain unchanged.
- The concepts can inform owner taste review without becoming production inputs.
- Exact armor continuity across action scenes remains an explicit future authority/evidence gap.
- No new provider, paid API, BFL upload, commercial claim, or production-planning structure is introduced.
