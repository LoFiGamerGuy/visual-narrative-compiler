# ADR-0089: Use variable panel cadence as a storytelling contract

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-01

## Context

Fourteen strongest engineering candidates now cover three connected CH05 sequences. A deterministic vertical-scroll assembly tests eight rendered widths, three alignments, four wide anchors, seven medium/tall beats, three inserts, and larger sequence-break gutters at 1200px and 390px review widths. Hair, wardrobe, role order, and causal flow pass provisional sequence review. Mixed finish density remains visible. Exploratory safe-zone texture statistics flag c005 and c014 for actual lettering rehearsal; those statistics cannot detect faces, hands, reading order, or transparency acceptability.

## Decision

Adopt variable width, alignment, and gutter cadence as the chapter-scale layout contract. Bind panel size to narrative function: wide for orientation/action/reveal, medium for dialogue/deduction/continuity, tall for complete causal chains, and small for object evidence. Preserve the 14-panel assembly as an unaccepted review demonstration.

Do not use texture statistics as an acceptance gate. Rehearse transparent lettering on c005 and c014, with phone-size inspection and explicit face/hand/story-object clearance, before any promotion. Do not force a single finish density until owner review establishes whether the mixed rhythm is desirable.

## Consequences

- ComicPanelPlan remains the only active production-planning structure; no AnimationShotPlan or E-Conte is created.
- The layout builder and validator become the reproducible chapter-cadence mechanism.
- Exact generation reproducibility, commercial clearance, panel acceptance, and production-base status remain absent.
- No provider call, upload, paid API, or budget authority follows from this decision.
