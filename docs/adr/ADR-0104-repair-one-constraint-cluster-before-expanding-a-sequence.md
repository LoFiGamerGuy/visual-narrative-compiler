# ADR-0104: Repair one constraint cluster before expanding a sequence

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-01

## Context

Nine of 26 canonical CH05 candidates have at least one warn/fail. Six exact repair links show different outcomes: literal safe-zone/spatial placement fixes pass, removing a dominating reference fixes target direction but leaves phone identity weak, lever terminology preserves duplicate/incomplete geometry, and one literal continuous-plank instruction resolves P036.

Broad rerolls would obscure which constraint changed. The next chapter expansion should also test continuity across different cast occupancies rather than add another isolated hero panel.

## Decision

Preserve six repair links as exact diagnostic evidence and change only one constraint cluster per targeted repair. Carry literal causal-object nouns and role verbs instead of mechanism shorthand when the plan's geometry is unusual.

After owner review, recommend P010–P013 as the smallest next sequence: Sigrid-only listening, Soren-only twine discovery, no-person taut-twine direction, and dual-adult creek-following re-entry. Bound it to four initial candidates plus at most two targeted repair slots, with zero prompts until review decisions exist.

- The microsequence exercises four adjacent plans, four cast transitions, object causality, continuity re-entry, and cadence.
- Median-only generation envelopes are 204.908s for four candidates and 307.362s with two repair slots; money remains unavailable, not `$0`.
