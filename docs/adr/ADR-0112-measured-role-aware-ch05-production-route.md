# ADR-0112: Measured role-aware CH05 production route

Date: 2026-09-01

Status: Accepted as an engineering recommendation; owner acceptance pending

## Context

CH05 now has 26 instrumented candidates, 14 selected sequence beats, four style arms, targeted repair evidence, measured phone density, conditional scale roles, three lettering studies, and an all-29 RenderRecord index including non-canon concepts. A practical pipeline recommendation is needed without treating visual appeal, style labels, or unbalanced samples as a universal score.

## Decision

Recommend a role-aware pipeline:

- Premium cel-painted treatment for character emotion, deduction, sensory reaction, continuity anchors, and selected wide hero action.
- Mature clear-line/watercolor staging for causal action, trail movement, object interaction, transitions, and geometry repair.
- Clean graphic treatment only for selected wide blocking/reveal or text-only composition control.
- Genuinely simplified limited-ink/flat treatment only for silent inserts after measuring the exact panel's phone density.

Use variable widths by narrative function, protect causal action from lettering, and preserve the current three-reference continuity mechanism plus plan-level hair/wardrobe/cast assertions and full-panel manual atlas review.

## Measured basis

- Cel-painted: 5/6 all-dimension passes.
- Clear-line watercolor: 5/8 passes, two warnings, one failure, and successful targeted safe-zone/single-plank repairs.
- Limited ink/flat: 4/6 passes, but c014 has 0.275889 edge occupancy; the label does not guarantee low density.
- Clean graphic: 3/6 passes; useful blocking evidence but insufficient as a uniform route.
- Targeted repair: six links, five target fixes, four final all-dimension passes.
- Hair/wardrobe: 26/26 manual pass labels; role order 25/26.
- Lettering: tested two-line in-art copy reaches the 13px phone target only at 1,200px; outside-art copy reaches 13.975px with +3.295% scroll height.

## Consequences

- Ten owner decision rows isolate route, density, lettering, next-sequence, non-canon, candidate, commercial, and exact-base questions.
- The P010–P013 microsequence remains the next highest-information experiment after its gates are resolved.
- No prompt, render, upload, ComicPanelPlan revision, acceptance, commercial clearance, or exact production-base selection is created.
- Attempt 1's source-schema field mismatch is preserved; no partial output was written.
