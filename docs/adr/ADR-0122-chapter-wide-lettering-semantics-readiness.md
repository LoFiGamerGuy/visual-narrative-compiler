# ADR-0122: Chapter-wide lettering semantics readiness

Date: 2026-09-01

Status: Accepted as a fail-closed semantics matrix

## Context

Measured lettering studies establish phone-size geometry, but final copy is unbound across CH05. Treating every open field as a balloon location would threaten faces, hands, causal objects, action silhouettes, and quiet insert rhythm. Outside-art bands also represent caption/direct-text semantics, not attributed speech.

## Decision

Classify every plan before copy exists:

- 16 silent-insert defaults.
- 14 silent action/motion panels whose protected geometry outranks lettering.
- 13 caption-or-silence decisions, eligible for the light outside-art band only after plan-level semantic revision.
- Six speech-or-reaction semantic decisions requiring speaker/caption binding first.
- One attributed-speech plan, P016, with Soren known as speaker but exact copy/font/tail/safe zone still unbound.

Retain 13px phone type, 1,200px tested two-line in-art width, 13.975px outside-art type, and 88% backing only as measured constraints—not blanket permissions.

## Evidence

- 50/50 final-copy fields null; zero overlap permissions, lettering acceptance, and plan revisions.
- P016 rejects outside-art caption as attributed speech and requires a 1,200px in-art test after exact copy binding.
- The 1600×1900 map is visually checked.
- 16/16 mutations are rejected.

## Consequences

- Action and inserts default to silence where text would compromise storytelling.
- Transparent backing never authorizes overlap with a face, person, important hand, silhouette, or causal object.
- Copy, font, tails, localization, accessibility, acceptance, and plan revisions remain open.
