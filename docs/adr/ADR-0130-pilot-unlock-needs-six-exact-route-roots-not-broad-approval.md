# ADR-0130: Pilot unlock needs six exact route roots, not broad approval

Date: 2026-09-01

Status: Accepted

## Context

The owner has given strong positive creative direction and broad permission to continue. The P010–P013 pilot nevertheless has six route-dependent choices whose alternatives cause different plan, finish, copy, or preflight work.

## Decision

Treat broad approval as creative direction, not as six fabricated structured values. Require exact decisions for the role-aware route, c005 density policy, c014 action punctuation, lettering semantics, P010–P013 finish rhythm, and P010–P013 copy. Permit only the engineering-default combination to proceed directly; any allowed alternate requires the smallest applicable revision. Defer lettering treatment, existing-candidate shortlist, non-canon direction, and commercial/exact-base authority when they do not gate a silent new pilot.

## Evidence

- Six exact roots are identified and 0/6 are structurally resolved.
- Four decisions are explicitly deferred rather than discarded.
- Fourteen existing-candidate reviews do not gate new pilot generation but remain mandatory before exact-base selection.
- The pilot remains four candidates, two repair slots, and five planned review artifacts.
- Prompt, render, promotion, decision, and human-minute state remains 0/0/0/0/null.
- Seventeen/seventeen mutations are rejected.

## Consequences

Local planning and non-executable prompt-blueprint work may continue. Production prompt compilation or execution cannot claim structured review approval until exact values or their explicit revisions exist.
