# ADR-0145: Engineering defaults reduce owner burden without ingesting approval

Date: 2026-09-01

Status: Accepted

## Context

The pilot unlock contract has six exact roots and four deferred choices. Broad positive direction exists, but converting it into exact structured values would fabricate decisions and blur candidate, rights, and exact-base authority.

## Decision

Publish ten evidence-bound engineering defaults with risks, consequences, artifacts, and an intentionally invalid/unfilled response template. Keep every owner-decision, reviewer, minute, resolution, prompt, render, acceptance, clearance, and executable field null or zero.

## Evidence

- Ten decisions partition exactly into six pilot roots and four deferred choices.
- The six pilot defaults match the prior direct-compile recommendations.
- Deferred LitRPG direction recommends advancing the oatmeal work-derived warden kit, plaid-preserving pathfinder kit, and peat/root/slate Mireback together only into a separate future-canon proposal.
- Candidate shortlist review remains individual; commercial and exact-base status remains open.
- Sixteen/sixteen authority and denominator mutations are rejected.

## Consequences

Tomorrow's review has explicit recommended values without claiming the owner supplied them. Production remains locked until the established decision workflow receives exact reviewed values.
