# ADR-0165: Completion readiness means handoff complete, not art accepted

Date: 2026-09-01

Status: Accepted

## Context

The overnight objective requires a final response with direct visual links, recommendations, measured counts/timing/spend/failures/limitations, changed files, ADRs, Git parity, and remaining review decisions. Those clauses need machine-checked coverage before the autonomous goal can end.

## Decision

Create a completion-readiness audit binding 12 owner deliverables, all 134 exact resources, all 67 priority direct links, closeout metrics, ranked engineering recommendations, limitations, changed files, reproducer, safe source, cost, and owner decision boundaries. Use `PASS_HANDOFF_COMPLETE_OWNER_REVIEW_PENDING`; never equate handoff completeness with art acceptance.

## Evidence

- Twelve/twelve required deliverables exist and match exact hashes.
- Direct groups reconcile to 10 contact sheets, nine sequence packets, 34 lettering resources, and 14 strongest candidates.
- Metrics reconcile to 29 candidates, 50 plans, 12 batches, 12 dimension sets, 1,385.036 observed seconds, 39 reference uses, four recommendations, 15 limitations, and ten remaining decisions.
- Safe source 971 paths, reproducer ten domains, and cost ledger 82 milestones are bound with current remote parity.
- Forty-one/forty-one state, denominator, source, planning, activity, and review mutations fail.

## Consequences

The delivery is structurally ready for owner review. Candidate dispositions, six root decisions, four deferred decisions, acceptance, commercial clearance, exact-base selection, and any ComicPanelPlan revision remain future explicit actions.
