# ADR-0172: P036 causal leverage earns hero-panel scale

- Status: Accepted engineering recommendation; owner visual review pending
- Date: 2026-09-02

## Context

The r3 P036 panel conveyed cooperation but partially hid the continuous relationship among Sigrid's brace, Soren's lift, the single plank, and the high tin. Because P036 is the chapter's strongest physical-cooperation beat, it was the highest-information remaining causal repair.

## Decision

Use the targeted r4 P036 candidate as the provisional chapter-review selection and let it occupy a substantially taller panel footprint than surrounding clue inserts. The scale is earned only because the candidate makes the single-plank force path, distinct adult roles, grounded body weight, and tip-to-tin contact legible at phone width.

This is an engineering and cadence recommendation, not acceptance or an exact production-base decision. The prior P036 crop remains diagnostic evidence.

## Evidence

- Exactly one P036 source changes between r3 and r4; 49/49 non-target panel hashes remain exact.
- Non-gating chapter triage improves from 45 PASS / 5 WARN / 0 FAIL to 46 PASS / 4 WARN / 0 FAIL.
- One continuous plank can be traced from Sigrid's lower brace through Soren's lift to the tin.
- Hair, wardrobe, adult count, set, and lettering clearance pass agent review.
- The 390-pixel phone scroll retains the full force path at a deliberately expanded vertical beat.

## Consequences

The panel adds 1,400 pixels to the 1200-pixel-wide chapter scroll compared with r3, creating a pronounced action pause before the quiet object/map inserts. This supports variable cadence rather than a uniform panel height.

Soren's raised-beam footing is more dramatic than the original plan implied and remains an owner-review point. The built-in product exposes no reproducibility or cost metadata. No rights or commercial conclusion changes.

## Next bounded milestone

Keep r4 as the current complete-chapter review baseline. Improve P039/P043 only if a single contiguous object-continuity repair can clarify the map clue and the deliberately left-behind tin without disturbing the successful P036 anchor.
