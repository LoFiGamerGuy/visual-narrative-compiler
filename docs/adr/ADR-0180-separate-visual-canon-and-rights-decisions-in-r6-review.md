# ADR-0180: Separate visual, canon, and rights decisions in R6 review

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-02

## Context

The current review spans visual quality, a historical/current character-design conflict, future LitRPG progression, commercial clearance, and exact-production-base eligibility. A single broad “approve” control would make it impossible to know whether the owner liked the art, changed canon, or made a rights/production decision.

## Decision

Compile one current start-here pointer with exact links/hashes and three separate decision groups:

1. Visual: overall R6 disposition, P032, lettering, style, and cadence.
2. Canon: current versus historical hair/wardrobe, plus future armor/weapons/monsters/classes/UI.
3. Rights/production: commercial clearance, exact-production-base status, and replacement of historical selected panels.

Navigation and a positive visual recommendation do not fill any of these decisions automatically.

## Consequences

- Ten packet artifacts, 13 strongest panels, and both P032 candidates are directly reviewable.
- The pointer validator rejects 12/12 authority/count/link mutations.
- No publication, acceptance, canon choice, rights determination, provider call, upload, generation, or spend is created.
