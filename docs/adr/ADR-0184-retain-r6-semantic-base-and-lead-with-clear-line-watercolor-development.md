# ADR-0184: Retain R6 semantic base and lead with clear-line watercolor development

## Status

Accepted as an engineering recommendation. Owner visual acceptance, exact-production-base selection, commercial clearance, and rights decisions remain open.

## Context

Three complete 50-ComicPanelPlan chapter routes are now directly comparable. Supplemental review gives R6 47 PASS / 1 WARN / 2 FAIL, the alternate-graphic arm 36 PASS / 7 WARN / 7 FAIL, and the clear-line watercolor arm 45 PASS / 2 WARN / 3 FAIL. The clear-line arm preserves Soren and Sigrid hair/wardrobe continuity across 50/50 panels and improves the alternate arm's independent-entry roles, continuous P036 leverage path, and fully extinguished P041 drum. R6 nevertheless retains the lowest current semantic defect burden and the strongest assembled causal sequence.

The clear-line arm is not lower-density by the recorded equal-panel proxy metrics. Relative to R6, its grayscale entropy is 7.025710 versus 6.889241 bits (+0.136469), FIND_EDGES>=32 density is 0.244026 versus 0.220004 (+0.024022), and native PNG bytes per pixel is 1.698281 versus 1.623124 (+0.075157). The metrics are proxies rather than quality or acceptance measures, but all three reject a lower-density claim.

Its remaining highest-information semantic targets are P001 departure direction, P032 far-bank heel/toe direction, P039 simultaneous three-mark clue, and P043 map possession before P046. P003 remains a non-blocking phone-width subtlety and does not justify displacing those four targets.

## Decision

Retain R6 plus explicit cross-panel gates as the semantic production base. Use clear-line watercolor as the leading style-development arm because it combines 50/50 character continuity with materially stronger semantic performance than the alternate-graphic route, while preserving complete-chapter generation and review evidence.

Do not describe clear-line watercolor as lower-density. Do not promote either route wholesale. Limit the next repair tranche to P001, P032, P039, and P043, and evaluate any replacements by exact ComicPanelPlan semantics, cross-panel state, phone readability, lettering clearance, and non-target hash stability before assembly.

## Consequences

- R6 remains the current semantic base; clear-line watercolor becomes the preferred source of style-development candidates.
- P001 must read as departure downhill with the cold farmhouse behind; P032 must show asymmetric prints on the far dry bank facing back toward Soren; P039 must expose all three distinct marks including the torn-edge upstream mark; P043 must leave the open tin while Sigrid retains the map for P046.
- Clear-line watercolor may be refined for broader quiet shapes, but no density improvement is claimed until measurements separate.
- No candidate, route, lettering treatment, or chapter is accepted, commercially cleared, rights-cleared, or selected as an exact production base.
- ComicPanelPlan remains the sole active production-planning structure; AnimationShotPlan and E-Conte remain null.
