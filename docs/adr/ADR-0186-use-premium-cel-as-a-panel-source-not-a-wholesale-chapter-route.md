# ADR-0186: Use premium cel as a panel source, not a wholesale chapter route

## Status

Accepted as an engineering recommendation. Owner visual disposition, commercial clearance, and exact-production-base selection remain open.

## Context

The premium-cel complete arm adds 11 sequence outputs and 50 deterministic ComicPanelPlan crops. Its non-gating triage is 40 PASS / 5 WARN / 5 FAIL with correct-role hair and wardrobe continuity on 50/50 panels. It strongly improves P041's fully extinguished drum, P043's open-tin/map possession, and the P048–P050 climax. It fails P001 departure, P013 role order, P029 independent exterior watch, P036 visible tin/contact, and P039 simultaneous three-mark composition. P032 remains WARN.

The style is only partly separated: premium-cel equal-panel measurements are edge density 0.227841, entropy 6.934245 bits, and PNG bytes/native pixel 1.656707. It is less dense than the clear-line arm but denser than r6 and alternate graphic, and it still contains high microtexture and one bright P035–P039 lighting discontinuity.

## Decision

Do not promote the premium-cel arm wholesale. Preserve its measured PASS panels as candidates for a review-only hybrid. Retain r6 as the semantic base and clear-line watercolor as the leading style-development route. Run only the three highest-information standalone repairs—P001, P032, and P039—under exact ComicPanelPlan and semantic-gate bindings.

## Consequences

The targeted tranche yields P001 PASS, P039 PASS, and P032 WARN at exact 390px review width. The generated P039 is 1023×1537 rather than the planned 1024×1536; the mismatch is preserved in evidence and does not invalidate its semantic review. No output is accepted, rights-cleared, commercially cleared, or selected as an exact production base.
