# ADR-0194: Close CH05 overnight with a measured three-block review route

## Status

Accepted as the final engineering recommendation for this autonomous goal. Owner visual disposition, art acceptance, rights/commercial clearance, canon promotion, and exact-production-base selection remain open.

## Context

The goal produced a complete 50-ComicPanelPlan CH05 reading draft, six aligned complete route arms, targeted repairs, two no-reference ablations, deterministic review packets, route/cadence analysis, and source/release evidence.

The active-goal output reconciliation binds 15 tracked manifests and reports 76 built-in service raster outputs, 312 panel-level candidates/crops, 132 authorized reference uses, 13 zero-reference outputs, and two unsplit no-reference ablation diagnostics. The six-route aligned comparison is a 300-candidate subset: 50 selected R6 panels plus 250 crops from five additional complete arms. It excludes nine additional R6 candidates and three targeted-repair candidates.

Timing records use incompatible scopes: approximate overlap-adjusted goal walls, overlap-adjusted batch walls, non-overlap arithmetic, individual observations, and unavailable end-to-end stopwatches. They cannot be added into a defensible aggregate elapsed duration.

The final closeout release replays nine domains: the prior integrated release, cost ledger r36, decision matrix, all-41-link handoff integrity, cadence sensitivity, safe-source inventory r2, frozen/baseline integrity, tracked-source scope, and Git parity. All nine passed in 42.965 seconds; seven suites contributed 135/135 upstream mutation rejections and the closeout validator rejected 30/30 record mutations. The first attempt correctly wrote no evidence when its parser did not recognize the inventory validator's alternate human-readable mutation syntax; the narrow parser fix was committed before the successful run.

## Decision

Present the sequence-constrained cadence as the strongest current review route: reduced-palette S01, R6 S02-S08, and premium cel S09-S11. Its selected panels measure 47 semantic PASS / 3 WARN / 0 FAIL with two route transitions. Preserve P003, P032, P045, and P005-to-P006 as explicit review questions.

Retain R6 as the semantic backbone, premium cel as a policy-sensitive late-block specialist, and clear-line watercolor as the leading unselected style-development fallback. Do not select any wholesale route or exact production base from automated scores or visual appeal alone.

Close the local cost ledger at r37 with 124 zero-external-cost milestones. The final revisions add no provider call, upload, generation call, candidate, or paid spend.

## Consequences

The project has a coherent full-chapter review surface and a reproducible chapter-scale pipeline rather than isolated style probes. The next visual work should follow an explicit owner disposition: either retain the three blocks, compare the already available R6 S10-S11 alternative, or revise one whole sequence under one style. Within-sequence cherry-picking remains disallowed.

All generated pixels remain ignored local evidence. Direct paid API/cloud spend is `$0`; built-in monetary cost, model, endpoint, request IDs, usage, and deterministic seed remain unavailable. No art is accepted, rights-cleared, commercially cleared, canon-promoted, or designated an exact production base.
