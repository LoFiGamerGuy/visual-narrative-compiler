# ADR-0196: Prioritize chronological chapter breadth over style duplication

## Status

Accepted as a provisional engineering and story-development direction. It does not accept art, settle commercial rights, or approve an exact production base.

## Context

The current inventory contains one complete 50-panel chapter, CH05, while CH01–CH04 are only 3–4-panel fragments. The completed CH05 study established useful rendering, cadence, continuity, and review evidence, but its six full-route style arms created 300 aligned candidates without moving the story timeline beyond the mill-signal morning.

The owner explicitly requests many chapters with drastic story and character progression rather than further duplication of the same chapter.

## Decision

1. Author and produce CH06–CH13 as an eight-chapter chronological arc, with a stretch outline through CH17.
2. Target 40 panels and eight contiguous sequences per required chapter: 320 new chronological panels before stretch work.
3. Require every chapter to carry forward the preceding closing state and change at least five declared state categories.
4. Use R6's semantic strengths and sequence-level density cadence as the default house route.
5. Produce one default candidate per panel. Do not run alternate whole-chapter style arms before the chronological chapter is complete.
6. After completion, style variants may cover at most 10% of a chapter and must answer a named chapter-level production question.
7. Limit failed panels to two targeted repair attempts; do not reroll passing panels.
8. Introduce the Garden Ledger, armor, weapons, monsters, factions, classes, injuries, and relationship changes only through explicit story-state and ComicPanelPlan bindings that persist across chapters.

## Consequences

The primary progress metric becomes completed chronological chapters and inherited state deltas, not raw candidate count. The CH05 alternatives remain valuable research evidence but are not a template for repeating every future chapter six times.

The first production batch is CH06–CH07: system revelation and a first practical Mireback defense. No render prompt is promoted until both complete panel collections pass the reusable semantic graph and anti-duplication gates.

This decision authorizes no external provider, new upload class, paid API, cloud GPU, large model, child or real-person material, LoRA, dataset, art acceptance, commercial clearance, or exact-production-base selection.
