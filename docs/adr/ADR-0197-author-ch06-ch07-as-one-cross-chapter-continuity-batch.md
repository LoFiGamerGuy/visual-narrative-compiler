# ADR-0197: Author CH06 and CH07 as one cross-chapter continuity batch

## Status

Accepted as provisional canon-development authoring. Render promotion, art acceptance, commercial clearance, and exact-production-base selection remain separate.

## Context

CH05 ends with Soren and Sigrid returning toward an unexpectedly smoking farmhouse. ADR-0196 requires chronological breadth, exact inherited state, and one default candidate per future panel rather than repeated whole-chapter style arms.

Writing CH06 and CH07 independently would risk losing the new adult ally, boundary key, damaged map, garment damage, Garden Ledger evidence, and threshold rule between the farmhouse revelation and the first monster defense.

## Decision

1. Author CH06 and CH07 together as two complete 40-panel ComicPanelPlan collections.
2. Use eight contiguous five-panel sequences per chapter and all six narrative phases.
3. Make CH07's initial continuity state byte-for-byte equal to CH06's final continuity state.
4. Introduce Tamsin Reeve only as a clearly fictional adult in practical, non-sexualized clothing with no real-person reference.
5. Bind the Garden Ledger, Warden's Reach, compact bow, and Mireback through explicit progression declarations under ADR-0196.
6. Protect faces, adult silhouettes, hands, tools, weapons, and story objects in every panel's lettering policy.
7. Keep both chapters authoring-complete but non-executable: no prompt, provider, upload, model, output, cost, seed, or RenderRecord fields.

## Consequences

The project now has 80 new chronological panel plans rather than more CH05 duplicates. CH06 materially reveals the system and the farmhouse boundary node. CH07 converts that state into a practical monster defense, lasting equipment/wardrobe damage, a cracked home ward, and the decision to travel north.

The next bounded milestone is one default-house-route prompt manifest covering CH06–CH07 exactly once, followed by fail-closed preflight. Alternate style arms remain prohibited until each chronological chapter has a complete assembled default route.

This decision grants no new external provider, upload class, direct paid API, cloud GPU, large model, child or real-person material, LoRA, dataset, acceptance, rights, or exact-base authority.
