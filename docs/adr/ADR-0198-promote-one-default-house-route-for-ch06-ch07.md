# ADR-0198: Promote one default house route for CH06–CH07

## Status

Accepted for authorized built-in ImageGen execution. Generated pixels remain unaccepted, commercially uncleared, non-reproducible research evidence.

## Context

ADR-0197 produced 80 validated chronological ComicPanelPlans. The current objective prioritizes complete story breadth over repeated whole-chapter style arms. Execution therefore needs one bounded prompt route that covers every plan exactly once and fails closed on reference, data, and provenance boundaries.

## Decision

1. Promote 16 chronological five-panel sequence requests covering all 80 CH06–CH07 plans exactly once.
2. Use the clean graphic/painterly house direction with selective cel finish; do not create a competing full-chapter style arm.
3. Use only the two authorized identity/wardrobe references by default. Add the third authorized composition reference only to sequences whose authored beats explicitly require leverage, weight, gatepost, polehook, or counterweight mechanics.
4. Repeat adult-only, fixed-hair, signature-garment, physical-Ledger, literal-motion, and lettering-clearance constraints in every request.
5. Preserve one candidate per plan by default. A failed panel may receive no more than two narrowly classified repairs, and passing panels may not be rerolled.
6. Keep built-in model, endpoint, provider request ID, usage, monetary cost, and deterministic seed null unless the product exposes them.
7. Do not re-upload any newly generated output as a reference.

## Evidence

The manifest contains 16 requests, 80 unique panel IDs, and 38 authorized reference uses. Its validator passes all checks, and 24/24 adversarial fixtures fail closed. Provider calls, outputs, and paid API/cloud spend remain zero at preflight.

## Consequences

The route is ready for already authorized built-in execution without introducing another provider, upload class, paid API, cloud GPU, model download, private reference, real-person material, child-related material, LoRA, or dataset. Visual quality, story readability, acceptance, rights, commercial clearance, and exact-production-base status remain review questions.
