# ADR-0201: Assemble CH07 and preserve only exact repair targets

## Status

Accepted as the CH07 production/review baseline. Owner review, acceptance, rights, commercial clearance, and exact-production-base selection remain pending.

## Context

CH07 is the first sustained monster-action chapter on the new one-route pipeline. It needs to establish practical weapons, causal terrain tactics, visible cost, and forward story momentum without returning to whole-chapter style duplication.

## Decision

1. Preserve all eight CH07 built-in sequence outputs and crop their horizontal or generated comic-grid layouts through hash-bound deterministic geometry.
2. Exclude the generator's S07/S08 numeric panel labels with recorded crop bounds while preserving the untouched sources as diagnostic evidence.
3. Keep P009 as a warning because Tamsin's hair lightens substantially from CH06; her clothing and role remain distinguishable.
4. Mark P030 as a failure for forbidden status prose on the gate.
5. Mark P040 as a failure because Warden's Reach drifts into an unexplained mechanical gun-like form during the departure beat.
6. Permit only panel-local no-text and weapon-form repairs for P030 and P040. Do not reroll passing panels or create a wholesale style arm.
7. Continue CH08–CH09 promotion and CH10–CH11 authoring while these repairs remain queued.

## Evidence

Eight sequence outputs produce 40 unique panel candidates and five review artifacts. Agent triage is 37 PASS / 1 WARN / 2 FAIL. The validator passes and rejects 17/17 adversarial mutations.

The eight exact client-observed ImageGen wall intervals sum to 2,370.565 seconds, but requests ran in two parallel groups whose wall intervals were 497.196 and 460.672 seconds. The sum must not be interpreted as total elapsed wall time. Built-in model, endpoint, provider request ID, usage, monetary cost, and seed remain unavailable. Paid API/cloud spend is $0.

## Consequences

CH07 supplies the requested action, monster, weapon, clothing-damage, and progression evidence in a complete chapter. Two exact repair targets remain, while 38 non-target panels are frozen against stochastic reroll. Generated pixels remain ignored, unaccepted, commercially uncleared, non-reproducible research evidence.
