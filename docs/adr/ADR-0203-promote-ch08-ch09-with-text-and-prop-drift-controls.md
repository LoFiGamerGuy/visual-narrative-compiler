# ADR-0203: Promote CH08–CH09 with text and prop-drift controls

## Status

Accepted for authorized built-in ImageGen execution. Art review, acceptance, rights, commercial clearance, and exact-production-base selection remain separate.

## Context

CH06–CH07 measured two recurrent failures: unrequested status prose and named-prop form drift. Tamsin's hair also lightened substantially in CH07. CH08–CH09 add evolved armor, the Hollow Stag, underwater action, injury, and Wayfinder progression without any newly authorized visual reference class.

## Decision

1. Promote one sequence request for each of 16 chronological sequences, covering 80 plans once with zero whole-chapter alternate arms.
2. Require one left-to-right row, no panel numbers, no grid labels, and no readable generated prose.
3. Replace authored Ledger wording in render prompts with nonverbal frost-green physical emblems; exact planned copy remains a local lettering operation.
4. Keep Warden's Reach a simple hay-fork-shaft/pruning-hook polearm and explicitly prohibit gun, firearm, crossbow, or complex-machine forms.
5. Use a provisional medium-chestnut single-braid text anchor for Tamsin to prevent the measured CH07 blond drift and distinguish her from Sigrid. This is a prompt continuity choice, not art/canon acceptance.
6. Use only the existing three authorized references: two identity/wardrobe images by default and the composition image only for explicit mechanics beats. Newly generated outputs remain ineligible for re-upload.

## Evidence

The preflight contains 16 requests, 80 unique plans, and 40 authorized reference uses. Validation passes and rejects 27/27 adversarial mutations. Provider calls, outputs, and paid API/cloud spend remain zero at preflight.

## Consequences

CH08 can proceed as the next complete production chapter with controls directly informed by measured CH07 failures. The evolved gear, Hollow Stag, brace, and Wayfinder visuals remain text-defined and may require bounded local repair. No provider, upload class, direct API, cloud GPU, model download, private reference, real-person material, child-related material, LoRA, dataset, license conclusion, or commercial-use conclusion is added.
