# ADR-0205: Retain the CH08 route after measured control improvement

## Status

Accepted as the CH08 production/review baseline. Human acceptance, rights, commercial clearance, and exact-production-base selection remain pending.

## Context

ADR-0203 added direct controls for CH07's generated text, grid labels, Tamsin hair drift, and Warden's Reach form drift. CH08 is the first full chapter using those controls and the first to carry evolved road gear and a non-hostile monster encounter.

## Decision

1. Preserve all eight CH08 sequence outputs and their 40 deterministic panel crops.
2. Retain the one-row/no-number and nonverbal physical-Ledger prompt controls: no CH07-class rendered prose or panel-number failure recurred.
3. Retain the explicit simple-polehook constraint: Warden's Reach does not become gun-like in CH08.
4. Preserve P028 as one non-gating warning for an unrequested white instructional arrow beside the polehook.
5. Execute no repair or alternate route before owner review; the arrow does not block story comprehension.
6. Continue directly into CH09 production and later-chapter authoring.

## Evidence

Eight outputs produce 40 unique candidates and five review artifacts. Agent triage is 39 PASS / 1 WARN / 0 FAIL. The validator passes and rejects 16/16 adversarial mutations. Exact client-observed request intervals sum to 2,158.140 seconds, with parallel-group wall intervals of 430.624 and 437.107 seconds. Paid API/cloud spend is $0; built-in service/cost fields remain unavailable.

## Consequences

The strongest current route supports chapter-scale continuity, environmental action, evolved wardrobe, physical system clues, and a creature encounter without a blocking failure. The Hollow Stag and evolved gear remain text-defined and stochastic because new outputs cannot be re-uploaded. Generated pixels remain ignored, unaccepted, commercially uncleared, and non-reproducible evidence.
