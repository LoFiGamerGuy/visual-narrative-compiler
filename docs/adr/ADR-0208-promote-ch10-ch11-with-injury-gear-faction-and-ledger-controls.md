# ADR-0208: Promote CH10–CH11 with injury, gear, faction, and Ledger controls

## Status

Accepted for exact built-in ImageGen prompt preflight and chronological execution only.

## Context

CH10–CH11 introduce a much larger continuity load than the earlier chapters: Soren's persistent rigid brace, forged Warden's Reach, Sigrid's owned bow/seax, removable quarry armor, adult Brackenwake factions, multiple Mirebacks, a public siege, earned classes, and a shared bond. Newly generated outputs cannot be re-uploaded as continuity references, so these states must be explicit and fail closed in text.

## Decision

1. Compile one default route of sixteen five-panel requests covering all 80 CH10–CH11 ComicPanelPlans exactly once.
2. Use only the OpenAI built-in ImageGen product and the same three owner-authorized fictional-adult reference hashes.
3. Lock Soren's left rigid brace/no spontaneous healing, the forged simple polehook's anti-firearm geometry, and Sigrid's owned simple bow/short seax.
4. Keep oatmeal/plaid garment ancestry visible beneath removable quarry guards and lock Tamsin to a chestnut braid.
5. Define Halvor, all Compact workers, and every visible ally as fictional adults; define Mirebacks as grounded quadrupedal peat/root/slate creatures.
6. Withhold seven class/status/bond phrases and one prop label from generated pixels, replacing them with nonverbal physical-surface emblems or cord identity for local lettering.
7. Preserve one candidate per panel, zero whole-chapter alternate arms, and at most two narrow attempts per exact failed panel.

## Evidence

Sixteen requests cover 80 unique plans with 44 uses of only the three authorized hashes. Prompt, plan, reference, safety, anti-duplication, execution-null, and decision bindings pass; 27/27 adversarial mutations are rejected. Provider calls, outputs, uploads, and paid API/cloud spend are zero at preflight.

## Consequences

CH10 is ready for chronological built-in execution. Model, endpoint, request ID, usage, cost, seed, reproducibility, acceptance, rights, commercial clearance, and exact-base status remain unavailable, false, or pending. New outputs remain ineligible for re-upload.
