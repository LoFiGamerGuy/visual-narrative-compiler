# ADR-0209: Retain the CH10 diagnostic baseline and continue to CH11

## Status

Accepted as the CH10 review baseline with three explicit repair targets. Human acceptance, rights, commercial clearance, and exact-production-base selection remain pending.

## Context

CH10 is the first faction/forge/equipment chapter. It must add a new adult rival, persistent injury mechanics, earned armor and weapons, and a political bargain without reverting to repeated style probing.

## Decision

1. Preserve all eight CH10 sequence outputs and 40 deterministic crops as the complete diagnostic baseline.
2. Record P007 and P009 as exact Halvor identity-substitution failures; do not treat similar clothing as sufficient role continuity.
3. Record P023 as a premature-unrequested-Mireback failure because it advances the siege trigger seventeen panels early.
4. Keep the other 37 selected panel hashes fixed pending owner review.
5. Continue chronologically into CH11 before repair, then permit only a paired P007/P009 repair unit and one P023 repair attempt.
6. Preserve one default route, zero whole-chapter alternate arms, and all existing safety/provenance limits.

## Evidence

Eight outputs create 40 candidates and five artifacts. Agent triage is 37 PASS / 0 WARN / 3 FAIL. The validator passes and rejects 17/17 mutations. Exact overlapping request intervals sum to 2,045.824 seconds; parallel group walls are 403.285 and 414.710 seconds. Paid API/cloud spend is $0; built-in service/cost fields remain unavailable.

## Consequences

CH10 materially changes setting, adult social structure, injury equipment, weapons, armor, mission ethics, and stakes despite three localized failures. The failures are small enough to repair without rerendering the chapter and do not justify delaying CH11 breadth. Pixels remain ignored, unaccepted, commercially uncleared, and non-reproducible evidence.
