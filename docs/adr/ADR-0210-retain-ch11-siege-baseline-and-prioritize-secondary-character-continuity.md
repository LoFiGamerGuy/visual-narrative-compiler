# ADR-0210: Retain the CH11 siege baseline and prioritize secondary-character continuity

## Status

Accepted as the CH11 review baseline with four linked Halvor identity failures and one Tamsin warning. Human acceptance, rights, commercial clearance, and exact-production-base selection remain pending.

## Context

CH11 must deliver a complete collective siege, persistent consequences, earned class/bond progression, and a route into North Garden. It also tests whether text-only descriptions can keep a recurring secondary adult distinct when only Soren and Sigrid have authorized visual references.

## Decision

1. Preserve all eight sequence outputs, 40 deterministic crops, and five review artifacts as the complete diagnostic baseline.
2. Preserve P003, P008, P013, and P025 as `HALVOR_ROLE_IDENTITY_SUBSTITUTION` failures; do not reinterpret a pale blond Soren-like actor as Halvor.
3. Preserve P040 as `TAMSIN_BRAID_NOT_LEGIBLE`, a non-blocking identity warning.
4. Keep the other 35 candidate hashes fixed pending owner review.
5. Treat the four Halvor failures as one linked secondary-character continuity problem. Any later repair must be the smallest experiment that can distinguish role anchoring from stochastic rerendering.
6. Continue to a six-chapter progression hub and CH12 chronological promotion before cosmetic repair. Keep one default route and zero wholesale alternate arms.

## Evidence

Eight outputs create 40 candidates and five artifacts from 21 authorized reference uses. Agent triage is 35 PASS / 1 WARN / 4 FAIL. Exact overlapping request intervals sum to 2,214.860 seconds; parallel group walls are 442.406 and 429.358 seconds. The validator passes and rejects 19/19 mutations. Paid API/cloud spend is $0; built-in service/cost fields remain unavailable.

## Consequences

The baseline demonstrates chapter-scale siege geography, causal mechanics, adult collective action, persistent injury, visible loss, earned class/bond progression, and a strong map reveal. It also shows that recurring unreferenced secondary adults are the leading continuity limitation. Pixels remain ignored, unaccepted, commercially uncleared, and non-reproducible evidence.
