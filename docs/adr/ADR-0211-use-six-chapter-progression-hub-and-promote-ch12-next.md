# ADR-0211: Use the six-chapter progression hub and promote CH12 next

## Status

Accepted for review navigation and production sequencing.

## Context

Six complete visual chapters now exist, but per-chapter folders obscure the requested drastic progression across the timeline. CH12 and CH13 are fully authored but still behind a separate prompt-promotion gate.

## Decision

1. Bind CH06–CH11 into one deterministic progression manifest and three cross-chapter review images.
2. Show the same nine fixed panel ordinals per chapter as navigation, never as an automated quality ranking.
3. Preserve all chapter-specific warnings and failures in the aggregate instead of averaging them away.
4. Continue chronological breadth by promoting CH12–CH13 next; do not add another complete alternate style arm.
5. Keep generated pixels ignored and keep acceptance, rights, commercial clearance, exact-base selection, and reproducibility false or pending.

## Evidence

Six source packet hashes bind 240 candidates and 48 sequence sources. Combined non-gating triage is 225 PASS / 5 WARN / 10 FAIL. The deterministic builder emits three review artifacts; the validator passes and rejects 10/10 adversarial mutations.

## Consequences

The owner can inspect six complete chapters and progression from investigation through ecology, injury, faction bargaining, equipment growth, siege, class/bond rewards, and the North Garden reveal in one surface. The hub changes no candidate disposition. The next gate is one stage-aware CH12–CH13 prompt manifest preserving the authored rupture, garment damage, key fusion, and Crownroot resolution.
