## Status

Accepted as the CH13 review baseline with four explicit localized failures. Human acceptance, rights, commercial clearance, and exact-production-base selection remain pending.

## Context

CH13 must turn North Garden into a chapter-scale moving environment, resolve Crownroot through measurable pipe/water/structure work and mutual consent, preserve the guardian, grant earned adult roles, establish an operational base, and expose a wider network. It must also carry CH12's irreversible garment and equipment state.

## Decision

1. Preserve all eight sequence outputs, 40 crops, and five review artifacts as the complete diagnostic baseline.
2. Preserve P020, P031, P036, and P039 as exact Soren shoulder-state failures rather than silently normalizing the reset.
3. Also bind P036's human-face Crownroot relief as an ontology failure; any repair should address both defects in one narrow unit.
4. Preserve the other 36 candidate hashes pending owner review.
5. Complete the CH06–CH13 progression hub before targeted repair or alternate style work.

## Evidence

Exact overlapping request intervals sum to 1,979.498 seconds and group walls are 421.512/399.711 seconds. Paid API/cloud spend is $0; built-in service/cost fields remain unavailable.

The baseline is strong enough for aggregate progression review while showing that irreversible cross-sequence garment state remains a leading limitation. Pixels remain ignored, unaccepted, commercially uncleared, and non-reproducible evidence.
