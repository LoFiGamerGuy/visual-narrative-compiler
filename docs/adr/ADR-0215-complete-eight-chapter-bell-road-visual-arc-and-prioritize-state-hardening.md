# ADR-0215: Complete the eight-chapter Bell Road visual arc and prioritize state hardening

## Status

Accepted as an engineering recommendation and owner-review baseline. It is not art acceptance, a rights decision, commercial clearance, or an exact-production-base selection.

## Context

The owner requested many chronological chapters with drastic story and character progression instead of repeated variants of the same early material. CH06–CH13 now provide an aligned eight-chapter evidence set. A rendering route must be selected using story, identity, causal action, continuity, lettering, cost, latency, and reproducibility—not visual appeal alone.

## Decision

1. Treat the mature clean-graphic/painterly house route as the provisional chapter-scale semantic backbone.
2. Bind all eight chapters, 320 candidates, and 64 sequences in one deterministic progression review.
3. Preserve the exact 296 PASS / 5 WARN / 19 FAIL triage; do not conceal localized failures behind a chapter average.
4. Prioritize one irreversible shoulder-state hardening unit spanning CH12–CH13 before another whole-chapter style arm.
5. Keep Halvor identity as a separate secondary-character mechanism experiment and keep lettering local.
6. Preserve variable panel cadence rather than selecting one aspect ratio or density.

## Evidence

Eight chapter packets contribute 320 candidates and 40 chapter artifacts; the hub adds three aggregate artifacts. Aggregate triage is 296/5/19. The route uses 166 authorized reference instances, no output re-upload, no alternate provider, and $0 direct paid API/cloud spend. CH07–CH13 expose 15,060.448 seconds of overlapping request intervals; CH06 timing is unavailable. The hub validator rejects 10/10 mutations and preserves ComicPanelPlan-only planning.

## Consequences

The project now has a reviewable visual timeline from investigation to co-kept operational base. The next hardening work is small and falsifiable: determine whether irreversible garment state can persist across independent sequence calls without weakening identity and action. The recommendation does not claim deterministic reproduction, provider metadata, licensing, commercial clearance, or final visual acceptance.
