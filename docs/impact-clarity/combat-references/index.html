What I found, and what to test next

Spend the peak on the event. The clearest examples let the background disappear or turn into directional fields. Our new artwork already improves body displacement, but still often preserves every wall, armor and ice detail at once.

Let force change the body. Compression, a turned head, loose trailing limbs and a planted pivot often convey more than extra sparks. Alternate close contact with a wider consequence.

Separate exciting from comfortable. Several references are deliberately dense boundary tests. Borrow their large directional organization while judging their texture independently. A famous series is not automatic approval of every component.
