Edition notes

Five complete sixteen-panel pilots, with preserved native artwork, editable lettering and three explicit copy revisions. This is a provisional editorial edition; owner approval is unrecorded.

Visible speakers receive directional balloon tails. Distant voices keep a small name cue and a tail pointing out of the composition. Black Petal’s borrowed voice has a consistent italic, double-border treatment; it is never named as the creature in the comic. Added lettering space protects faces, hands, clues and contacts where the original raster has no room.

Known artwork limits

  • Nightglass: the cyan tether in NG09/10 intersects the blade/beak contact; it does not read as a clean pommel-to-anchor line.
  • Soft Thunder: ST11 charges the circular glove, but ST12 releases from the crescent glove.
  • Red Current: the charged gauntlet and key construction drift. RC15 retains the original dim reveal, not the brighter finishing attempt.
  • Floodline: FL05 shows ripples while the memory remains visible. The pole’s two jaw contacts are not clearly demonstrated.
