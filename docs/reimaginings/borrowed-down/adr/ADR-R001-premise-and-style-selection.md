# ADR-R001: Select *Borrowed Down* and the pressure-print visual system

Status: accepted

## Candidates

| Premise | Dynamic | Progression | Visual opportunity | Ten-chapter close | Weighted score / 100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|
| *Borrowed Down* — workers traverse a city beside a vertical ocean as civic gravity fails | municipal ropewright vs. blacklisted forecast smuggler | force-memory knots earned by carrying real loads and consequences | inverted streets, wall-sea, rigging, graphic force vectors | cut a corrupt central anchor and replace it with a citizen load-web | 93 |
| *Mothlight Debt* — lamplighter and tax auditor beneath a sleeping titan | duty vs. mercy | promises woven into luminous cloth | nocturnal moth ecology, textile magic | wake or free the titan | 83 |
| *Ashfall Relay* — courier and retired siege cook cross migrating volcano towns | speed vs. stewardship | route scars and craft mastery | ash, modular towns, kinetic vehicles | deliver a treaty through an eruption | 80 |
| *Red Orchard Station* — botanist and salvage captain protect an orbital fruit biome | evidence vs. instinct | grafted ecological permissions | luminous zero-g plants and hull ruins | choose ecosystem autonomy over ownership | 86 |

Criteria weights: dramatic momentum 15, protagonist chemistry 15, originality 10, earned progression 10, environment/monster variety 10, causal action 10, sustainable continuity 10, phone readability 8, lettering clearance 5, Chapter 10 culmination 7.

## Decision

Select *Borrowed Down*. Its central image is instantly legible, its rope-and-load action naturally exposes physical causality, and its progression marks live on tools and bodies rather than floating interfaces. The two protagonists need one another while disagreeing about institutions, which supplies durable chemistry.

Select **pressure-print**: chunky black carved edges, flat off-register teal/coral/acid-yellow ink, paper grain, adult figures with long angular torsos and large practical hands, simplified backgrounds except for mechanical anchors, and white negative-space force paths. It is unmistakably distinct from North Garden and can sustain sequence sheets without requiring high rendering density everywhere.

Rejected style probes:

1. Luminous soft-painted science fantasy: attractive, but too atmosphere-dependent and too close to generic concept art.
2. Monochrome brush manga with one cyan spot color: superb motion but weaker location and wardrobe separation at phone size.
3. Cut-paper storybook collage: strong silhouettes and humor, but insufficient facial nuance for the adult relationship arc.
4. Pressure-print woodcut/risograph hybrid: strongest combined identity, causality, legibility, and sustainable density.

At least the luminous painted and cut-paper candidates belong to visibly different productions. Text-only probes precede any reference reuse. Final generated candidates remain owner-review-pending, unaccepted, commercially uncleared, and non-reproducible.
