# ADR-R003: Preserve the torn coral collar after the cape-tail loss

Status: accepted continuity clarification

CH05's mechanical consequence is narrowed from losing every piece of coral cloth to losing the **long load-bearing cape tail** in the bridge gears. The torn collar/shoulder wrap remains.

This is not a reversal: the cape can no longer catch wind, brake a fall, cover the torso, or serve as a large cloth tool. The missing tail remains irreversible. The retained short collar matches the generated evidence consistently and preserves Dax's phone-scale color anchor.

The original r1 generation prompts contain a contradiction between a static initial-costume character descriptor and later irreversible-state text. Their RenderRecords remain unchanged as historical evidence. The final continuity contract and story source supersede that wording; the contradiction is disclosed as a production limitation rather than hidden through broad rerendering.
