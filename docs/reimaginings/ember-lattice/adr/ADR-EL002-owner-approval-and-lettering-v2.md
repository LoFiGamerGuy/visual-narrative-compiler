# ADR-EL002 — Owner approval, Candidate B lock, and lettering v2

Status: accepted and locked for Phase B on 2026-09-04.

The owner explicitly approved the pilot and praised Candidate B. Phase B is authorized. Candidate B's clean modern action-anime/manhwa line, adult facial construction, cel-value grouping, and restrained ember accent are now the immutable visual base unless a hard failure requires a localized repair.

The owner also required deeper wording, smaller and less obstructive dialogue treatment, experimentation with transparency or contrasting text overlays, and more visible system menus, leveling, XP, and skills. Lettering v2 therefore uses compact 84%-opaque soft balloons, outlined open dialogue on validated quiet backgrounds, border-butted or linked balloons for longer speech, and narrower high-information Ledger menus. Research and exact acceptance thresholds are recorded in `research/lettering-and-dialogue-research.md`.

Phase B chapter contract: exactly 24 panels per chapter, exactly CH01–CH10, four meaningful system moments per chapter, 300–520 dialogue words per chapter, 14 px phone-equivalent dialogue/caption type and 13 px wrapped system type, 159/60/21 low/moderate/high density across the volume, and 35–45% action overall. Every chapter must still work if its lettering is hidden; the lettering then supplies voice, causality, and exact system state rather than rescuing unclear art.
