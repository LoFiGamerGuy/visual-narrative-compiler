## Rejected assumptions

- Structural PASS does not prove artistic success: *The City Keeps Oaths* logged 186/240 metric WARN panels and the owner's binding diagnosis rejects its visual result.
- Five-panel strips are not a neutral convenience: *Borrowed Down* retained baked blank regions and dense later sheets.
- Abstract visible progression is not LitRPG: the prior Covenant system deliberately had no numerical interface and therefore cannot satisfy this genre brief.
- A clean cinematic palette alone does not distinguish a new work from generic painterly fantasy.

## New hypotheses and measurable decisions

- Individual critical panels plus a small hash-pinned character anchor will reduce crowded compositions and identity drift.
- Fracture-based combat can integrate action causality with readable system state.
- Pilot density is precommitted at 11 low / 3 moderate / 2 high and manually checked at 390px.
- Organic SVG dialogue and brass cut-corner system UI will remain semantically distinct.
