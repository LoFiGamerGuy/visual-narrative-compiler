# Bounded inspiration research and derivation matrix

Research date: 2026-09-04. Observations are high-level and non-extractive. No published panel, screenshot, character, costume, creature, UI, logo, composition, or third-party artwork was downloaded, traced, uploaded, or registered.

## Sources

1. [The Beginning After the End — official Tapas comic page](https://tapas.io/series/tbate-comic/info), accessed 2026-09-04. The official episode list makes the genre promise concrete early through mana, sparring, ambush, and teamwork beats; the page identifies separate script, art, color, QC, and lettering roles.
2. [Solo Leveling — official Yen Press comic page](https://yenpress.com/series/solo-leveling-comic), accessed 2026-09-04. Official classification and volume presentation confirm action/adventure, fantasy, and game-related genre legibility plus dedicated lettering.
3. [Tower of God — official WEBTOON page](https://www.webtoons.com/en/fantasy/tower-of-god/list?title_no=95), accessed 2026-09-04. The official hook foregrounds desire and upward progression; the long-running work is natively presented in vertical-scroll format.
4. [Interview with Tower of God creator SIU](https://animecorner.me/interview-tower-of-god-creator-siu/), 2025-09-04, accessed 2026-09-04. SIU connects battle-story roles to readable team positions and describes the tower as a multicultural setting.
5. The report emphasizes storyboard/drawing/color/QC division, action atmosphere, facial expression, and natural pose research.

## Originality boundary

The visual target is defined only through clean tapered contours, simplified adult anime facial geometry, controlled two-step cel shading, ember-orange accents against charcoal/ivory/teal, low-detail ordinary panels, strong vertical depth, and causally linked motion. Named works are never used in prompts and no living artist is imitated.
