# ADR-001 — premise, schema, and provisional route

Status: accepted.

The preregistered premise rubric selects **The City Keeps Oaths** at 97/100. No further premise churn is authorized. `ComicPanelPlan` is the sole planning structure; the baseline 18-field procedural contract is retained and extended with explicit irreversible state, `[left, top, right, bottom]` safe zones, local lettering, and generation-group crop metadata. `AnimationShotPlan` and E-Conte are null.

Exactly ten chapters contain four sequences and 24 panels each: 40 sequences and 240 panels. This is deliberately calmer than the prior 300-panel grid volume. The provisional route is two three-frame vertical strips per sequence (80 requests), contingent on the controlled topology pilot. This is the smallest request count within the allowed one-to-three-moment canvases while avoiding six equally dense moments.

Images remain ignored under `experiments/reimaginings/the-city-keeps-oaths/`; only source, hashes, prompts, crop records, review statuses, and reconciliation evidence are tracked.
