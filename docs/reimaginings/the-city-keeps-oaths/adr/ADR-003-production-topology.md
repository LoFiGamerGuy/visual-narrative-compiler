# ADR-003 — lock three-panel vertical strips

Status: accepted.

The controlled topology pilot scores individual, two-panel, and three-panel routes at 87, 91, and 94. The selected three-panel vertical strip is the smallest request grouping that provides reliable continuity without forcing six dense beats onto one canvas. Production is fixed at two strips per six-panel sequence. No fallback probe was required; all initial routes passed hard requirements.
