# Inspiration research and derivation boundary

Research used official or licensed sources first: [The Beginning After the End on Tapas](https://tapas.io/series/tbate-comic/info), [TurtleMe creator Q&A](https://tapas.io/newsfeed/97), [Solo Leveling on Tappytoon](https://www.tappytoon.com/en/book/187), [Solo Leveling at Yen Press](https://yenpress.com/series/solo-leveling-comic), [Solo Leveling producer interview](https://cocotame.jp/en/series/110047/), [Tower of God on WEBTOON](https://m.webtoons.com/en/fantasy/tower-of-god/list?title_no=95), [Tower of God director interview](https://www.crunchyroll.com/news/interviews/2024/11/21/tower-of-god-director-akira-suzuki-interview), [Forbes analysis](https://www.forbes.com/sites/laurenorsini/2020/04/01/tower-of-god-puts-battle-anime-tropes-to-the-test/), and [GameSpot analysis](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/tower-of-god-review-shonen-comfort-food/1900-6417472/).

| Source observation | Generalized principle | Original implementation | Prohibited similarity |
|---|---|---|---|
| TBATE pairs capability with regret and loneliness | Progress matters when it repairs an internal deficit | Every Sola chord forces a relationship or responsibility choice | No reincarnated king, child prodigy, academy, mana core, dragon, or copied costume |
| TurtleMe discusses accessible serial adaptation | Each chapter needs one clear satisfaction and one clean question | Calm setup, legible complication, visual payoff, quiet hook | No copied episode structures, jokes, dialogue, or cliffhangers |
| Solo Leveling begins from a clear baseline and makes growth observable | Show increments, application, escalation, and cost | Six original covenant milestones shown through tools, posture, clothing, and environment | No hunters, gates, dungeon ranks, stats, quests, daggers, shadow army, or blue UI |
| Producers emphasize compact reading and cathartic advancement | Constraint → decision → technique → consequence | One dominant action per panel, pause before impact, aftermath proof | No copied fights, transformations, poses, monsters, or shot sequence |
| Solo Leveling adaptation expands supporting psychology | Advancement should alter a social graph | Tarin, Kesin, residents, Varo, and Serac interpret every gain differently | No character analogues, guild structure, or relationship replicas |
| Tower of God organizes pressure around conflicting desires | Shared objectives deepen when private needs collide | Each adult has a stated want, concealed need, and non-negotiable | No tower climb, floors, Regulars, Rankers, Shinsu, royal families, or wish premise |
| Analyses emphasize varied tests and complementary teamwork | Rotate the faculty being tested | Perception, restraint, negotiation, memory, navigation, sacrifice | No copied tests, positions, teams, or betrayal configurations |
| The director prioritizes atmosphere, dynamics, and physically coherent rooms over literal angles | Compose from intent and geography, not homage shots | Establish entrances, depth, light, and screen direction once, then simplify | No recreation, tracing, signature angle, setting geometry, or named-style imitation |
| Strong webtoon spectacle depends on vertical scale and clear power | Stage silhouette and values before effects | Midnight/pearl staging with one cyan-gold covenant accent | No proprietary palettes, auras, weapons, interfaces, or title iconography |

The synthesis is a new house style expressed through contour, value, palette, density, camera distance, motion grammar, and emotional tone. No published panels or third-party artwork are uploaded or used as references.
