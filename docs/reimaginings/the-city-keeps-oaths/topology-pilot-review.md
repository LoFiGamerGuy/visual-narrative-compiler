# Compact topology and readability pilot

The controlled pilot renders the same three beats—expressive adult close-up with progression effect, quiet two-person interaction, and physical action in a vertical-scale environment—through individual panels, a two-panel strip plus individual action panel, and one three-panel vertical strip. All use the registered Candidate A anchor.

The individual route gives maximum per-frame room but visibly varies the action geometry and requires three requests. The two-panel route preserves close-up/interaction identity but the separately generated action changes spear and gap geometry. The three-panel strip preserves Sola, Tarin, wardrobe, tool, effect, screen direction, and causal escalation in one request. Each third remains independently readable at 390 px. Its action frame is measurably denser (edge 0.150868; high-frequency occupancy 0.185657) but well separated from two quieter frames and below the calibrated spectacle ceiling.

The three-panel strip is therefore locked for production: two strips per six-panel sequence, 80 production requests. This does not revive the rejected 3×2/six-moment sheet. Each canvas contains only three chronological moments with generous vertical area and one dominant action per frame.
