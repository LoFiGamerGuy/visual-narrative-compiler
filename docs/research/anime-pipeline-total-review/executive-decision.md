4. **Ember premise is worth keeping as payload.** Irreversible spend, failed quests, adult partnership are specific. The 24-panel / four-Ledger template and “next volume” endings kill propulsion.
5. **Engineering outran the page.** 91 tracked HTML readers, 1368 SVGs, 603 `north_garden` modules, unused future-cast. Keep lettering/records; stop evidence-about-evidence.
