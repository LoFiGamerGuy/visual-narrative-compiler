Where the page fails

Narrative intent → storyboard → source request → selection → repair → lettering → acceptance → phone reader. Fix the earliest demonstrated error; later polish cannot change the event depicted.

C1 · critical

Story events change or happen before their cause

Stage: selection and benchmark-to-chapter mapping

24 existing benchmark sources were assigned to revised chapter beats; some exact original requests contradict cast, body state or timing.

Change: Storyboard-led source binding, independent semantic rejection and replacement of incompatible plates.

C2 · critical

Excellent PASS scores coexist with visibly broken sequences

Stage: validation and acceptance authority

Workflow score presets, round-robin scenario tags and protected rectangles chosen around lettering replace independent observations.

Change: Remove computed quality presets from decisions; independently freeze annotations and review actual phone sequence.

C3 · high

Large speech is readable in isolation but spills out of balloons or hides meaning

Stage: lettering layout and source composition

Type grew while curved container geometry and real-image protected regions were not jointly reviewed; authored boxes are not actual quiet space.

Change: Fit glyphs to real balloon interiors, real mouth targets and fixed image annotations; split/rewrite before shrinking.

C4 · high

Many panels feel like similarly finished dark fantasy illustrations

Stage: storyboard, composition and rendering brief

Full-frame asset production and repeated surface/lighting hierarchy give each beat comparable visual weight; median filtering cannot redesign shapes or acting.

Change: Variable shot footprint, simpler backgrounds in emotional/action beats, explicit color script and selective detail; test cleaner separable route against well-directed current renderer.

C5 · medium-high

Coherent systems do not reliably create attachment or urgent action

Stage: episode editorial and performance

Theme and numerical reconciliation are clearer than personal stakes, expressive range and reward felt through action. Serious technical dialogue often replaces chemistry.

Change: Keep premise provisional; stage one relationship pressure and one capability change visibly, with state exposition only at decision/consequence.

C6 · medium

Large engineering surface produces limited visible quality returns

Stage: production architecture and incentives

Hypothesis: copied evidence/validation machinery and record milestones may displace sequence-level revision. Actual time allocation and causal direction were not measured; difficult art may instead have prompted more machinery.

Change: Retain small proven core; consolidate only when pilot demands it, allocate explicit editorial correction time.

Bounded attempts

Human gate

Zero semantic, lettering or delivery hard failures. Two independent humans pass comprehension and required dimensions; owner must want to continue. B/C promotion needs explicit preference and predeclared improvement.
