Proof scope and production limits

Art heights follow frozen V2 targets: 5,360 CSS pixels total at a 390-pixel reader width. Lettering sits in independent flowing lanes; expansion increases scroll length rather than shrinking type or covering art. These lanes are a layout proof, not finished speech-tail design.

The cord routing is an explicit rough proposal resolving the residual physical-continuity question. A skilled storyboard artist must still verify grips, travel length, joint/contact mechanics and the crossing timing. No production acceptance gates or human comprehension tests have been passed by this schematic.
