# Recommended roadmap

Ranges, not false precision. Direct paid spend remains $0 until the owner separately authorizes otherwise. Built-in renderer cost is null.

## 30 days — prove the page

- Owner reads `START_HERE.md`, `executive-decision.md`, the HTML hub, and **one** phone reader from Ember CH01 plus this hub at 390×844.
- Authorize or reject the 14-panel pilot spec. Rejection should say whether the disagreement is story, craft, or staffing.
- If authorized: freeze beats/copy/rubric; draw or block storyboards; run at most two routes; record minutes; blinded scores.
- Stop: ten-chapter work, future-cast pages, new validator families, batch `REVIEWED_PASS`.
- Gate: non-null human minutes + blinded comparison exists.

**Effort range:** 40–120 hours if C/B pilot runs; 4–8 hours if owner only reviews this package.  
**Risk:** owner time never appears.

## 90 days — one episode or stop

- If the 14-panel pass criteria hit: produce **one** 16–40 panel episode on the winning route with Ember payload or the new originals from the pilot.
- If layouts were ignored: add a human line/cleanup pass (full C) on the same 14 beats before any episode.
- If owner prefers control hybrid: **do not scale**. Revisit A only as targeted redraws of contact plates, then stop.
- If craft passes and story is rejected: start D on the same spine (new title, 14 panels, not a bible farm).
- Keep: SVG lettering, ledgers, hash records. Freeze `north_garden` validator sprawl.

**Effort range:** 80–200 hours for one episode with a small human pass.  
**Spend:** $0 default; paid renderer only with a new owner message.

## Scale-up (only after the 90-day gate)

- Weekly or biweekly cadence is an industry fact (KOCCA ~6.3 days/episode; WEBTOON Originals often weekly). Do not promise it until one episode is owner-accepted.
- Staffing target if scaling: storyboard/action, cleanup, letterer, editor — even if some seats are the owner plus one specialist. Industry studios use 6–7 artists for full-color weekly; a solo-plus-AI show cannot silently absorb that load.
- Commercial clearance, font licensing (Arial is unbundled), and AI disclosure remain **open**. USCO Part 2 (2025-01-29): prompts alone are not authorship; human modification can be. That is not a platform contract.

**Stop conditions (any phase):** human minutes stay null; owner still names the ToG/SL gap after a competent storyboard; extra-digit or stage-break rate does not fall; programme returns to evidence-about-evidence milestones.

## Dependencies

Pilot → blinded scores → owner questions → (pass) one episode → (pass) cadence discussion. Nothing skips the pilot.
