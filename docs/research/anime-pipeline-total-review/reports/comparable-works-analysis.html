Licensed comparable works: what the reference bar actually asks for

The transferable target is directed attention, character pressure and visible change, rather than a particular fantasy palette or finish. The samples support multiple visual approaches. They do not prove that one model, renderer, flat-color recipe or amount of detail is necessary for premium comics.

Tower of God: desire and relationships carry the image

Observed, prologue: several sharply different faces and creature silhouettes are placed against simple color fields, separated by generous white space. The promise centers on desire before explaining a rules system. Inference: readers can attach to a want and anticipate differentiated participants with little lore burden. Official prologue.

Observed, opening exchange: a distant chase becomes a face, reaching fingers, contact, displaced bodies and reciprocal expressions. The drawings are uneven in anatomy and finish, yet small changes in gaze, mouth and hands tell the interaction. Relationship friction precedes taxonomy. Inference: physical causality and emotional legibility can outperform more polished stationary portraits. This is a craft mechanism, not an explanation of the title's popularity. S1 Ep. 1 opening.

Observed, later sample: the finale opens with elaborate mechanical forms, luminous rings, particles and a tall reveal, followed by a much tighter detail. It contains substantially more surface rendering than the opening samples. This is evidence against prescribing flat or sparse art everywhere. The durable principle is hierarchy and planned contrast; technique and density can vary. No claim about the finale's story success follows from three strips. S3 Ep. 235.

Sourced creator account: in a 2025-09-04 roundtable, SIU discusses avoiding reader boredom, receiving and applying feedback quickly, and characters' chemistry changing the initial story direction. Treat this as his account, not a universal studio formula. It supports giving reader response and character behavior authority over a prewritten plan. Jay Gibbs' SIU interview.

Solo Leveling: make the promise physical

Observed: small hand/weapon details lead to an injured body, an overwhelming size comparison, an eye, a raised weapon and an implied strike. Broad light/dark groups survive the dark setting; isolated effects and breathing balloons control attention. A bright city reset changes the sequence's color and density. The close-up visibly strains rather than merely presenting a handsome face. Inference: the baseline of helplessness makes later agency desirable. Limit: this sample does not show the later progression payoff. Tapas Episode 1.

The prologue intentionally elides contact with a sound/effect transition. A validator requiring every published action scene to spell out anticipation, attack, counter, reversal, impact and aftermath would falsely reject purposeful ellipsis. Require that complete chain in a capability-testing pilot; judge actual scenes by the information their dramatic purpose needs. Same opening sequence.

Sourced premise, not observed payoff: the official Tappytoon synopsis explicitly promises exclusive growth from a weak starting position; it identifies the English edition as licensed. The Yen Press listing adds concrete livelihood pressure and names separate translator and letterer credits. Those facts support clear stakes and division of labor, not guessed salaries, turnaround time, asset practices or AI usage. Tappytoon, Yen Press.

One additional contrast, with a narrower evidence grade

Omniscient Reader's official premise gives its protagonist useful knowledge before physical dominance. Inference from synopsis: Elian's structural perception could be a satisfying agency engine if the reader can anticipate a tactic, see it applied, and understand its cost. A dashboard of escalating numbers is not the only progression contract. This report does not claim to have reviewed Omniscient Reader's panels or score it visually. Official synopsis.

What this changes in the repository

The existing visual bible already asks for simplified shapes, expressive acting, controlled detail and aftermath. The story bible already offers structural perception, shared timing and the cost of self-sacrifice. Another adjective-rich prompt is therefore a weak intervention. Prove those commitments at thumbnail, candidate-selection and finishing stages.

A directly inspected matched example, editorial p039 versus its unique-art source, retains the same posed combatants, dense stone cracks/rivets/chains and dark environment after cleaning. This bounded example supports a ceiling on surface cleanup; it does not alone establish a chapter-wide failure. Use the independent local audits to test frequency. Source paths: experiments/reimaginings/ember-lattice/premium-rd/ch01-unique/p039.png and editorial-clean/p039.png in the protected editorial worktree. No source image was altered.

The derivation matrix converts these observations into original implementation tests. Retain Ember Lattice's fault-reading/shared-timing possibility; require visible chemistry, readable leverage and a satisfying change in capability before scaling. None of these samples establishes that Ember Lattice must be abandoned, that generated imagery has a proven universal ceiling, or that a greenfield pipeline will work without skilled execution.

Counterevidence and exclusions

Popularity is context only: no view count contributes to a score. Early and late episodes are not interchangeable production benchmarks. Simpler opening art can tell a readable relationship; dense later art can deliver a clear spectacle. No later Solo Leveling chapters, paid episodes, full studio production records or commercial rights transfers were inspected. A 2020 Crunchyroll SIU interview redirected to a regional-unavailability page and is excluded as substantive evidence. This analytical access grants no right to reuse designs, settings, characters, compositions or artwork.
