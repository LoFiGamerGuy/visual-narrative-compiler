Controlled proofs and limits

Two bounded proofs address high-value questions without making another comic volume.

Hash validity cannot establish event correctness. The read-only counterexample replay rehashes the actual selected raster for P012/P017/P029/P033/P037. All five recorded selection hashes match, while original source requests and independent visual review identify wrong cast, contact, body state, timing or contextual pressure. P033 is the contextual case, not an equally binary cast/count error. This juxtaposition demonstrates a false-pass boundary of the existing hash gate; it is not a new semantic detector and does not estimate the chapter-wide failure rate.

Static layout and lettering proof. The 14-panel vector rough implements the frozen neutral beats and live text, with original adult schematics, variable footprints and explicit geography. Synthetic copy expansion stresses layout without smaller type. It is a storyboard planning artifact, unaccepted and commercially uncleared, not final drawing or evidence of an A/B/C art winner. Plan and browser QA distinguish target heights from measured proof scroll geometry.

No fair production route comparison was run: original controlled assets, an equivalent skilled correction pass and real human review capacity were unavailable within this audit. Merely invoking an image generator would not supply those missing controls. Human correction hours, owner preference, generator fidelity, final lettering artistry, actual localization and sustained throughput remain untested.

Four deterministic tests pass. They inject meaningful hash/reference/counting failures and constrain proxy interpretation; they do not certify aesthetic quality. The future pilot must use the best-current renderer as an eligible control, preserve all failures and let the owner plus two independent humans choose under the predeclared gates.

Original analysis; no third-party comic panels redistributed. $0 incremental paid/cloud spend. AI reviews are not human acceptance. Source and rights limits remain explicit.
