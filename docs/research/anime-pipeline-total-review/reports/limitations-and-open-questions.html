Metric boundaries

Pixel density/darkness/noise depend on the declared resize/threshold and do not score beauty. Camera/scenario/density tags conflict with actual imagery and cannot be treated as an observed shot histogram. Balloon areas are authored rectangles, not glyph pixels; visible containment/collision is independently reviewed. No complete corpus OCR, manually coded shot/pose/error census, real localization, throttled-device performance or attributable human labor study was performed. These fields remain null or scoped observations in measurements.

The root metadata scan covers all files; content hashing is scoped to eligible files≤32MiB outside excluded vendor/model/dataset/reference directories. This detects ordinary overwrite/change but is not a hostile filesystem-forensics guarantee. Access timestamps and shared mutable Git object/log/fetch administration are excluded.
