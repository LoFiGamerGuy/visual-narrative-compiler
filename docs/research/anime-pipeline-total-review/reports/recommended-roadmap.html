Gated roadmap

This is a planning horizon, not work already performed, a staffing commitment or permission for paid production. Calendar ranges assume access to the required people; if they are unavailable, the gate remains closed. The audit’s current authorization ends at this review package and its bounded static proof.

Effort and direct-spend assumptions

Single14-panel planning ranges including shared preparation: A24–52 human hours, B34–74, C46–102. All three with shared prep80–180h; a fresh story adds8–20h concept/cast exploration. These are estimates, not measured performance, contractor quotations or industry norms. They assume skilled practitioners and existing tools, one correction round, and exclude hiring/training. There is no assumption that AI agent elapsed time equals an artist’s work. Detailed capacity model.

Incremental paid/cloud spend remains$0 for this assignment. A future production budget, artist availability and target platform are unresolved. Do not purchase hardware/models or start a new subscription to avoid confronting a weak storyboard or undefined editorial authority.

Dependency order and ownership

Story intent → thumbnail/action map → actual source fit → lettering/finishing → numeric/delivery checks → independent preference → owner production decision. The selector cannot be the sole judge of its own result; the engineer cannot promote an authored score into artistic approval. Humans may combine roles if competent, but independent review must remain independent.

Retain narrow useful infrastructure. Consolidate repeated hash/schema code only when a pilot operation needs it. Preserve old branches, failed attempts, source revisions and exact nullable records. A character/gear bible becomes production-ready only when a model/prop sheet and an action/acting sample demonstrate its descriptive claims.

Rollback and fallback

  • B loses to A on the same beat/copy surface: retain current renderer and bounded repairs; keep the new storyboard/source-review discipline.
  • Correctly directed A/B still miss acting/contact under budget: C is the fallback only with actual capable human capacity, not a hypothetical team.
  • All art routes fail a shared rough: repair editorial/action planning first; a new model does not fix an incoherent layout.
  • Art passes but Ember preference fails: test D’s premise alternatives on the same proven route before throwing away engineering or commissioning chapters.
  • No decisive gain: stop after the pilot, record why and do not relax thresholds to justify sunk cost.
