Visual and sequential audit

The strongest still images are better than the sequences they assemble into. This is not a finding that every image is bad. The archive contains appealing mature faces, readable monster silhouettes, useful environment scale and some convincing contacts. It also contains wrong event art, repeated visual weight and lettering that covers the information a reader needs. Those are reasons a polished panel collection can remain unlike compelling action comics.

This audit uses direct full-size images and actual continuous390×844 browser reading, not contact sheets alone. Independent B records more than150 sampled captures and exact boundaries; A records locked before reading B; reconciled scores preserve disagreements. Neither AI pass substitutes for owner or human professional review. Reader QA, with the cold-load editorial recheck, records final mobile geometry; source measurements provide defined proxies.

Thirty-five of52 panels are declared low density. Full-size and phone images still repeatedly describe every coat tear, masonry crack, chain and joint. The problem is weak hierarchy across functions, not detail itself. Borrowed’s flat orange tools, Ember volume’s pale-background impact and licensed later Tower of God’s dense spectacle are counterexamples to a blanket sparse/cel rule. An effective color script changes the reader’s state—quiet, threat, impact, relief—rather than applying one global prestige-fantasy treatment.

Use filtering for a confirmed surface defect, not as a substitute for finishing/acting/layout revision.

Stills versus competitive sequence quality

The appealing P029 profile and expressive source pilot P003 demonstrate assets worth retaining. Monster silhouettes and architectural scale can be strong. Neither the audit nor a pair of AI reviewers can certify professional market competitiveness or owner preference. The evidence supports a narrower conclusion: some stills are polished and useful, while the source-to-sequence and assembly process is insufficiently controlled to sustain them.

A professional art director would first ask which line/shape/value supports the beat, then correct expression and contact. An action director would reject an impact without contact and a restraint that silently releases. A reader would feel lost or detached before caring whether the ledger reconciles. More beautiful wrong-event images make those problems harder to notice internally, not less consequential.

Sampling limits and access fidelity

Every named pipeline/mechanism family is inventoried; not every historical candidate or every page of every volume was seen. Each major finished-output family has high-information opening/dialogue/continuity/action/reveal/quiet/dense/ending samples where they exist; UI and boss chains remain unassessed when absent. Engineering controls and previz are not falsely treated as final comics. The exact visual matrix and inaccessible dimensions in B’s report are part of this audit, not fine print.
