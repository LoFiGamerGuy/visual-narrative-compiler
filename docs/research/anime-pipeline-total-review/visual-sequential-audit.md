# Visual and sequential audit

Lead synthesis of independent reviewers visual-a and visual-b plus lead inspection. Pixel paths are in sibling gitignored trees; they were not copied. Full reviewer notes: `evidence/visual-a/notes.md`, `evidence/visual-b/notes.md`, `evidence/lead-visual-inspection.md`.

## Evidence hierarchy applied

Finished reader experience > full-resolution panels > matched before/after > owner records > measurements > scripts. File integrity does not raise art scores.

## Pipeline-by-pipeline

### North Garden / Garden’s Anchor legacy

Inspected: `pagecomp3/page03_p06–p10.png` (accepted kitchen research set), earlier `pagecomp` two-shots, actstage style probes. No lettered reader. No monster/action chain on those pages.

Strength: graphic-novel ink, lamp/fire value grouping, two adults in a real room. This is the closest *drawing language* to comics in the corpus (A: line/value 4).

Failure: the kitchen set mutates (stove vs fireplace, door, shelves) across four “same room” frames. Not a serial. Human minutes null. ComfyUI/LoRA arm is dormant and gitignored.

### Borrowed Down

Inspected: CH01 lettered s01–s03, CH03 action, CH05/CH10 lettered samples, creature-fail diagnostic.

Strength: specific world (dock city, coral/teal print, laboring adults). Mae has body-and-work magnetism. CH03 contact sheet shows weight and rope direction. Highest shared weighted score (2.79).

Failure: lettering is empty beige rectangles in the art plus captions under the border. Square-ish plates, not native vertical manhwa. Face/cape/prop drift. CH10 still ships a kaiju-like head after a named fail. 85 exact duplicate files among 809 PNGs.

### The City Keeps Oaths

Inspected: CH01 landscape opener and crowd, CH05 civic two-shot, phone preview strips, viewer.html.

Strength: coherent blue/gold presentation; braid/cuff identity better than Ember; productized viewer.

Failure: landscape cinematic plates in a vertical-scroll assignment. Generic prestige fantasy. Hands/streak drift. Owner rejected the visual result. Action is pose-and-glow.

### Ember Lattice ten-chapter volume

Inspected: pilot p001–p016, later CH03/CH05/CH10, overlays, phone/full HTML.

Strength: correct portrait webtoon aspect intent; reserved HUD corners; distinctive Belljaw cylinder in CH01; Fault Sight → ankle as a written combat sentence; irreversible ledger.

Failure: each frame is a finished illustration. CH01 action dumps the vault for a cream cyclorama (p007–p008), then outdoor ruins (p010). Hands fail (p013; CH04 p017 six fingers). Chapter endings are party posters plus “next volume” speech. 224/225 `REVIEWED_PASS` by one agent with 10 batch notes. Volume CSS phone column is **430px**, not 390. Source PNG aspect is not locked (104 files at 864×1821 vs SVG viewBox 1024×1536). 132 of 240 volume overlays have min type < 14px at 390.

### Ember premium unique 52-panel CH01

Inspected: unique p002/p015/p039/p026, pre-repair fails, editorial-clean.

Strength: higher peak stills (chain, some impacts). Hybrid overlays are the only 390px-targeted lettering surface.

Failure: photoreal chain vs cel characters vs bakeoff nave plates in one “chapter.” Monster redesign (stone dome vs cylinder). Mira hair/undercut flips. 28 unique rasters of 52. Hybrid/baseline/raw lettering bytes are identical — a false three-route bakeoff.

### Ember editorial hybrid

Strength: deterministic SVG lettering as an object; collision diagnostics; occupied lettering area 367.7% → 221.5%; original 32/52 panels < 12px at 390 vs hybrid 0/52.

Failure: does not redraw hands, hair, or contact. Soft balloons ~94% opaque, not bible 84%. Copy got shorter, not deeper. Cannot repair pose-assembly in the plates.

## Answers to the owner’s visual questions

1. **Stills vs sequences.** Strongest stills can look like mid-tier fantasy illustration. They are not competitive comic panels. Sequences fail *and* many panels are already below the named bar (hands, cream cutouts, landscape City plates).
2. **Generic prestige-fantasy.** Yes for Ember (later painterly plates) and City. No for Borrowed Down print language. No for North Garden kitchen ink.
3. **Magnetism vs consistency.** Ember locked Elian’s costume without making him a character. Mira’s undercut is the only iconic silhouette. Mae (Borrowed Down) has more body-as-person.
4. **Action.** Assembled from poses. Contact is occasional. Aftermath is sit/stand/poster.
5. **Phone.** Premium CSS is 390px. Volume is 430px. Borrowed Down captions under squares. City phone is a landscape filmstrip.

## Reviewer disagreement (preserved)

Visual B is more generous on Ember volume vertical format (pacing/value 4 vs 2). Visual A is harsher on equal-height poster stacking. Lead: format intent is real; the *page* is still poster-stack. North Garden lettering zeros (A) beat B’s 2–3 on pages with no balloons.
