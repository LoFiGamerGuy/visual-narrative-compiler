# CH05 Mill Signal visual-direction assessment — 2026-09-01

## Scope

This is a human-directed assessment of four provenance-limited visual-smoke candidates: P001, P029, P036, and P050. It is not a benchmark result, a production acceptance decision, or a claim that this rendering route is commercially cleared or reproducible.

## Decision captured

The project owner approved the clean CH05 development script, the four-panel contact sheet, and the narrative-development promotion template. The resulting canonical/story/asset/scene-beat/comic-plan records are protected by `production/decisions/ng-decision-ch05-mill-signal-promotion-r1.json`. The four smoke outputs remain unaccepted research candidates.

## What the samples establish

- The desired direction can support atmospheric, grounded-feeling dramatic art with clear adult silhouettes and a legible Soren/Sigrid relationship.
- P036 and P050 communicate practical movement through pose, diagonal staging, cloth, and terrain; this is more useful than blanket speed-line decoration.
- P050 supplies a genuine upper-sky field for dialogue or caption placement. P001 has a small usable atmospheric field. P029 and P036 do not currently preserve a quiet lettering field.

## Density judgement

The samples are closer to a richly illustrated graphic-novel hero-panel treatment than the average high-throughput vertical webcomic panel. That is a strong visual asset for establishing shots, emotional peaks, reveals, and selected action anchors. Used at this density on every panel, however, it will slow phone-size scanning and force speech balloons over faces, texture, or important directional lines.

This is a design judgement from the actual four samples, not a quantitative industry average. The next controlled experiment should test the hypothesis that dialogue/information panels improve when they reserve one deliberately low-detail, high-contrast field while retaining the same palette, character design, and material language.

## Direction added to ComicPanelPlan development

- Use one dominant value mass and a primary phone-size silhouette read before adding environment texture.
- Protect a text field during composition when dialogue/captions are anticipated; a translucent review rectangle is not a substitute for a renderer-directed quiet field.
- Use local, causal motion cues: cloak/twine lift, running weight shift, splash, smoke drift, vibrating bell, or a single directional action line. Do not apply generic speed lines across held observation panels.
- Alternate dense hero/action anchors with calmer dialogue, orientation, and transition panels. A chapter needs visual rest as well as spectacle.
- Keep final copy, balloon shape/tail, reading order, and accessibility in the separate lettering/layout phase.
