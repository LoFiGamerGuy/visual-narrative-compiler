# CH06–CH13 sparse lettering-copy notes r1

## Purpose and boundary

`production/comic/ch06-ch13-lettering-copy-r1.json` supplies deterministic local-lettering copy for the eight complete Bell Road visual chapters. It binds ten sparse lettering beats to existing safe-zone-bearing ComicPanelPlans in each chapter: 80 beats total across CH06–CH13.

This is authoring copy, not rendered pixels, generated-art acceptance, commercial clearance, or chapter promotion. `ComicPanelPlan` remains the only planning structure; `AnimationShotPlan` and E-Conte remain null. Lettering placement inherits each selected panel's existing safe zones and protected-subject contract, so faces, adult silhouettes, important hands, tools, story objects, physical Ledger surfaces, injury/load geometry, and consent/role-order geometry remain unobscured.

## Voice rules

- Soren speaks in compact mechanical observations, load calls, timing, and accountable admissions. His injury changes what he offers and how he moves; wit never erases risk.
- Sigrid speaks in route evidence, decisive verbs, observable comparisons, and explicit authority or consent. Her conclusions follow physical signs rather than unexplained intuition.
- Tamsin is guarded and factual. Her short admissions gain specificity over time; the copy distinguishes falsifying a deterrent route from intending later lethal instability.
- Halvor is terse, procedural, and command-shaped. His bargain and confession acknowledge responsibility without turning him into a simple villain or granting absolution.
- The adult Compact worker voice is collective and practical. It appears only where shared labor, rather than a lone hero, owns the action.
- Narration is sparse and reserved for chapter orientation, quiet evidence turns, and hooks that images alone may not sequence clearly.
- SFX names consequential physical events—weight, fracture, impact, release, and signal cadence—without generic action texture.
- SYSTEM copy is always local lettering attached to an already specified physical substrate such as brass, stone, wet glass, an anvil, a socket, or a real tool. It is never a floating HUD, omniscient prose layer, or replacement for causal evidence.

## Story progression carried by the selected beats

- CH06, *The House That Answered*: suspicious smoke becomes a cautious threshold entry, Tamsin and her injury are introduced, the cellar trap opens, and the physical Garden Ledger recognizes two custodians rather than one keeper before something moves in the orchard.
- CH07, *Mireback at the Gate*: the trio declares roles, defeats a Mireback through water and leverage, learns that defended ground feeds its armor, and commits to repairing the failing northern chain.
- CH08, *The Root Road*: Soren and Sigrid follow physical cairns over faulty ink, revise their threat reading of the Hollow Stag, free it through timed load work, and accept an evidence-led stabilization mission that ends at the Black Weir.
- CH09, *Below the Black Weir*: Sigrid triangulates the true submerged route, Soren's leg is crushed and remains impaired, authority transfers by consent, a Wayfinder path is earned through verified return, and physical sabotage points to Brackenwake.
- CH10, *Iron for a Name*: Halvor tries to bargain for the key, repair work earns access and practical gear, Sigrid records enforceable terms, Halvor admits sacrificing outer nodes for winter light, and the orchard wards erupt before judgment can settle.
- CH11, *The Orchard Siege*: Sigrid rejects abandonment and unsafe fire, Soren coordinates gates within his mobility limit, adult crews defeat the brood Mireback collectively, shared public leadership is earned, and Tamsin reveals the concealed North Garden route.
- CH12, *The Map That Lied*: material forensics expose Tamsin's falsification, unilateral protection breaks the partnership and its physical bond, signals reunite the pair in action, negotiated authority restores consent, and the fused key opens green summer under winter sky.
- CH13, *The North Garden*: the Garden demands a sole keeper, complementary route and load work exposes its consuming design, both adults separately renew consent, Crownroot is spared and bound as a living guardian, shared classes are physically recorded, and a wider failing network becomes the next threat.

## Density and readability

Ten beats per forty-panel chapter leave approximately three unlettered panels between selected beats on average. Dialogue and captions remain short enough for phone reading; quiet panels, causal action, expressions, and visual evidence retain room to carry story. The manifest points to source panel IDs instead of duplicating safe-zone geometry, preventing copy placement from drifting away from the canonical ComicPanelPlans.

No provider, prompt, model, endpoint, request, cost, upload, image, RenderRecord, generated candidate, acceptance decision, or new canon state is created here.
