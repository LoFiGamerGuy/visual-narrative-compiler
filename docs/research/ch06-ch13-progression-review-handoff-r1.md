## Aggregate failure pattern

The strongest route is not failing uniformly. Nineteen blocking candidates cluster into a few repairable classes: generated prose or incorrect prop form in early chapters; Halvor-to-Soren substitution in CH10–CH11; two under-shown causal actions in CH12; premature creature timing; and irreversible shoulder-state resets after CH12 P024. The recurring route otherwise preserves core Soren/Sigrid identity, hair, wardrobe ancestry, practical action, variable cadence, and broad story progression.

## Ranked engineering recommendation

1. **Retain the mature clean-graphic/painterly house route as the chapter-scale semantic backbone.** It produced eight coherent chapters and 296 provisional passes without wholesale rerenders.
2. **Harden irreversible state before style diversification.** One small shoulder-persistence experiment across CH12–CH13 has more information value than another chapter style arm.
3. **Treat secondary-adult identity as a separate mechanism problem.** Halvor failures cluster independently of Soren/Sigrid continuity and should not trigger a whole-route rejection.
4. **Keep lettering local and deterministic.** Generated physical emblems work; generated prose does not. Safe-zone overlays remain the assembly contract.
5. **Use variable panel scale as narrative cadence.** Wide environmental/action anchors, tall causal mechanics, medium dialogue/deduction, and small object inserts are all represented successfully; no single ratio should become the chapter default.
