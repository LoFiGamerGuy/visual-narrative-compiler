## Story progression

CH07 starts at CH06's orchard threat. Tamsin identifies the Mireback's approach; Soren extends a pruning hook into Warden's Reach; Tamsin lends Sigrid a compact recurved bow and five arrows; the pair use rain channels, cloth markers, roots, a gatepost, and the creature's own weight to expose and break its vulnerable root-knot. The defense tears Sigrid's plaid wrap, damages Soren's coat and polehook, fractures the home ward, and ends with the party choosing the north road rather than waiting for another attack.

The monster behaves as peat, slate, root, and momentum—not as a generic dragon or glowing demon. Action is caused by footing, water, impact, leverage, and structural failure.
