# Reusable impact and surface process

Keep the existing [texture-refinement guide](../../docs/texture-refinement-kit/GUIDE.md) as the finishing foundation. This experiment adds action lessons; it does not replace each source image's own drawing treatment.

1. Specify the decisive physical event before the scenery: who applies force, through which limb or implement, at what contact, and what visibly changes in the target's balance or shape. Give the body an active pose and the opponent a distinct reaction.
2. Choose a camera that exposes the decisive contact and consequence. A dramatic lens can increase speed while hiding the ability mechanism, as X02 demonstrates. Judge both independently.
3. Give the image one dominant motion direction and a clear area of highest contrast. Allow broad directional shapes or negative space to do work that dense aura particles cannot.
4. Design the ground as large light/shadow planes first. Keep only joints that explain perspective or footing. Avoid automatic reflection fields, pervasive paver mottling, tiny debris carpets and equal-weight cracks.
5. Generate the full composition with its actual drawing anchor. Review the native image and the actual phone gallery. Check continuous weapon construction, hands, weight-bearing limbs, creature joints, depth and source/action/target/consequence.
6. If structure materially fails, use a bounded targeted repair and review the whole returned image against the source. Record any regression. A repaired contact does not erase a changed claw or an unresolved other hand.
7. Only when visible surface noise warrants it, apply one source-only texture redraw. Protect the actual face, pose, construction, contact and framing. Simplify broad surface interiors while retaining selective face and functional accents. This pass must not quietly redesign anatomy, camera or style.
8. Compare the result at the same phone width and native size. Prefer the finish only if the composition, acting and action survive. Keep every earlier attempt and the exact executed prompt and source hash.

Do not mistake a technically coherent picture for owner approval. Keep excitement, clarity, drawing preference and texture comfort independent. A single successful impact frame also does not prove that an ability or a long fight reads across a sequence.
