# Following arc: work that can be shared

Provisional development outline, grounded in the complete actual reading of Chapters4–6. Chapter6's fresh portable delivery remains a production prerequisite before Chapter7 illustration. These are sequential outlines, not three simultaneous image commitments or owner-approved canon.

The second arc ends with a working route and a paid room. Keep both. Its strongest moments concern useful work, unfamiliar quiet and people making room for one another; its weakest stretch is the repeated administrative counter sequence. The next arc should put a new personal uncertainty into its opening: can Aren entrust part of a paid promise to another worker when he cannot watch every handoff? Give that question a visible journey and an actual choice, with much less time spent explaining records.

## Chapter7 — A Place on the Round

Leave one specific uncertainty about a further receiving place or shared schedule for Chapter8; do not append a manufactured disaster.
