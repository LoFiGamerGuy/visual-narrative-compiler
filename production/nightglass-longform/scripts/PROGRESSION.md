# Nightglass progression and first arc

Provisional edition, not owner-approved canon.

Courier rank records completed entrusted work. Route access is separately granted by the responsible office; combat XP never creates legal permission. A receipt is evidence of a delivered promise, not a score for killing fauna.

Opening: Aren, courier Rank 1, Tension Line I. One visible metal anchor within line of sight, one cyan line from sword pommel. Pulls the holder toward the anchor. Cannot push, move an anchored structure or lift a car. Body and support bear the load. Losing control does not remove momentum.

Chapter 1: rescue overload earns a choice of Reach or Hold under Sera's inspection; it does not erase missed qualification. Aren chooses Hold I: brake during a pull by sustained concentration. Cannot attack while braking; lost concentration resumes ordinary pull. First attempt stops but snags a sleeve; later deliberate use stops cleanly. The Lower Post grants a brass service-door badge and paid provisional route after he opens the entrance. Badge visibly unlocks a door and first delivery gets a real receipt/payment. Neither gain is cancelled.

Chapter 2 realized in the complete49-panel reading: carry Lower Post's receipt-backed petition to an office that requires current doors and an inspected entrance. Maret keeps the case open; Sera walks the actual Hessa/Dain/Rusk doors, excludes the broken public span and witnesses the intact long stair. The petition and yellow folder stay at the office; Aren receives an intake acknowledgment and registered manual return-route authority, not the substantive reply yet. A two-shoulder padded load-spreader and hip belt replace the old single-shoulder carry arrangement. The same gray bag and unchanged load remain; the old gray strap is rolled on the bag side. An authorized staff transfer demonstrates one RIGHT rear-pommel-to-fixed-post line and concentrated supported Hold, followed by ordinary unloading and a returned receipt. The harness distributes weight across shoulders and hips; it grants no added magical force, range, passenger permission or upper-route qualification. Ossa pays the completed round and offers recurring paid morning work. Aren can ask about a room after his first week; he has no key or lease yet. Prior wages, badge and chosen Hold remain. Ossa wants residents counted; Sera wants witnessed evidence; Pell wants usable access and manageable loads; Aren wants a wage and a route that belongs to him.

Chapter 3 intended: restore one local postal circuit and get the first official reply delivered to the district. Conflict involves an improperly energized route beacon drawing glass fauna toward the neglected lift infrastructure. Use observation, signed work orders, Hold and load judgment to shut down/re-align that local beacon with helpers. No giant new combat skill. Consequential local repair plus documented route recognition resolve the arc. An older closure instruction references a second still-operating district, earning the next three-chapter investigation.

No arbitrary damage/XP arithmetic, no cost retrofitted after drawing, no resurrections of handed-off items. Track each scene's owner/location/line/garment state. Chapter2 and3 complete scripts must precede their respective art.
