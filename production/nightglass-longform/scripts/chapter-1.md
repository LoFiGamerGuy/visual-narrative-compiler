# Chapter 1 — An Address That Counts

Provisional new edition. Original pilot wording and sources remain in research/pilot-chapters and production/pilot-chapters.

## N1-01 · tall extreme wide
Night city bridges carry small couriers between warm inhabited windows. Aren crosses toward the upper admission gate.

## N1-02 · medium
Earlier that evening. Aren crouches at an apartment door, handing a modest flat parcel to an older woman in a teal housecoat. She hugs it, surprised.
State: Aren jacket intact; sword sheathed; no powers; ordinary paid delivery complete.
Caption: Earlier that evening.
Recipient: She remembered.
Aren: She booked the delivery last winter.

## N1-03 · intimate threshold two-shot
The woman opens the door wider. Warm dinner light reaches Aren; another place is set at her little table.
State:
Recipient: Stay for soup?
Aren: Tonight I have to be somewhere.

## N1-04 · waist-up private smile
Outside, Aren smooths a crease in his ivory sleeve, glancing up at the distant gold-lit upper gate.
State:
Aren: Please let the jacket survive the interview.

## N1-05 · close hands with editable card space
On a public landing Aren unfolds his courier card. Behind it are the familiar upper route towers.
State:
Card: COURIER · Rank 1
Tension Line I
Upper routes: unlicensed

## N1-06 · medium two-shot
Pell repairs a rail beside the upper approach. Aren stops to offer him the last half of a wrapped roll.
State: No damage, no power, blade sheathed.
Pell: Big night?
Aren: A route with a wage. Not just whatever is left in a pocket.

## N1-07 · close acting
Pell accepts the bread. Aren turns toward the gate, no swagger now.
State:
Aren: I want one room that is mine. And enough time to sit in it.
Pell: Then go earn it.

## N1-08 · wide horizontal mechanical clarity
Aren checks his cyan pommel ring against a small practice post on the bridge. One short thin line connects ring to post; both endpoints visible.
State: Sword in anatomical right hand. No blade contact. No charge counters. Intact jacket.
Aren: One anchor. Pull only. My body carries the load.

## N1-09 · Intimate eye-level two-shot, chest-up.
At the gate Sera indicates the lit delivery-intake booth far BEYOND the turnstile. Aren holds his sealed wallet; his smile is hopeful but guarded. Sera checks entry, not delivery completion.
State: Both on stable bridge; no creature nearby; no power or damage.
Sera: Intake is beyond the gate. Deliver before closing. Then we discuss upper routes.
Aren: I practiced looking unimpressed. How am I doing?

## N1-10 · Tight mechanical/creature insert; beak, coupling and cable clearly separate.
A glass beak clamps the maintenance lift’s suspension coupling below the bridge; one thick cable bends under its bite.
State: Lift remains attached but skewed; Pell inside, hands on rail; ray beak intact.

## N1-11 · Wide vertical geography: Aren upper right, car lower left, ray outside car.
Pell’s car hangs tilted below the walkway. Aren turns away from the gate toward his shout.
State: Single taut suspension cable holds lift; Pell still harnessed; Aren on bridge above.
Pell: The brake is gone!
Sera: Aren. That gate will not wait.

## N1-12 · Close hands and Aren’s determined face; no ray or floor detail.
Aren places the sealed dispatch wallet on Sera’s open palm and draws his crescent with the right hand.
State: Wallet transferred unopened to Sera; sword right hand; jacket intact; no line.
Aren: Neither will he.
Sera: You understand what you are giving up?

## N1-13 · Low medium side, complete boots and both line endpoints.
Aren crouches at the bridge edge and attaches his pommel line to the heavy post behind his right shoulder.
State: One taut line from right sword pommel to visible post; both boots braced on bridge; lift below out of frame.
Aren: It pulls me. Not the whole car.
Aren: Keep your harness on, Pell.

## N1-14 · High oblique wide with clear upper anchor, lift and adult size contrast.
Aren descends onto the tilted lift roof, a taut line climbing from pommel to the bridge anchor above-right. Pell looks up through the open hatch.
State: Aren on roof, both boots spread; line holds him toward upper post; Pell harness still clipped. Jacket intact.
Pell: I’m still clipped.
Aren: Wait for my hand.

## N1-15 · Low extreme-wide threat scale, Aren under 18% frame height; one focal cyan eye.
The ray releases the coupling and turns its beak toward Aren on the roof; its immense wing blots out the upper city.
State: Cable still supports car; ray beak intact; Aren right sword between beak and hatch, left arm free.

## N1-16 · tight side contact
New storyboard: close from Aren’s RIGHT side. The crescent blade in his RIGHT hand catches the lower beak; the cyan pommel ring behind his fist connects diagonally UP-RIGHT to the distant post, visibly clear of the blade and beak. Left hand braces a roof rim. The huge ray pushes inward.
State: Rescue underway; Pell inside roof hatch; jacket hem snags and tears; right weapon; single pommel-to-post line.
Aren: Stay behind me!

## N1-17 · wide force/geography
New storyboard: roof side view. Aren yields one step toward the upper-right anchor, using the pull to move HIS BODY and keep the blade between beak and hatch. The ray’s beak slides past the roof edge to screen right; blade disengages. Pell reaches the hatch behind Aren. One line from pommel behind right fist to post, never from blade.
State: Torn hem persists. Right weapon throughout. Boots on roof; no magical lift of car or ray.
Aren: Pell. Unclip. Now.

## N1-18 · tight forearms and open safety snap; no weapon in crop
Explicit storyboard revision 3: tight forearm-and-clasp frame at the roof hatch. Aren’s LEFT forearm enters from upper-right. Pell’s RIGHT gloved hand grips it securely. Pell’s LEFT hand has just unclipped an OPEN safety snap from the D-ring on his own worn harness; the released tether still trails into the car, attached to its rail. Pell remains supported at the hatch. Aren’s right-hand weapon and line are outside this close frame.
State: Aren LEFT arm rescues, RIGHT arm retains sword outsideframe. Pell RIGHT hand grips Aren; Pell LEFT hand holds open unfastened snap. Aren sleeves intact; torn hem edge may show. No leap until next panel.
Pell: I’m clear.
Aren: Hold on.

## N1-19 · wide rear three-quarter; two people, empty car and both line endpoints
Explicit storyboard revision 3: rear three-quarter wide view, Aren’s ivory jacket BACK visibly faces the viewer. His anatomical RIGHT arm rises up-right holding the crescent sword, with one cyan line from the pommel behind his fist to a fixed post on the upper-right bridge. His LEFT arm reaches down-left, securely clasping Pell’s RIGHT hand. Pell has cleared the empty lift car below-left; his safety tether no longer connects him to the car. The line pulls Aren and the person he carries toward the bridge, leaving the falling car behind.
State: RIGHT weapon, LEFT rescue arm; one rear-pommel line to fixed bridge post. Pell has no car tether attached to his harness. Aren sleeves intact, torn hem persists. Pull lifts the carrier and carried person; never the car.
Aren: One line. Two people. Move.

## N1-20 · Ground-level medium three-person aftermath with quiet surface.
Aren and Pell sprawl safely on the bridge, Sera gripping Pell’s harness shoulder; Aren’s sword rests in his right hand beside him.
State: Both rescued adults supported by dry deck; line slack; no new wounds. Gate visibly shut behind Sera.
Pell: Thank you.
Aren: Give me a moment.

## N1-21 · Close face and rejected wallet, city reduced to broad shapes.
Sera returns the unstamped sealed wallet. Aren stares at the closed gate over her shoulder.
State: Wallet remains unopened; jacket hem torn; right sword sheathed; no active power.
Sera: No stamp. I cannot change that.
Aren: I know. That does not make me less angry.

## N1-22 · Tactile close envelope and hands; enough quiet area for label and speech.
Pell offers a second small black envelope with a simple silver broken-circle seal. No generated address lettering is required; Pell identifies the addressee in dialogue.
State: Original wallet on belt; new black envelope intact in Pell’s hand. All safely on bridge.
Pell: This is addressed to you. From Lower District.
Aren: That district has been empty for twenty years.

## N1-23 · Long rear three-quarter, Aren under 20% frame height; vast lower-city descent.
Aren stands at a descending stair into the fog, black envelope in LEFT hand, right sword sheathed; far below, a dark doorway lights cyan.
State: Safe bridge behind; torn jacket hem retained; no active tether; new address opens a future route, no resolution claimed for city mystery.

## N1-24 · hands and faces
At the stair top Aren has not opened the black envelope. Pell gently takes it back for a moment to point out its broken-circle seal.
State: Pell holds black envelope; Aren cream wallet closed at belt; torn hem, sleeve still intact.
Pell: A job offer. Show them this seal. I gave them your name.
Aren: Who is them?

## N1-25 · long over-shoulder
Pell points down the immense old postal stair. Several warm windows lie beneath the official empty-district sign.
State:
Pell: People who still need their post.
Aren: You could have told the gate inspector.

## N1-26 · close Sera; no weapon action
Sera joins them at the stair top, carrying her route ledger under her arm.
State:
Sera: He did. Twice. I filed both reports.

## N1-27 · intimate two-shot with small editable card
Sera shows Aren the recorded overload mark on his courier card; his sore shoulder stays low.
State:
Sera: Your line held a second person. You qualify for a control branch.
Aren: But not the gate.

## N1-28 · close card and eyes
The card offers two plain options. Aren studies the narrow landing below rather than the lit upper towers.
State:
Card: CHOOSE A BRANCH
Reach: longer anchor range
Hold: brake during a pull
Sera: Hold needs your full attention. You cannot strike while braking.

## N1-29 · close decision; earned before action
Aren deliberately chooses Hold with his left thumb. The pommel ring on his sheathed sword shows one steady cyan band.
State:
Aren: Then teach me to stop.
Card: Hold I learned
One line · fixed metal anchor
Release concentration: pull resumes

## N1-30 · wide downward geography
Pell points to a lower postal landing three metres below the upper stair. The normal stair is blocked by an old closed wicket; a black anchor post stands on the lower landing, with a snagged iron handrail tooth beside it.
State: Black envelope in Pell hand; intact left sleeve; hem torn. Aren on upper landing, sword sheathed.
Pell: You can reach that post. Open the wicket from below.
Sera: Look at where you will stop.

## N1-31 · close hands, Pell left and Aren right
Pell transfers the closed BLACK envelope with broken silver-circle seal into Aren’s open LEFT palm. Aren’s right hand rests on his sheathed crescent hilt. Both stand on the upper landing.
State: End: Aren owns black envelope LEFT hand; blade sheathed right hip; hem torn, sleeves intact; no line.
Pell: Keep it dry. They have waited long enough.

## N1-32 · chest/waist three-quarter
Aren tucks the black envelope completely inside his inner chest pocket using his LEFT hand. His RIGHT hand draws the crescent sword. Pell watches from the upper stair.
State: Envelope now hidden inside chest, never at belt; right hand owns sword; no line; torn hem, sleeves intact.
Aren: Inside the coat. Both hands free to work.

## N1-33 · wide side showing whole human and both endpoints
Aren steps off the upper stair toward the landing THREE metres down-right. One thin cyan line runs from the pommel ring behind his RIGHT fist to the BLACK metal post on that lower landing. Sword points left, away from the line. Empty left arm balances; boots airborne.
State: Envelope hidden chest; one taut pommel-to-post line; torn hem; left sleeve intact; Pell remains upper stair.

## N1-34 · medium side, feet and snag readable
Aren applies Hold and slows at the lower landing. Boots touch stone; his LEFT elbow catches the projecting iron tooth beside the post, making one clear tear in the ivory LEFT sleeve. His right sword points down-left; cyan line remains ring-to-post, taut during his last braking instant; boots are just making contact.
State: New torn LEFT elbow sleeve; previous torn hem persists. Envelope still hidden chest. One ring-to-post line, no blade contact with rail.
Aren: Hold—

## N1-35 · tight left-profile face and torn elbow; offscreen Sera upper-left
Explicit storyboard revision 2: the line is off and the sword is now sheathed. Close from Aren’s anatomical left side: he lowers his eyes toward the torn LEFT elbow sleeve; both empty hands hang naturally outside the close framing. ## N1-36 · intimate envelope insert, tight crop deliberately excludes hem
Explicit storyboard revision 2: close insert of Aren’s two gloved hands holding the one intact BLACK envelope against his black shirt. ## N1-37 · medium latch and opening passage
The envelope is back inside Aren’s coat. With sword still sheathed, he lifts the wicket latch with his LEFT hand. Pell and Sera descend the regular stair through the opening; show the opened passage and the three separate bodies.
State:
Pell: First working entrance in six months.

## N1-38 · wide inhabited interior reveal
Beyond the wicket, a small postal hall is lit by amber lamps. Several adult residents stand patiently with envelopes; an older woman with silver braid and indigo apron waits at a sorting table.
State: Aren sword sheathed; envelope hidden. No glass creature inside.

## N1-39 · medium counter two-shot
The postmistress Ossa accepts the black envelope from Aren; she reads his name on the inner note, then looks at the torn sleeve.
State:
Ossa: Aren Vale. You found the door.
Aren: It needed an argument.

## N1-40 · tactile letters and worn hands
Ossa lays the opened black envelope beside a bundle of ordinary outgoing letters, each with a different hand-written address kept illegible in art.
State:
Ossa: We have written for twenty years.
Ossa: The returns say no such address.

## N1-41 · quiet affectionate close; siblings revealed through familiarity
Pell hands Ossa the remains of the bread Aren gave him earlier. She splits it without asking and puts half back in his hand.
State:
Pell: I said he would come.
Ossa: You always say that. Today you were right.

## N1-42 · close reward and face
Ossa offers Aren a small brass rectangular district route badge, plain and practical, on her open palm.
State:
Ossa: Bring one delivery back with a receipt. This route pays.
Aren: A real route?

## N1-43 · close pleased acting
Aren fastens the brass badge at the top of his red courier cord, smiling openly for the first time since the gate shut.
State:
Ossa: A real address. Start there.
Card: Lower Post · provisional courier
Route access: postal service doors

## N1-44 · close badge-to-lock and open gap
At a service door Aren presses the small brass rectangular badge on his red cord to the lock plate with his LEFT hand; the door has just swung open. His RIGHT hand holds the one small cloth-wrapped parcel Pell has given him. Beyond the door a missing span interrupts the external maintenance bridge; across it the apartment recipient’s dark-green door and warm lantern are recognizable. Pell stands behind with empty hands.
State: New access earned in prior panel visibly works. Blade sheathed; jacket tears remain.

## N1-45 · wide side clean landing, deliberately echoing failed stop
Aren crosses the missing span of the external maintenance bridge with his one pommel line and Hold, stopping cleanly on the apartment landing this time. Cloth parcel secured inside coat, no sword strike; both endpoints unobscured.
State: Hold used with full concentration; one right pommel line to visible fixed metal post. Envelope now remains Ossa. Existing tears persist; no fresh damage.
Aren: Before the railing.

## N1-46 · intimate payoff
He hands the wrapped parcel to the waiting older apartment recipient from panel 2, at her own apartment door reached by the reopened maintenance shortcut. She opens it to find a repaired kettle handle and laughs in relief.
State: Delivery done. Blade sheathed. Torn sleeve/hem remain.
Recipient: Now you have time for soup?
Aren: One bowl. Then I have letters.
Caption: She signed for the parcel. He stayed for the soup.

## N1-47 · hands and warm face, reward held not cancelled
Back at Ossa’s counter, Aren returns alone and hands over the signed receipt. Ossa gives him the outgoing bundle and his first small payment; no arbitrary coin total.
State:
Ossa: First delivery received.
Aren: Keep the door open. I am coming back.
