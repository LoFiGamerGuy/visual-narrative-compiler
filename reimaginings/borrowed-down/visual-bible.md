# Pressure-print visual and production bible

## Thesis

A tactile gravity adventure printed like an urgent civic broadside: carved black edges, three imperfect spot inks, big readable bodies, and empty paper used as visible force.

## Exclusions

No clear-line watercolor, soft painterly fantasy, photorealism, glossy 3D, generic anime faces, generic floating UI, living-artist imitation, micro-text, embedded dialogue, ornate decorative borders, sexualization, or child-coded proportions.

## Drawing system

- **Line/edge:** thick dry-brush black keyline; interior hatch only for load, injury, and weather. Off-register ink is intentional but faces remain aligned.
- **Shape:** wedges, hooks, counterweights, arcs. Crown architecture uses rigid triangles; South Hook uses braided curves.
- **Proportions:** adult heads roughly 1:7–1:7.5 of height; long angular torsos; large readable hands and grounded feet.
- **Faces:** block brows, simple nose planes, two-value mouths; expressions carried by brow angle, jaw tension, shoulder posture, and eye aperture.
- **Hair anchors:** Mae's single blunt black wedge braid reaches shoulder blades; Dax's silver-black crest leans left; Orra is shaved; Eshe's bob is horizontal.
- **Silhouettes:** each lead recognizable in solid black at 96 px high. Avoid cape/braid overlap that merges identities.

## Palette progression

- CH01–02: soot black, oxidized teal, warning coral, warm paper.
- CH03–04: teal deepens; acid yellow appears only in charged saltglass and clues.
- CH05–06: coral dominates danger; paper fields widen during betrayal and loss.
- CH07–08: indigo substitute for teal in drowned spaces; yellow load-lines multiply.
- CH09: near-black civic interior with coral alarms.
- CH10: all three inks interlock; dawn paper becomes brightest value.

## Light and texture

Lighting is graphic rather than realistic: one dominant value break per panel. Paper grain visible in calm fields; no more than 20% hatch coverage in dialogue panels, 45% in action anchors. Background tiers: T0 blank force field, T1 silhouette geography, T2 functional props, T3 chapter-establishing mechanical detail.

## Action and motion

Show footing, taut line direction, load source, contact, and remaining consequence. Motion appears as displaced ink blocks, rope catenaries, spray geometry, and repeated silhouettes—not generic speed lines. Before/after pairs preserve camera geography.

## Monsters

Sea life derives from pressure organs and navigation fins: asymmetrical but physically coherent. Keelbacks are whale-sized black crescents with coral rib vents; glass skates are translucent teal diamonds with yellow nerve knots; knot crows are six-winged paper-black scavengers. Never add eyes or teeth merely to signal evil.

## Costume/equipment evolution

- Mae: ochre coat → torn right sleeve CH03 → compression wrap CH04–06 → short indigo civic cape CH08 → coat cut into load-web tabs CH10.
- Dax: coral half-cape → scorched hem CH02 → long tail torn away CH05 while the short coral collar wrap persists → archive sash CH07 → yellow witness cords CH09–10.
- Harness knots accumulate visibly and used knots turn gray and fray.
- Tools: Mae's folding marlinspike, Dax's barometer gauntlet, shared anchor-heart in a padded triangular cradle.

## Prop/system language

Functional objects use triangles and exposed pins. Saltglass cord glows acid yellow only while charged. Stored forces are visible as tiny repeated marks within the braid; release is a white negative-space vector ending at a real substrate. No floating status windows.

## Panels and density

Each chapter has six sequence sheets of five panels. Crop grid is a 1×5 vertical strip with gutters; individual panels alternate 4:3 landscape, 1:1, and 3:4 portrait roles within the assembled long scroll through deterministic letterboxing. Use one T3 anchor, two T2 causal panels, one T1 emotion panel, and one T0/T1 breath panel per sequence when story allows.

## Lettering

Local Atkinson Hyperlegible or DejaVu Sans Bold; cream balloons with black 3 px stroke, coral speaker ticks, rectangular captions, hand-cut SFX rendered locally. Minimum final phone text: 16 px at 390 px preview width. Maximum 24 words per balloon and normally two balloons per panel. Safe zones are `[left, top, right, bottom]`, never width/height. Tail endpoints are deterministic metadata.

## Continuity priority

1. adult role identity and count; 2. hair/silhouette; 3. irreversible injury and garment state; 4. anchor-heart and harness state; 5. causal geography and line tension; 6. expression; 7. hands; 8. palette chapter state; 9. texture refinement.

## Generation reliability

Generate text-free five-panel sequence strips. Prefer two visible adults or fewer per panel. Reassert exact order, wardrobe, equipment, and irreversible state in every sequence prompt. Treat beauty without semantic compliance as a failure.
