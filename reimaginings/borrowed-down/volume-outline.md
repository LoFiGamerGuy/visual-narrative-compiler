# Volume I — *Borrowed Down*

Exactly ten chronological chapters, 30 panels and six sequences each.

| Chapter | Title | Objective and choice | Persistent change | Closing turn |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CH01 | The Evening Bell Falls Up | Mae must keep a tram from falling during a gravity reversal; she breaks protocol to accept Dax's illegal forecast. | Mae stores her first pull-knot; right glove tears; Dax becomes wanted. | The civic bell points toward the sea instead of down. |
| CH02 | Weight for Sale | Reach the South Hook engine; expose selective weight rationing or preserve calm. Mae publicly shows the diverted load gauge. | Dax's cape hem scorches; district learns the fraud; anchor-heart stolen. | Orra orders South Hook detached at dawn. |
| CH03 | Five Streets Sideways | Carry the anchor-heart across rotating streets. Dax admits he knew a hidden path would collapse. Mae saves trapped inspectors anyway. | Mae tears right sleeve and bruises ribs; supplies lost; inspectors owe her. | A glass skate emerges from a wall-sea crack carrying a Council tag. |
| CH04 | The Animal in the Equation | Follow the tagged skate into a maintenance rib. They choose to free it instead of harvesting its pressure organ. | Mae gains compression wrap; Dax earns breath-knot; Orra loses a tracking asset. | They see the engine chain embedded in a juvenile keelback. |
| CH05 | What Dax Did Not Say | Cross the market during an induced lean. Dax reveals his dead crew found this evidence years ago and he used Mae to reach it. | Trust ruptures; Dax loses the long cape tail saving Mae but retains its torn collar wrap; anchor-heart casing dents. | Mae walks away with the heart; Orra offers her command. |
| CH06 | A Better Kind of Obedience | Mae enters Crown works to verify Orra's math while Dax helps South Hook evacuate. Mae rejects sole command and leaks the load map. | Mae becomes a fugitive; Dax organizes without charm; public sees sacrifice plan. | Eshe reveals the original lattice required thousands of voluntary nodes. |
| CH07 | The Archive Has Tides | Siblings Dax and Eshe retrieve consent protocols as the archive floods sideways. Dax chooses evidence over revenge. | Dax earns witness cords; Eshe joins; Mae and Dax begin repair, not forgiveness. | The protocol names a living "Span" beneath the city. |
| CH08 | Under Veyr | Descend to the old span chamber and wake its mechanisms. Mae must share rather than absorb a lethal load. | Tavi becomes Caller; Mae gains civic cape; group can braid shared force. | The Span is the adult keelback mother, pinned to power Crown stability. |
| CH09 | The Plumb War | Reach the central anchor while Council guards and collapsing gravity split the city. Mae refuses to kill Orra; Orra chooses to maintain the chain. | Dax takes a shoulder puncture; Orra's staff breaks; Crown loses privilege. | Cutting the chain will drop every district unless citizens answer the call. |
| CH10 | All of Us Are Down | Build a citywide voluntary load-web, cut the mother free, and survive the redistributed fall. Mae trusts thousands; Dax broadcasts the unvarnished risk. | Central anchor destroyed; mother and calf reunited; injuries and debts persist; citizen Span holds. | At dawn the wall-sea leans away—and distant seas answer with new bells. |

The volume resolves the corrupt anchor crisis and transforms civic power. The last signal opens a wider world rather than undoing the victory.
