# Ember Lattice cultivation bible

Pressure Breath routes ambient dungeon heat through three anatomical circuits: collarbone seed, sternum channel, and diaphragm core. Cultivation is physical capacity and control; levels remain Brass Ledger recognition of verified contribution.

## Ordered states and gates

| State | Capacity effect | Required gate | Failure state |

| Breath Seed I | baseline | awaken collarbone circuit | coughing, no advancement |
| Breath Seed II | +8 max Qi | complete a broken breath while under real threat; catalyst allowed | Overburn |

## Visible grammar

Seed states show one ember fissure at Elian's right collarbone. Channel I extends a thin line to the sternum and changes exhaled vapor into two deliberate pulses. Channel II adds a short lower arc at the ribs; it never becomes decorative full-body fire. Advancement must visibly change posture, breath timing, resource capacity, and available skill control.

Every cost, capacity change, injury, catalyst, and failure consequence appears once in the SystemState transactions and persists until explicitly reversed.
