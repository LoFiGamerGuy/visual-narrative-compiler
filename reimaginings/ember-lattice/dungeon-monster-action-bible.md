# Dungeon, monster, boss, and action bible

## Hollow Meridian

An original descending dungeon built from vast fired-clay ribs and suspended iron chains around a geothermal void. Architecture stays simple in ordinary panels: one arch, one chain, one depth gradient. Ember Vault is teal-black with isolated orange cracks; Verdigris Sump is flooded copper-green; Crownshaft is ivory ash and black iron.

## Belljaw Warden (`enemy-belljaw`)

Level 6 quadruped, six meters long. Iconic silhouette: low ivory kiln-ceramic shoulder shell, long black forelimbs, blunt bell-shaped lower jaw with one orange seam, no horns or wings. It attacks by charge, jaw clamp, and rotational wrench. Weak point is a hairline ankle fault visible only after Mira fixes its jaw direction. No gratuitous gore.

## Pilot choreography

P05 geography; P06 charge; P07 Mira initiates anchor; P08 boss counters by clamping and twisting spear; P09 Elian reads ankle fault; P10 Elian moves under jaw; P11 boss backhands him into chain; P12 consequence and bottleneck; P13 inventory sacrifice; P14 breakthrough and skill unlock; P15 Fault Step plus Rampart Thrust combo breaks ankle plate and collapses boss; P16 aftermath and state change.

Movement direction remains boss right→left through P08; Elian reverses left→right in P10; P15 converges Mira left→right and Elian bottom→top at the orange ankle seam.

## Zones

- **Ember Vault / Chainworks:** black basalt, suspended iron chains, ivory ceramic mechanisms, sparse ember seams, strong vertical void. Ordinary panels simplify to charcoal/teal fields with one chain or platform cue.
- **Glass Gallery:** dark translucent mineral ribs, pale echo planes, narrow brass survey rails. Glass is localized to silhouette edges; no all-over glitter.
- **Verdigris Sump:** oxidized copper pump columns, dark teal water, broad moss planes, one pale pump-heart glow. Acid/flood effects are flat directional sheets, not particle fog.
- **Resonance Cistern:** concentric brass ribs over deep green water, large negative-space arches, one vibrating central throat.
- **Crownshaft:** ivory lift ribs, charcoal vertical cable, brass crown mechanisms, cold dawn at the distant surface. Final chamber retains readable platforms and three stable landmarks.

## Enemies and bosses

- **Cinder Mites, Lv2:** palm-sized ivory beetles with ember abdomens; attack by cutting chain pins, not swarming as visual noise.
- **Glassback Skitter, Lv5 sub-boss:** six low legs, one transparent mineral carapace, blunt wedge head; resonance counter sends a flat shock arc. Weak point moves only after its three-note pulse.
- **Crownspike Bailiff, Lv9 guardian:** tall ivory-and-brass biped with one vertical crown spike and a migrating chest sigil. It defeats the party because a static fault read becomes false.
- **Mire Choir, Lv4–6:** three reed-bodied amphibious constructs sharing one breath rhythm. Their coordinated water lanes make geography explicit.
- **Collapse Hound, Lv8 major sub-boss:** broad ceramic quadruped with a counterweight tail; bites load-bearing faults and weaponizes ceiling failure.
- **Brass Maw, Lv10 boss:** squat ring-bodied cistern predator, three hinged jaw arcs, one resonant throat; every attack follows an audible/visible three-beat cycle.
- **Bell Regent, Lv12 volume boss:** towering ivory bell torso over four long jointed legs, suspended crown yoke, hollow face aperture. It climbs rather than flies. Its hostile note travels as one readable ring that bends cables and platforms.

## Fight map

Six substantial fights: Belljaw (CH01), Cinder-Mite chain chase (CH02), Glassback (CH03), Bailiff defeat (CH04), Mire Choir/pump defense (CH06), Collapse Hound (CH07), Brass Maw (CH08), Crownshaft guards (CH09), Bell Regent (CH10). Each has an eight-beat causal plan stored with the chapter plans. Poses, vectors, contact, consequences, and altered state must remain legible without effects or copy.
