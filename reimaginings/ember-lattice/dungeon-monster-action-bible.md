# Dungeon, monster, boss, and action bible

## Hollow Meridian

An original descending dungeon built from vast fired-clay ribs and suspended iron chains around a geothermal void. Architecture stays simple in ordinary panels: one arch, one chain, one depth gradient. Ember Vault is teal-black with isolated orange cracks; Verdigris Sump is flooded copper-green; Crownshaft is ivory ash and black iron.

## Belljaw Warden (`enemy-belljaw`)

Level 6 quadruped, six meters long. Iconic silhouette: low ivory kiln-ceramic shoulder shell, long black forelimbs, blunt bell-shaped lower jaw with one orange seam, no horns or wings. It attacks by charge, jaw clamp, and rotational wrench. Weak point is a hairline ankle fault visible only after Mira fixes its jaw direction. No gratuitous gore.

## Pilot choreography

P05 geography; P06 charge; P07 Mira initiates anchor; P08 boss counters by clamping and twisting spear; P09 Elian reads ankle fault; P10 Elian moves under jaw; P11 boss backhands him into chain; P12 consequence and bottleneck; P13 inventory sacrifice; P14 breakthrough and skill unlock; P15 Fault Step plus Rampart Thrust combo breaks ankle plate and collapses boss; P16 aftermath and state change.

Movement direction remains boss right→left through P08; Elian reverses left→right in P10; P15 converges Mira left→right and Elian bottom→top at the orange ankle seam.
