Free Delvers reputation begins 0, moves +4 CH02, +2 CH03, −2 CH04, +8 CH05, +15 CH06–07, +5 CH08, +6 CH09, +20 CH10 = 58 (`Trusted Salvager`). Ash Crown reputation begins 0, becomes +5 after the race result, then −15 when Elian aids Sable, and −25 when the conspiracy is exposed = −35 (`Marked Enemy`). Party trust begins 3 after the pilot and ends 42. All transitions require a shown cause.
