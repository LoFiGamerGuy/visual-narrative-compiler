# *Ember Lattice* — preliminary story bible

## Promise

Adult dungeon salvager Elian Voss can see structural faults but has never possessed the class strength to exploit them. When the buried Hollow Meridian wakes early, the brass Ledger offers a locked class path: descend, clear three meridian zones, and earn a Cinder-Key before the Bell Regent climbs to the surface lift. Every gain changes how Elian fights; every shortcut risks Breath Deviation.

## Cast

- **Elian Voss, 27:** fair/light neutral complexion; failed Salvager, Level 3; observant, dry humor, refuses to abandon partners. Starts with a hooked shortblade and frayed gray-green coat. Volume path: Salvager → Faultline Adept → Rift Temperer.
- **Mira Vale, 32:** fair/light neutral complexion with peach undertone; Bastion Lancer, Level 5; copper-red angular bob, steel-gray eyes, tall athletic build. Direct, protective, impatient with self-pity. Uses split shield and long spear.
- **Orin Pell, 41:** light complexion, broad square face, dark hair graying at temples; adult forge-medic and item refiner introduced CH03.
- **Sable Renn, 35:** fair complexion, narrow face, black undercut, amber eyes; rival guild delver whose efficient build hides a dangerous cultivation debt.

All humans are fictional adults with mature proportions and practical, non-sexualized equipment. Complexion is never tied to morality, class, faction, purity, or power.

## Volume conflict

The Ash Crown guild intends to harvest the sleeping Bell Regent's core by letting its ascent destroy the public lift. Elian's party must clear the Meridian before the planned breach. CH10 resolves the ascent and exposes the guild's sponsor; a sealed fourth meridian provides the next-volume vector.

## Ten-chapter outline

1. **The Bridge That Bites:** live crisis in Ember Vault; Level 3 status; Elian and Mira defeat Belljaw Warden; Level 4 and class key unlock.
2. **Choose the Crack:** Faultline Adept selected over safer Appraiser; first gear/loadout decision; cinder-mite chase through collapsing chainworks.
3. **Breath Has Teeth:** Orin teaches pressure breathing; Fault Sight acquired; Glassback sub-boss defeated at a visible wound cost.
4. **The Wrong Victory:** Sable races the party; Ash Crown guardian overwhelms Elian; serious defeat, cracked focus-lens, Mira's spear bent.
5. **Inventory of Ash:** scarce salves force healing choice; hookblade refined with Belljaw tendon; Breath Seed bottleneck and trust conflict.
6. **Verdigris Sump:** second zone; reputation quest; first three-person combo; Fault Step ranks up after a flood hazard.
7. **Rift Temperer:** class evolution and Breath Channel breakthrough under a ceiling collapse; new two-stage fighting method wins only part of the zone.
8. **The Guild's Short Cut:** Sable's cultivation debt detonates; Brass Maw boss; hidden quest advances and party loses the surface-route token.
9. **Crownshaft Assault:** final-zone preparation, explicit inventory discard, equipment payoff, coordinated combo; Bell Regent awakens early.
10. **Strike the Silence:** decisive vertical boss fight; Rift Draw evolves; Bell Regent defeated, public lift saved, sponsor revealed; Chapter 1/10 status comparison and sealed fourth meridian hook.

## Emotional line

Elian begins believing usefulness means spending himself. Mira's refusal to treat sacrifice as strategy forces him to build a class based on shared timing rather than solitary damage. Their bond remains adult, restrained, and action-tested.
