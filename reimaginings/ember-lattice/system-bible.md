# Brass Ledger LitRPG system bible

The **Brass Ledger** is an impartial dungeon accounting phenomenon that records verified risk, contribution, and material provenance. It does not choose morality and cannot invent loot. Its local UI uses cut-corner charcoal plates, brass hairlines, ember ticks, and ivory type.

## Identity and advancement

- Levels grant one attribute point and increase HP/Qi according to class growth.
- XP thresholds: L3→4: 100; L4→5: 140; L5→6: 190; L6→7: 250; L7→8: 320.
- Carry XP remains after a level. Provenance tags: kill share, quest, discovery, refinement, training.
- Core stats: Force, Step, Sense, Guard. Derived resources: HP and Qi. No ornamental stats.

## Cultivation

Ordered realms: Breath Seed I–III → Breath Channel I–III → Ember Core I–III. Pressure Breath cycles ambient dungeon heat through the collarbone meridian. A breakthrough needs (a) full declared qi pattern, (b) insight or catalyst, and (c) a real risk. Failure applies Breath Deviation. Cultivation raises qi capacity and unlocks the physiological control needed for class skills; it does not replace XP.

## Class path

- Salvager: reads material value, two inventory utility slots.
- Faultline Adept (CH02): exploits verified structural faults; gains Fault Sight and Fault Step compatibility.
- Rift Temperer (CH07): can create a temporary fault only after reading and surviving an enemy pattern; cannot mark living allies.

## Pilot skills

- `skill-fault-sight-1`: Fault Sight I; active; 12 Qi; 20s cooldown; reveals one verified stress line for 6s; acquired from damaged survey lens training.
- `skill-fault-step-1`: Fault Step I; active; 8 Qi; 8s cooldown; one acceleration burst along a verified fault; unlocked by Breath Seed II breakthrough.
- `skill-rampart-thrust-2`: Rampart Thrust II; Mira; 18 stamina; 12s cooldown; anchors shield then redirects one charging mass.

## Items and rarity

Rarities: Common, Tempered, Rare, Relic. Pilot inventory capacity is 6 slots / 10 weight. Spark Talisman (Tempered, weight 1) can be consumed for +14 Qi and two panels of Overburn (-1 HP/panel). Iron Seal x2 (Common, stack weight 1) stabilizes one bridge anchor. Cinder-Key (Rare, weight 1) unlocks class selection and Meridian lift access.

## Quests and dungeons

`quest-bridge-that-bites`: defeat Belljaw Warden and keep at least one bridge anchor intact. Reward: 85 kill-share XP, Cinder-Key, Faultline class options. Failure: Ember Vault lift remains sealed. Hollow Meridian zones: Ember Vault, Verdigris Sump, Crownshaft. Enemy threat is explicit level or class.
