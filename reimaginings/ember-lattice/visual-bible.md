# Original visual bible — clean ember-line action

## Line, shape, and rendering

Clean tapered charcoal contour with purposeful weight increase on contact edges; minimal interior lines; simplified mature anime/manhwa facial construction; expressive eyes, brows, mouths, hands, and posture. Two-step cel shading with a restrained soft transition only on skin. Matte, clean material shapes. No painterly brush texture, crosshatching, grunge, paper grain, glossy 3D, or soft-focus fantasy finish.

## Palette and values

Charcoal `#17191f`, ivory `#f2eee5`, muted dungeon teal `#284c50`, iron green `#536864`, brass `#b78a45`, ember orange `#ff6a32`. Ember is the sole luminous accent. Dialogue balloons are ivory/charcoal; Ledger UI is near-black with brass rules, cut-corner geometry, and ember state ticks—never blue holographic glass.

## Camera and motion grammar

Ordinary panels: close/medium crop, one face/gesture/object, simple graded or silhouette background. Action: establish screen direction, exaggerate foreshortening only along the attack vector, show grip and footing, reserve debris and speed wedges for contact. Every impact is followed by changed posture, injury, environment, HP, or inventory. Vertical-scale panels use a centered depth shaft plus a small readable human silhouette.

## Density plan

Pilot: 11 low, 3 moderate, 2 high (68.75 / 18.75 / 12.5). P01 and P15 are high and never adjacent. Quiet/negative-space beats: P03, P12, P16. Low-detail means one dominant subject, ≤2 background elements, no texture field, and ≤1 effect family.

## Prompt exclusions

No generated text, letters, numbers, symbols pretending to be words, balloons, captions, cards, rectangles, watermarks, signatures, logos, named titles, named artists, children, sexualization, gore, painterly fantasy, game splash art, multiple moments, or decorative UI.
