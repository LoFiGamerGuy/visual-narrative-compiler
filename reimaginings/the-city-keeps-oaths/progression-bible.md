# Covenant Lattice progression bible

## Rule

A usable covenant requires a voluntary witness, a concrete cost, and observable completion. Oathglass records—not grants—the resulting capability. A person cannot fabricate a chord, own another person's vow, or keep the benefit after violating its terms. Vague, coerced, or unbounded promises become unstable and dangerous.

## Earned chords

1. **Witness line (CH01):** Sola stays until the last resident crosses. A thin gold line circles her copper bracer.
2. **Listen (CH02):** she accepts the pain of hearing unfinished public obligations. The bracer opens into a cyan tuning arc; she can isolate truthful oath pulses.
3. **Hold (CH03):** residents renew specific duties and Sola stakes her office on truthful repair. A broad cyan-gold forearm chord anchors one defined place. It burns out in CH06 because she tries to hold beyond her injured capacity.
4. **Span (CH05):** Sola stops owning the solution and aligns independently held promises. Parallel light bands connect witnesses without merging ownership.
5. **Sever (CH08):** she relinquishes the genuinely kept identity vow that made her a pathwright. Her copper bracer breaks; a clean white line across her palm can release coercive or stolen bindings without destroying truthful cores.
6. **Chorus (CH09):** she coordinates bounded promises while routing each line back to its witness. A translucent cyan-gold mantle appears over her shortened field coat.

## Costs and limits

- Listen causes fatigue and overload.
- Hold is local, singular, and vulnerable to overreach.
- Span requires willing separate owners and clear terms.
- Sever always surrenders a real kept identity or obligation; it cannot be spammed without self-erasure.
- Chorus coordinates but does not command. Any witness can withdraw, reducing the structure.
- Conflicting or undefined covenants fracture black-gold. No numerical interface or floating system screen exists.

## Visible progression contract

The progression is shown through bracer geometry, injury persistence, coat changes, a broken seal, Tarin's badge and spear transformations, posture, and environmental response. It is never conveyed solely by exposition. Every gain follows a choice and leaves a cost.
