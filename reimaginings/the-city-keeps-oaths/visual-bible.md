# Clean cinematic visual bible

## Locked direction

Original clean cinematic fantasy-webtoon hybrid: crisp controlled dark-navy contours, limited internal linework, smooth cel shading with restrained painterly gradients, mature adult proportions, clean material shapes, generous negative space, and disciplined cinematic value separation.

Palette: midnight blue is dominant, pearl/blue-gray supports, and cyan-gold oathlight is the single accent family. Black-gold fracture appears only for corrupted covenants. Brightest light, strongest saturation, and sharpest edge belong to the narrative focal point.

## Camera and staging

- Establish geometry once with a detailed vertical-scale panel, then simplify follow-ups.
- Close faces, hands, tools, and posture carry decisions.
- One dominant focal subject per ordinary panel; no more than two competing actions.
- Action is staged as constraint → contact → choice → consequence.
- Screen direction persists inside each immutable sequence.
- Reveals receive empty air before and after; no two maximum-density panels are adjacent.

## Rendering density

Each 24-panel chapter is precommitted to 16 low-detail panels (66.7%), five moderate panels (20.8%), and three high-detail panels (12.5%). High panels occur at positions 1, 11, and 19. A low-detail or negative-space beat appears at least every four panels. Metrics are calibrated from the selected pilot; they flag deviations but do not replace visual inspection.

## Source and lettering boundary

Generated source art contains no text, letters, numbers, balloons, captions, rectangles, placards, logos, signatures, or watermarks. Quiet space is compositional, not a visible box. Local deterministic balloons and captions use normalized `[left, top, right, bottom]` safe zones. One lettering unit per panel is the default, and no unit may cover eyes, hands, tools, injury, progression marks, causal contact, or creature anatomy.

## Explicit exclusions

No woodcut marks, dry-brush noise, risograph offsets, paper grain, hatching, crosshatching, grunge, ornamental machinery, equal detail everywhere, muddy contrast, tiny figures lost in texture, stacked competing effects, generic glossy 3D, named-artist imitation, or title-specific designs.

## Character continuity

Sola: warm brown skin; dark auburn segmented low braid; one silver forelock; ochre scarf. Long navy coat until its left sleeve is cut in CH04; short coat and ivory brace from CH06; copper bracer broken in CH08; Chorus mantle from CH09. Left forearm injury never vanishes.

Tarin: deep umber skin; short tight curls with silver temples; pearl-gray coat and teal layers. Badge cracks CH02, is surrendered CH03, broken CH07, then melted into a short two-tine chorus fork CH09. Ivory spear bends CH05 and shatters CH07.

Kesin: shaved head, round amber spectacles, plum-gray layers, gloves and satchel. Serac: white-streaked swept hair, ivory magistrate coat, mirrored gauntlet; borrowed mantle appears only CH10 and is removed before resolution.
