# Returning to the anchors without losing the gains

The original images supply drawing treatment, character appeal, material language and world atmosphere. A later action frame supplies evidence about an action experiment; it does not silently replace the original as the style reference. Every new scene in this round therefore records its original anchor directly. The three continuing encounters also use a dedicated new subject sheet and a separate editable composition control.

## The implemented sequence

1. Describe the original medium in visible terms: line weight, face modeling, painted or hatched masses, palette, edge treatment and environmental hierarchy. Preserve each direction's differences. Do not substitute a generic quality adjective for a medium description.
2. Establish a new subject sheet with a recognizable face, costume silhouette, functional weapon and world scale. Inspect every view: generated turnarounds can reverse equipment and add unrequested weapons. Name the authoritative view and exclude unreliable inserts from interpretation.
3. Draw an editable layout for every beat. Vary actual camera distance and body scale. Record weapon ownership, support contacts, force endpoints, current damage and the opponent's response. A layout is a reference, not a guarantee that the final image obeys it.
4. Feed only the current visible state into that beat. Exclude future wounds and later outcomes. Keep intended rule and actual visible evidence separate in the review.
5. Inspect native art before selecting it. Trace shoulder to elbow to wrist to implement; trace the power source to its target or anchor; check feet against the supporting surface. For impact, look for deformation, displacement, loss of balance or changed support, not just an effect mark.
6. Spend structural repairs on the errors that break the action. Preserve the original attempt. One repair may improve a mechanism while leaving a hand swap; record the remaining fault and never call that full compliance.
7. Finish busy surfaces using the exact native source as the sole visual reference. Protect face, pose, contact, current damage, cast shadows and medium. Quiet the ground first. Keep intentional hatching or brushwork when it carries the drawing treatment. Compare the finish with its source before changing the display default.
8. Read the uncaptioned sequence at phone size. Captions can explain intent but cannot prove a rule is visible. Review the full rhythm as well as each still. Store technical gallery checks separately from art judgment and owner choices.

## What this round exposed

The subject sheets keep faces and costumes recognizable but do not reliably lock handedness. A circle or crescent identifies equipment better than verbal left/right alone, yet the decisive Soft Thunder punch still swapped arms and marks. Nightglass's tether migrated from sword pommel to an extra hip ring. A repaired frame removed a duplicate sword and restored the line while retaining the wrong attacking arm. These are observed limitations, not reasons to assert that a more detailed prompt solved anatomy.

The wide-shot controls also transferred imperfectly: the final generation often enlarged the characters and reduced the intended difference between an establishing view and a close encounter. Future wide-scene tests should judge subject height against frame height before using that image to establish scale. Strong scenery and a large monster are not by themselves evidence of an extreme-wide camera.

Black Petal's severed root and collapsed flower provide visible consequence. Its earlier shield closure and root-plant timing are less clear; the creature's inability to turn remains partly caption-dependent. Soft Thunder's mouth blast, airborne reaction and subsequent grounded recovery communicate more directly. This is useful evidence about staging a readable rule, independent of which style the owner likes most.

The Cathedral Engine texture finish is a necessary counterexample: it calms the image but removes too much engraving into smooth sepia shading. The primary remains the display default, with the finish available as an alternative. Surface quietness must not erase the medium that made a direction appealing.

## Reuse

Use the saved texture guide in `docs/texture-refinement-kit/GUIDE.md`, then customize the protected features and distracting surfaces for the actual source. Exact prompts, source-only edit references and every native result are retained under `production/anchor-return/`. Do not add other style references to a finishing call. Do not silently use a failed finish as the reference for the next round.

No result in this round is automatically cast, canon or an owner preference. Display selection is a review convenience; the gallery's owner choices remain blank and prior exported answers remain separate.
