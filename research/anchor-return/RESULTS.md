Open [the offline gallery](../../docs/anchor-return/index.html). Start with **Read an encounter** and turn captions off once: Nightglass, Black Petal and Soft Thunder each have eight beats. The other six drawing directions have one new scene each. Original anchors, new subject sheets and editable layouts are available separately; none is automatically cast or canon.

This round restores the tailored faces, inhabited vertical city and navy/cyan atmosphere of Nightglass; Black Petal's elegant portrait treatment and translucent floral horror; and Soft Thunder's rounded painting and expressive humor. The surface finishing makes the floors, vegetation and repeated small marks much quieter while retaining the actual actions. ## What changed in the pipeline

Every primary scene uses its original drawing anchor directly. Three new cast/equipment sheets establish the recurring subjects. Twenty-four editable layouts specify camera, contacts, weapon ownership and current damage before generation. Prompts exclude future injuries. Structural repairs and source-only texture finishing are separate, with exact sources and every attempt retained. Native and phone reviews compare what the images show with what the plan intended. The reusable procedure and observed limitations are saved in [PIPELINE.md](PIPELINE.md) and the root AGENTS instructions.

The original Nightglass remains a useful leading benchmark because its face, costume, creature and city support one clear invitation into a larger world. A stronger combat moment does not automatically improve that entire balance. This round recovers much of that integrated appeal; the next judgment is whether the new actions make you care about these particular characters, rather than simply looking dramatic.
