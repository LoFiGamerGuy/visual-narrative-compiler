# Anchor Return — frozen independent rubric

Frozen before viewing new sheets or compositions. Production plan will receive a separate binding; this rubric stays unchanged. No numerical beauty ranking, owner approval or comfort vote is inferred.

Review criteria:

- **anchor_fidelity:** Compare actual line, edge, facial construction, shading, material and space against the original anchor. Palette, costume and style labels alone do not demonstrate transfer. New adult identities are allowed; copying an anchor person is not the fidelity test.

- **appeal_and_focus:** Read expressions, gesture, silhouette and composition for charisma, menace, warmth and felt impact. Grip correctness alone does not establish appeal. Keep distinctive powers and target reactions rather than substituting a repeated airborne pose.

- **causality_and_sequence:** Track preparation, commitment, contact, counter, reversal, consequence and recovery through actual eight-beat images. Identify source, path, target and resulting body/environment change. Effects are not proof of contact. Preserve geography, screen direction and available exits; captions cannot supply missing construction.

- **identity_and_state:** Trace anatomical hands to shoulders, continuous weapon construction, actor/creature counts, grip and weight support. Follow exact injury side, gear damage and persistent material marks from first appearance to aftermath. A compact sheet is a hypothesis until actual pixels establish it; sheet defects must not become invisible defaults.

- **ground_and_material:** Judge floor/water/foliage competition at native and actual phone size. Quiet microtexture while retaining medium-defining brush or hatch marks, material distinction, contact shadows, depth and scale. A dry floor becoming polished or reflective is a material change even if smoother.

- **source_only_finish:** Compare exact source and F at matched size for actual relief and regressions in face, silhouette, anatomy, contacts, force accents, damage and world depth. A smoother image that deletes a needed chip or impact accent is partial or rejected; do not repair anatomy or restage the shot under finishing.

- **phone_pacing:** Inspect actual complete uncaptioned phone sequences, not only isolated native art or screenshot receipts. Assess framing variation, transitions, readable small figures and fatigue from repeated patterns. Distinguish AI-observed reading risks from uncollected owner comfort feedback.

Original-anchor drawing systems, recovered from all nine actual native files:

- **01:** Economical tapered ink and restrained angular face; cool luminous depth behind a clean, long fashion silhouette. Dense city can support scale without copying every floor reflection. Avoid treating armor density or grimacing as the style.

- **02:** Warm outlined painting, angular color/light planes, expressive thick brows and lively asymmetry; saturated cloth and pale masonry. Not flat vector art and not a glossy Nightglass recolor.

- **05:** Matte opaque brush shapes, subdued earthy paint, selective scratchy edges, substantial face/body and warm mechanical weight. Do not polish every surface into smooth cel-shaded plastic.

- **06:** Coarse black/white brush masses, broken dry-brush edges, heavy adult facial planes and a single pink accent. Preserve material and ink character; control busy reflected water separately.

- **13:** Energetic nib contours, directional hatching, muscular gesture, white negative space and sparse red. Its finer line-based modeling differs from06’s coarse brush-mass construction.

- **15:** Engraved sepia linework follows curvature and models volumes; dark/light hatch density separates forms. Fewer well-placed hatch bands can calm it, but removing the engraving defeats the treatment.

- **17:** Thin fairly even contour, broad flat warm color, simplified appealing eyes/nose and spacious architecture. Preserve restrained line economy; do not add glossy dimensional armor rendering.

- **18:** Rounded volumes, oversized expressive eyes/mouth, bold eyebrows, playful scale and painted color masses. Muscular adults can remain broad and elastic; avoid shrinking everyone into the same narrow heroic face.

- **19:** Soft near-real facial gradients and elegant slender silhouette; translucent veined petals, luminous hollow center and restrained monochrome with dark red ground. Opaque ivory armor and hard cel face planes are a substantive drift.

Preflight caveats:

- Original anchors themselves contain busy floors and backgrounds; recover their drawing systems without reproducing every distracting mark.

- Supplemental sheets can inadvertently homogenize faces, bodies, creature joints or weapon styles across routes. Review their actual construction before propagation.

- Editable contact layouts and state cards establish intended relationships, not final-pixel compliance. Distinguish each shot’s current state from future damage to avoid early contamination.

- An eight-beat sequence requires sustained state transitions, varied reversals and a visible cost/recovery; eight attractive isolated illustrations are insufficient.

- Recent EN drift is a hypothesis to test: NG heavier armored sameness, BP harder opaque creature/face construction, IO smoothing toward cel metal, ST narrowing away from rounded original shape language.

- Keep factual disagreements, source hashes and exposure disclosures. Never turn global praise into per-image ratings or canon.
