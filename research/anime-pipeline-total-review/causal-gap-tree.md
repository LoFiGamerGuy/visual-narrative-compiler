# Causal gap tree

The leading proven cause is wrong event art admitted by self-confirming acceptance checks. Generator capability and premise preference remain important, but are less resolved. A change of model or title would leave the proven error untouched if the same selector and validator survived.

## C1 — Story events change or happen before their cause

**Severity/frequency:** critical; 5 high-information mapping cases in 52 (P033 is contextual pressure mismatch; the others provide more explicit event/cast/body-state evidence); not an exhaustive failure rate. **Earliest causal stage:** selection and benchmark-to-chapter mapping.

Observed mechanism: 24 existing benchmark sources were assigned to revised chapter beats; some exact original requests contradict cast, body state or timing.

Competing explanation: Generator disobeyed a correct story prompt. Not primary for these five: original source requests already describe the incompatible event. Other newly generated failures remain possible.

Concrete remedy: Storyboard-led source binding, independent semantic rejection and replacement of incompatible plates. Proof: Neutral14 panel source-to-beat review; zero unexplained actor/state/time/contact contradictions.

Evidence: `P012`, `P017`, `P029`, `P033`, `P037`, `evidence/technical-measurements.json`; see [technical mapping](technical-pipeline-audit.md), [visible audit](visual-sequential-audit.md) and [measurement definitions](evidence/structural-measurements.json).

## C2 — Excellent PASS scores coexist with visibly broken sequences

**Severity/frequency:** critical; 52/52 hybrid rows share the same quality vector. **Earliest causal stage:** validation and acceptance authority.

Observed mechanism: Workflow score presets, round-robin scenario tags and protected rectangles chosen around lettering replace independent observations.

Competing explanation: Only rubric weights are miscalibrated. Changing weights cannot rescue non-observational inputs.

Concrete remedy: Remove computed quality presets from decisions; independently freeze annotations and review actual phone sequence. Proof: Historical false-pass cases rejected despite valid hashes/JSON; two blind human reviews plus owner preference.

Evidence: `integrate.py criteria_scores`, `editorial.py uniform scores`, `validation-goodhart-audit.md`; see [technical mapping](technical-pipeline-audit.md), [visible audit](visual-sequential-audit.md) and [measurement definitions](evidence/structural-measurements.json).

## C3 — Large speech is readable in isolation but spills out of balloons or hides meaning

**Severity/frequency:** high; Confirmed P004/P005/P012/P033/P034 and matched sample; population count not established. **Earliest causal stage:** lettering layout and source composition.

Observed mechanism: Type grew while curved container geometry and real-image protected regions were not jointly reviewed; authored boxes are not actual quiet space.

Competing explanation: Font size alone is too small. Current actual362px minimum12.30px is above UI floor; visual containment/attribution still fails. Prior minimum was smaller.

Concrete remedy: Fit glyphs to real balloon interiors, real mouth targets and fixed image annotations; split/rewrite before shrinking. Proof: Zero visible glyph/body overflow or essential-feature occlusion at390; +30/+50percent copy stress with no smaller type.

Evidence: `lettering-ui-mobile-audit.md`, `evidence/structural-measurements.json`; see [technical mapping](technical-pipeline-audit.md), [visible audit](visual-sequential-audit.md) and [measurement definitions](evidence/structural-measurements.json).

## C4 — Many panels feel like similarly finished dark fantasy illustrations

**Severity/frequency:** high; 35/52 declared low density but52 identical1024x1536 sources; visual sampling shows texture across quiet/action beats. **Earliest causal stage:** storyboard, composition and rendering brief.

Observed mechanism: Full-frame asset production and repeated surface/lighting hierarchy give each beat comparable visual weight; median filtering cannot redesign shapes or acting.

Competing explanation: All dense/painterly art is inferior. Rejected: licensed later TOG and strong repository stills demonstrate useful detailed art. Density must serve attention and contrast.

Concrete remedy: Variable shot footprint, simpler backgrounds in emotional/action beats, explicit color script and selective detail; test cleaner separable route against well-directed current renderer. Proof: Blind review recognizes intended attention order and quiet/spectacle contrast; no proxy-only art threshold.

Evidence: `visual-sequential-audit.md`, `evidence/deterministic-measurements.json`, `evidence/structural-measurements.json`; see [technical mapping](technical-pipeline-audit.md), [visible audit](visual-sequential-audit.md) and [measurement definitions](evidence/structural-measurements.json).

## C5 — Coherent systems do not reliably create attachment or urgent action

**Severity/frequency:** medium-high; Recurring in sampled early/late episodes; audience preference hypothesis, not survey result. **Earliest causal stage:** episode editorial and performance.

Observed mechanism: Theme and numerical reconciliation are clearer than personal stakes, expressive range and reward felt through action. Serious technical dialogue often replaces chemistry.

Competing explanation: Ember premise intrinsically lacks appeal. Unproven; owner strongly liked pilot and fault-reading/shared-cost engine supports original tactics.

Concrete remedy: Keep premise provisional; stage one relationship pressure and one capability change visibly, with state exposition only at decision/consequence. Proof: After route isolation, an Ember micro-opening is preferred/understood by owner and independent readers; story reset only after controlled rejection.

Evidence: `story-progression-action-audit.md`, `owner-feedback-ledger.md`; see [technical mapping](technical-pipeline-audit.md), [visible audit](visual-sequential-audit.md) and [measurement definitions](evidence/structural-measurements.json).

## C6 — Large engineering surface produces limited visible quality returns

**Severity/frequency:** medium; 603 legacy modules/76613 physical lines/317 validate modules in scoped count. **Earliest causal stage:** production architecture and incentives.

Hypothesis, not observed displacement: copied machinery and record milestones may displace sequence revision. Actual time allocation and causal direction were not measured; difficult art may instead have prompted more machinery.

Competing explanation: All infrastructure is wasted. Rejected: hashes, preservation, nullable provenance, state ledgers, editable SVG and failure injection solve real problems.

Concrete remedy: Retain small proven core; consolidate only when pilot demands it, allocate explicit editorial correction time. Proof: Accepted-sequence quality and human correction hours, not module/report count, control scale.

Evidence: `technical-pipeline-audit.md`; see [technical mapping](technical-pipeline-audit.md), [visible audit](visual-sequential-audit.md) and [measurement definitions](evidence/structural-measurements.json).

## Branches that remain unresolved

Reference conditioning may constrain poses and surface finish, but this archive does not isolate that ceiling from mismatched briefs and source selection. Human direction may improve action and finishing, but there is no measured human-first bakeoff here. Character magnetism may be a taste/acting problem rather than a premise problem. Blinded sequence tests—not new synthetic scores—must decide.
