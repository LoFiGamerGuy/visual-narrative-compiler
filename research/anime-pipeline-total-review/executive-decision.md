## Five findings that change the decision

1. **The images often depict the wrong event because they were selected for the wrong beat.** The52-panel chapter reuses24 benchmark sources and adds28 new images. P012’s original source request explicitly includes four adults; P037’s source requests a collapsed monster before the chapter’s finishing blow. P017/P029/P033 also substitute incompatible moments. All52 hashes are distinct and match. Novelty is not narrative fidelity. [Exact mapping](technical-pipeline-audit.md).
2. **The quality scores do not constitute independent evaluation.** Workflow presets and uniform editorial assignments produce the same89.095 mean, median and weakest-panel result. Scenario coverage cycles by index; protected rectangles are chosen around lettering without locating the actual faces/hands. These checks explain why internal PASS did not predict the owner’s response. [Validation audit](validation-goodhart-audit.md).
3. **The strongest stills exceed the sustained sequence.** Useful faces, creature scale and contacts coexist with unexplained grip resets, premature aftermath and repeated full-height visual weight. A dark or detailed image is not inherently bad; several earlier and licensed samples contradict that shortcut. [Visual audit](visual-sequential-audit.md).
4. **Lettering improved and still fails visibly.** At an equal362px art width, minimum SVG type rises9.40→12.30px, copy349→308 word tokens and authored rectangle area falls. Yet P005/P033 cross balloon contours, P004/P051 cover facial performance, and P034 exceeds its UI box. A nominal type/collision pass cannot rescue that reading. [Matched audit](lettering-ui-mobile-audit.md).
5. **Ember has assets worth testing, not a proven serial.** The owner strongly liked Candidate B; structural perception, finite costs, allies and gear have a credible original engine. Serious explanatory dialogue and schematic cast/gear catalogs have not yet established magnetic characters or addictive propulsion. [Story audit](story-progression-action-audit.md), [owner record](owner-feedback-ledger.md).

## What to retain and stop

Retain usable original character/environment direction; the fault-reading/shared-cost premise; exact state and inventory ledgers; immutable source revisions and real hash checks; nullable provenance; failure preservation; editable SVG/text and useful local readers. Recover earlier strengths—CH05’s variable cadence, Borrowed’s cast distinction and City/pilot emotional warmth—without inheriting their defects.

Stop calling preset scores editorial evidence, filling a beat with a thematically related but wrong image, declaring protected zones from the balloon’s position, filtering whole plates as a substitute for shape/acting correction, and expanding catalogs or chapters before complete-reader preference is established. Do not spend another cycle refactoring the entire legacy framework before making a better short sequence.
