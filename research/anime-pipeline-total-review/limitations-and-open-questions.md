## Metric boundaries

SHA256 measures byte identity, not correct events, ownership or regeneration. dHash candidate flags do not detect all repeated poses or compositions; zero flags is not proof of none. Pixel density/darkness/noise depend on the declared resize/threshold and do not score beauty. Camera/scenario/density tags conflict with actual imagery and cannot be treated as an observed shot histogram. Balloon areas are authored rectangles, not glyph pixels; visible containment/collision is independently reviewed. No complete corpus OCR, manually coded shot/pose/error census, real localization, throttled-device performance or attributable human labor study was performed. These fields remain null or scoped observations in [measurements](evidence/structural-measurements.json).

The root metadata scan covers all files; content hashing is scoped to eligible files≤32MiB outside excluded vendor/model/dataset/reference directories. Other files are compared by size, modification/change timestamps and mode, not cryptographic content digest. This detects ordinary overwrite/change but is not a hostile filesystem-forensics guarantee. Access timestamps and shared mutable Git object/log/fetch administration are excluded. Prior heads/status/index/control files and protected refs are checked explicitly.
