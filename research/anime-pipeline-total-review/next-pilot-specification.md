## Acceptance, kill and next authority

### Hard gates

- 14 exact beats; no unexplained extra/missing principal actor, state reset, wrong event order, omitted required contact or unexplained prop transfer
- All required text visible inside intended balloon/UI shape; zero face/contact occlusion; correct tail attribution
- Actual dialogue minimum14CSSpx, UI minimum12CSSpx at390 viewport; these are project floors, not universal standards
- Zero missing source assets, broken required links or horizontal overflow; transcript and keyboard navigation
- One-to-one provenance and attempt log; candidates remain unaccepted/review-pending/commercially-uncleared until explicit review

### Human gates

- Two independent human readers each reconstruct >=6 of7 tested action functions (anticipation, attack, counter, reversal, new-skill application, impact, aftermath), all three charge states and the earned unlock without explanatory script; required contact/state hard gates still all pass.
- Each human rates sequence and lettering categories >=4/5, art and reader impact >=3.5/5, with every required core dimension assessed and >=3; no selective omission. See required_core_dimensions and permitted_not_assessed.
- For absolute eligibility, owner finds the complete route compelling enough to continue; two independent humans pass the absolute rubric/comprehension gates. A may qualify or win. For replacing A with B/C, owner and both independent humans explicitly prefer the replacement complete reader.
- For replacing renderer, both human reviewers show >=0.75/5 improvement in sequence or art category, no >=0.5 regression in the other and zero hard defects; if within margin call tie and keep cheaper route
- Record all labor; modular route must fit agreed34–74h single-route planning envelope or approved replacement ceiling, never treat agent runtime as human time

### Kill gates

- Any hard defect remains after one targeted retry plus one bounded correction round
- Do not promote B/C without clear preference and improvement sufficient to justify correction burden. An absolutely eligible A survives a tie; any route failing absolute gates stops.
- Missing skilled human capacity or destination prohibits the chosen production method

### Blinding and owner review

Randomized route labels/order, equal390 andfull-size surfaces, same copy/assets/scene; reviewers submit independent dimension scores before discussion. Preserve qualitative reasons, panel flags and hours. Owner can review unblinded after preference lock.

- Which complete version feels most compelling to continue, and which exact moment earns that preference?
- Where did you lose track of position, force, object ownership or emotional intent?
- Which adult character would you want to see again, and why?
- Is the remaining objection mainly story desire, character appeal, art finish or reading friction?

## Required scoring and comprehension key

Every dimension in the machine plan’s `required_core_dimensions` is mandatory: all reader-impact, sequence, art and genre dimensions, plus eight lettering/mobile dimensions. None may be omitted because it performs badly. Actual localization and ten production-fitness dimensions are the only permitted unscored categories, for the explicit evidence limits recorded there. Medium is not a quality criterion: judge attention, coherent shapes and motivated detail.

The machine plan freezes the seven-function answer key. A 6/7 score never excuses a critical actor/contact/order/prop/state or earned-skill misconception. Human reviewers retell without seeing that key; a coordinator grades after their answers lock. These prospective clarifications leave historical scores unchanged.
