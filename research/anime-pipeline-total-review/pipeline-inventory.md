The machine-readable [inventory](pipeline-inventory.json) resolves every requested field for every family, including unknown provider metadata, acceptance limits, inputs, selection, repair, inheritance and an eight-stage map. [Technical measurements](evidence/technical-measurements.json) independently verifies source and code claims. Availability is not a claim that the archivist visually reviewed every artifact. Visual findings and scored judgments belong in the independent visual audit.

**Rights scope:** legacy likeness/model cautions are not evidence those inputs fed every later original route. Route-specific input descriptions govern; no cross-route private-reference use is inferred.
