# Strategy decision matrix

**Choose B: replace source selection and sequence preproduction, keep Ember provisional, and let a fair pilot decide how much of the renderer survives.** C is the fallback if a correctly directed current/modular route still cannot deliver and skilled human capacity is available. If A wins the matched test, retain it: the audit is not a mandate to rebuild for its own sake. D needs evidence that story preference—not the production failure—remains the blocker.

These are ordinal decisions with explicit assumptions, not predicted success percentages. Human-time ranges are unrun planning estimates from [economics](economics-rights-risk.md), not quotes or observed industry averages.

## A — Refine latest hybrid

**Retain:** Ember canon, current full-frame renderer, most compatible plates, SVG/state/hashes.

**Replace:** Wrong-beat plates, defective lettering, acceptance logic; selective grading/pacing.

**Discard:** Preset superiority claims and unsupported source bindings.

**Quality ceiling:** Useful visible gains plausible; sustained premium sequence ceiling unproven.

**Owner fit:** Moderate, supported by pilot enthusiasm but weakened by repeated dissatisfaction.

**Evidence:** Owner CandidateB approval; P012/P017/P037 direct repairable mismatches; Matched typography improvement.

**Unknowns:** How many existing plates need replacement; Whether targeted repair fixes acting cheaply.

**Pilot:** Best current renderer on same frozen14-beat sequence; no reuse unless exact beat is met.

**Roles:** Editor/action reviewer, art selector/repairer, letterer, production engineer.

**Tooling:** Keep current generator; add actual annotation and rendered-glyph review.

**Iteration:** One selection/repair cycle after storyboard review.

**Human hours estimate:** 24–52 hours (estimate).

**Spend:** $0 incremental during this audit; future external labor/cloud unpriced and unauthorized.

**Risks:** Retained fused plates limit edits; temptation to repeat late-stage cosmetic fixes.

**Rights:** Existing source/model/reference clearance remains unresolved.

**Reversibility:** High: replace selected revisions without changing canon.

**Leading indicators:** Zero wrong-beat bindings; readable exchange; actual owner preference.

**Kill:** One allowed repair cannot resolve essential action/state/lettering failures, or sequence remains unpreferred.

**Scale:** All shared pilot gates pass and actual correction hours fit ceiling.

**Opportunity cost:** Could consume another cycle preserving unsuitable imagery.


## B — Modular replacement with storyboard-led source selection

**Retain:** Provisional Ember world/cast/progression, valid IDs/hashes/state/nullable records, editable SVG and readers.

**Replace:** Late mapping of finished plates with approved thumbnails and source-to-beat contracts; comparison route adds separable character/background/line/effect finishing.

**Discard:** Requirement to reuse current imagery; synthetic quality/protection/scenario evidence.

**Quality ceiling:** Highest useful next uncertainty reduction; premium capability plausible but unproven.

**Owner fit:** Best current decision fit: respects approved premise while addressing demonstrated stage failures.

**Evidence:** Exact incompatible source prompts for5 cases; Current architecture permits new raster/layer sources; Professional revision-before-finish evidence.

**Unknowns:** Renderer fidelity under correct layouts; Benefit/cost of separable clean shapes and manual corrections; Human skill availability.

**Pilot:** Two routes on frozen14 beats: best-current directed baseline versus materially different controlled-layer route; optional human-first third only if staffed.

**Roles:** Story/action editor, storyboard/character artist, finishing artist or skilled equivalent, letterer, two independent human readers.

**Tooling:** Editable thumbnails/model sheets; layout-conditioned asset/layer intake; semantic acceptance before rendering/lettering.

**Iteration:** Freeze → rough correction → one candidate → at most one hard-failure retry → bounded finishing → blind sequence review.

**Human hours estimate:** 34–74 hours (estimate).

**Spend:** $0 audit; if skilled human time is unavailable, stop at plan/rough proof; no purchase implied.

**Risks:** Can become an expensive hidden C rebuild; cleaner finish alone may not appeal.

**Rights:** Keep only assets with documented permitted use; no current route declared commercially cleared.

**Reversibility:** High: current sources and story remain intact, routes share manifests.

**Leading indicators:** Correct event art, increased attention hierarchy, easier specific edits, preferred complete reader.

**Kill:** No decisive preference or unacceptable correction burden after frozen attempts; no extra beauty loop.

**Scale:** Shared semantic/phone gates, independent+owner win, timed follow-on proof.

**Opportunity cost:** Setup time exceeds A, but tests selection and editability instead of polishing wrong plates.


## C — Human-directed hybrid rebuild

**Retain:** Useful canon and narrow engineering core only as assets merit.

**Replace:** Creative production spine with conventional thumbnails/model sheets/layout/line/color/finishing and accountable human approval.

**Discard:** Automation as default owner of artistic acceptance; fused full-frame generation where it obstructs edits.

**Quality ceiling:** Potentially highest with capable artists and editorial direction; not demonstrated here.

**Owner fit:** Promising long-term fit if staffed; uncertain practical availability.

**Evidence:** Industry workflow sources; Current correction/acting/mapping failures; No direct fair route test.

**Unknowns:** Actual artist performance/hours/cost; Whether AI adds any verified advantage; Sustainable staffing.

**Pilot:** Same14 beats with human-first layouts and corrections; generation only for isolated advantageous tasks.

**Roles:** Experienced sequential artist/action director, color/finish, letterer/editor, reviewers.

**Tooling:** Conventional layered art tools; simple3D if useful; generation optional.

**Iteration:** Rough approval then line/acting revision before rendering; one scoped finishing round.

**Human hours estimate:** 46–102 hours (estimate).

**Spend:** Unpriced; $0 means existing authorized human capacity only, no hiring/payment authorized.

**Risks:** Skill/time dependency; labor cost and availability; no guarantee new management creates great art.

**Rights:** Human creation clarifies process but still requires font/asset/source licenses.

**Reversibility:** Medium: preserve masters and source records, avoid training/buildout until proof.

**Leading indicators:** Specific editorial correction succeeds without collateral redraw; two-reader comprehension and owner appeal.

**Kill:** No skilled capacity or costs exceed agreed future budget; pilot does not beat cheaper route.

**Scale:** Same quality gates plus measured capacity and buffer on two later episodes.

**Opportunity cost:** Highest setup/labor burden among retained-story approaches.


## D — Greenfield story and visual restart

**Retain:** Only proven engineering lessons and safety/provenance practices.

**Replace:** Premise/cast/art direction/production topology via new original pilot.

**Discard:** Obligation to preserve Ember canon or source art.

**Quality ceiling:** Unbounded in principle, no evidence of a better realized ceiling.

**Owner fit:** Unknown; current dissatisfaction does not establish rejection of premise.

**Evidence:** Current prompt permits restart; Earlier repeated new stories did not remove production problems; Explicit positive Ember direction approval.

**Unknowns:** New premise preference; New creative team/visual identity; Same technical failure recurrence.

**Pilot:** Only after route-controlled evidence isolates story rejection: compare two short original premise hooks using same proven craft route.

**Roles:** Development editor, writer, concept/sequence artist, all C production responsibilities.

**Tooling:** No speculative new stack before approved roughs.

**Iteration:** 8–20h estimated premise/cast exploration in addition to chosen route pilot.

**Human hours estimate:** 54–122 hours (estimate).

**Spend:** Unpriced and no paid authority; estimate combines C46–102 plus8–20 exploration.

**Risks:** Restarts diagnosis; loses approved strengths and repeats bottleneck under new title.

**Rights:** Fresh characters reduce carry-over ambiguity, not automatic clearance of tools/assets.

**Reversibility:** Medium-low creatively; preserve all prior work so technical rollback remains possible.

**Leading indicators:** Owner prefers new premise when art route is held constant.

**Kill:** New title inherits same visual mismatch or lacks stronger hook.

**Scale:** New premise preference plus all same route/capacity/rights gates.

**Opportunity cost:** Largest loss of useful canon and additional concept cycle before pipeline proof.

## Guard against a false choice

Replacing five wrongly bound images is an A-sized repair; it does not require new models. B becomes justified when the same error is prevented at thumbnail/source-selection stage and when the next test can change composition without preserving arbitrary finished plates. C is not a free upgrade: an unstaffed human-directed diagram produces no art. D must not be selected merely because the existing package feels disappointing. Run the shared [pilot](next-pilot-specification.md) and stop if no route clears its real reader gates.
