# Visual and sequential audit

**The strongest still images are better than the sequences they assemble into.** This is not a finding that every image is bad. The archive contains appealing mature faces, readable monster silhouettes, useful environment scale and some convincing contacts. It also contains wrong event art, repeated visual weight and lettering that covers the information a reader needs. Those are reasons a polished panel collection can remain unlike compelling action comics.

This audit uses direct full-size images and actual continuous390×844 browser reading, not contact sheets alone. [Independent B](evidence/visual-review-b.md) records more than150 sampled captures and exact boundaries; [A records](evidence/subjective-scores-a.json) locked before reading B; [reconciled scores](pipeline-scorecards.md) preserve disagreements. Neither AI pass substitutes for owner or human professional review. [Reader QA](evidence/reader-browser-mobile-qa.json), with the [cold-load editorial recheck](evidence/reader-browser-mobile-editorial-recheck.json), records final mobile geometry; [source measurements](evidence/deterministic-measurements.json) provide defined proxies.

## Five decisive source-to-beat failures

P012 introduces two extra party members where the revised opening requires two leads and boss geography. P017 places `THOOM` over a visibly separated stand-off instead of jaw/shield contact. P029 supplies a calm upright profile for the hanging injury beat. P033 places both people seated at rest while the script needs a dangerous split-distance exchange. P037 shows the monster already down before the later finishing chain.

These are not deductions from filenames. They were seen in the reader, then traced to exact source requests: four-person benchmark `bm06`, approach `bm12`, portrait `bm01`, seated exchange `bm03`, and collapsed-boss `bm24`. The source generator was often asked to create the very thing now incorrectly reused. [Exact causal mapping](technical-pipeline-audit.md) separates selection fault from model disobedience. Five examples are a **confirmed minimum**, not a randomly sampled5/52 error-rate estimate.

Independent B additionally finds a spear/jaw reset in P020–23 and an upright portrait under injury-binding copy at P049. P018→P019 is a readable local clamp chain and P026 does show a strike; those successes do not connect every surrounding state. ## Attention, pacing and finish

The final52 sources are all1024×1536. At true390px mobile viewport the editorial art width is362px, each source **543CSSpx** high; final page height32,639px, about38.67 viewports including review chrome. Most panel starts are610.72px apart, with a few longer gaps. Earlier premium panel pitch is590.22px throughout the measured reader. The editorial gaps change timing, but do not make an insert physically small or a reveal uniquely large. See [structural definitions](evidence/structural-measurements.json); metadata “low” is not a measured visual-density score.

Thirty-five of52 panels are declared low density. Full-size and phone images still repeatedly describe every coat tear, masonry crack, chain and joint. The problem is weak hierarchy across functions, not detail itself. Borrowed’s flat orange tools, Ember volume’s pale-background impact and licensed later Tower of God’s dense spectacle are counterexamples to a blanket sparse/cel rule. An effective color script changes the reader’s state—quiet, threat, impact, relief—rather than applying one global prestige-fantasy treatment.

Thumbnail pixel proxies find33/52 current hybrid frames with over60% pixels below grayscale48 under the declared256×256 procedure, compared with32/52 prior selected sources. That does **not** rank beauty or imply the extra dark frame worsened art. Twelve median blends alter high-frequency detail; direct P044/P039 comparisons leave the camera, event and major shapes essentially intact. Use filtering for a confirmed surface defect, not as a substitute for finishing/acting/layout revision.

## Stills versus competitive sequence quality

The appealing P029 profile and expressive source pilot P003 demonstrate assets worth retaining. Monster silhouettes and architectural scale can be strong. Neither the audit nor a pair of AI reviewers can certify professional market competitiveness or owner preference. The evidence supports a narrower conclusion: some stills are polished and useful, while the source-to-sequence and assembly process is insufficiently controlled to sustain them.

A professional art director would first ask which line/shape/value supports the beat, then correct expression and contact. An action director would reject an impact without contact and a restraint that silently releases. A reader would feel lost or detached before caring whether the ledger reconciles. More beautiful wrong-event images make those problems harder to notice internally, not less consequential.

## Sampling limits and access fidelity

Every named pipeline/mechanism family is inventoried; not every historical candidate or every page of every volume was seen. Each major finished-output family has high-information opening/dialogue/continuity/action/reveal/quiet/dense/ending samples where they exist; UI and boss chains remain unassessed when absent. Engineering controls and previz are not falsely treated as final comics. The exact visual matrix and inaccessible dimensions in B’s report are part of this audit, not fine print.

Initial volume screenshots repeated the header because the capture selected `figure` instead of `article.panel`; they were discarded for visual coverage and replaced. Initial desktop-at-phone-width captures leave15px for a scrollbar; final true mobile captures use390px content. A cold editorial pass measured85 pending images, not missing assets; the recheck scrolls/awaits all104 art/overlay images and reports zero pending or broken. Natural image dimensions alone still miss the pilot’s nested-SVG art visibility failure. Browser health and visual correctness are separate gates.
