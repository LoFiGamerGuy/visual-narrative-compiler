# Chapter 1 editorial evaluation

Source: completed 48-panel Chapter 1 at commit `23704e1`. Concurrent Chapter 4
work is excluded. Exact reading copy, intent, source paths and image hashes are
frozen in packet.json. Original deliveries remain unchanged.

## Questions

1. Does the loss of upper-route admission have a clear cause and lasting cost?
2. Does Aren's desire for stable work, a room and rest drive his choices?
3. Are rescue, unclipping, load limits and the later Hold upgrade understandable?
4. Can a reader follow the two different sealed objects, custody and receipts?
5. Does the ordinary delivery and soup payoff earn the provisional route?
6. Do actual images preserve handedness, contact, anchors and persistent damage?

## Procedure

Run a small pilot on two accessible credit-eligible Bedrock models with verified
regional prices. Start with independent script-only editorial and continuity
passes. The model receives no prior reviewers' conclusions. A visual pass uses
hash-verified images and exact reading copy, with a bounded group of consecutive
panels. No visual claim can be inferred from a script alone.

Each finding needs existing panel IDs, evidence, severity, confidence and a
specific possible fix. A model may return zero findings. It must not invent a
reader popularity score or human approval. Structural validity and valid panel
references are automatic checks, not proof that a criticism is correct.

Use synthetic counterfactual continuity controls to measure whether the reviewer
notices clear errors and avoids claiming the unmodified control has that error.
Keep expected answers outside the model input. Controls are not story changes.

The integrator adjudicates findings against the exact chapter. Accepted changes
become separately reviewable revisions, with a before/after table and remaining
art or lettering work made explicit. Model agreement is not human acceptance.

## Budget

Initial pilot at most $5; complete priority at most $40. The shared credit-sprint
runner records token counts, requests, responses and costs and blocks unsafe
replays. All outputs stay local. No publication or new image-generation spend
is included in this evaluation.
