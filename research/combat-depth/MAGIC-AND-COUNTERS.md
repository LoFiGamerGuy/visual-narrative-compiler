# Provisional combat vocabulary

These are testable ideas for this round, not selected canon. The gallery distinguishes intended rules from what each image actually communicates.

| Ability | Visible action | Cost or counter | Progression possibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Borrowed Horizon / Held Threshold | A saber links two supported visible edges for a short step. | One pair and one body; the committed exit can be predicted. The art must show the endpoints, not rely on a caption. | Carry momentum or choose a more difficult exit while keeping the destination vulnerable. |
| Downfall Writ | A planted Axis Lance redirects weight within a narrow strip; water and bodies follow the same vector. | The caster must remain within reach and outside the strip. Lift or dislodge the butt to end control. | Redirect a heavier burden or part of a structure at greater strain. |
| Quiet Harbor / Rebound | A braced crescent shield catches one pressure front and returns a focused pulse. | A flank strike breaks the stance and spills the held pressure. | Better timing, a narrower return, or sharing cover with allies; not unlimited absorption. |
| Deep Winter / Riverturn | A held broad sword meets flowing water to grow a low wedge or an escape route. | Break contact and the working edge stops growing. Existing ice need not vanish instantly. | Control load, reach or growth direction while remaining physically committed to the water. |
| Anchorbreaker | A physical kick dislodges the lance’s planted lower shaft. | Requires entering dangerous reach and committing balance. | A martial technique that wins through timing and knowledge of an opponent’s rule. |

The duel tests anticipation and counterplay. The group fight tests interdependence: cover, interruption, a trip and a strike against the pressure source. The siege tests collective cost: gaining retreat time by sacrificing the main bridge while the enemy still exists. These are separate studies in the same provisional world, not one continuous chronological episode.

Known image-level limits are retained in the independent review: some gravity and ice-contact states disappear between moments; an apparent teleport has extra cyan markers; a horn stream stops too early; a giant’s joint response remains partial. Prompts describe intention, not proof of successful depiction.
