## What remains unresolved

Weapon handedness is the clearest recurring problem in the late duel: Riven sometimes uses the wounded left hand despite the intended right-hand saber. Damage changes location or shape, incidental spectators enter and disappear, and the raised landing is abandoned without a shown descent.

Spell continuity is less reliable than local action. Ilyra sometimes lifts the lance while gravity is meant to continue. The short-step escape shows three cyan markers instead of one clear pair. The horn stream disappears before the horn is cut. Maintained ice contact and the route disappear and return between shots.

Some outcomes remain partial. The siege creature’s joint response does not fully communicate whole-body weight transfer. The group’s exit is still contested. The last war image shows an occupied ice route, water contact and an open gate, so it depicts ongoing evacuation rather than completed clearance. Some planned distant views become larger foreground hero compositions.

Finishing is also fallible. D05 becomes calmer but loses some spray that showed continuing force. D11 has readable shoulder-to-pier pressure and loaded stance, while the instantaneous collision is less convincing than a sustained pin. These distinctions remain in the image notes and independent review; a clean surface is not proof of correct action.

## Useful decisions for your review

1. Read each sequence without captions and note where the action stops making sense or loses excitement.
2. Judge **power appeal** separately from **causality**: an idea worth developing can still be poorly demonstrated in an image.
3. Identify the faces, roles and specific combat moments you want to follow longer. None are automatically selected by this delivery.

The production priority after your review should be reliable action continuity: exact weapon ownership, active spell contact, injury markers and geography across several linked moments. Keep the approved quiet-surface method, protecting directional water, load-bearing contacts and damage cues during every redraw.
