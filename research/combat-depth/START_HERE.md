# Read the fights first

Open [the offline reader](../../docs/combat-depth/index.html). Start with **Read duel**, then **Read group** and **Read war**. Captions are off by default so you can judge what the artwork communicates on its own. These are three separate studies in a provisional shared world.

The delivery has **24 new compositions**: a twelve-moment duel, six-moment group fight and six-moment siege. All **54 native attempts** remain in history, including the underlying primaries, six structural repairs and twenty-four source-only surface redraws. Every returned image is newly generated for this round.

Use **Gallery** or **Browse** to judge individual images. Drawing, character, impact, power, causality, recognition, texture, setting and overall appeal are independent. N/A is available. Comfort, notes and shortlist are separate. No choices are prefilled. The displayed finish is a production selection, not your approval of a character or power.

Use **Save / load** to export your choices and notes. Imports must match the dataset and actual image bindings. Your earlier combat selections remain intact in **Previous choices**, separate from this round. **Reference library** holds the original full Nightglass benchmark and recurring-subject references.

Click an image for full-image viewing or native pixels. Compare images, follow recurring subjects, and open **References & image history** for every exact prompt, actual input reference, frozen call plan and retained attempt. Optional story captions describe intended beats; **AI technical observations** identify actual shortcomings separately from your own notes.

Read [results and remaining weaknesses](RESULTS.md), the [reusable combat workflow](COMBAT-WORKFLOW.md), and [magic rules and counters](MAGIC-AND-COUNTERS.md).

For sharing, use the portable ZIP rather than copying only the HTML. Extract it completely, then open its gallery launcher. The archive includes native images and references that are intentionally outside Git. [Verification and additive restoration instructions](asset-bundle/README.md) explain the integrity checks; final archive and restoration receipts are stored beside the ZIP.
