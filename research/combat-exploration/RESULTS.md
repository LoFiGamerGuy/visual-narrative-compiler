## Next decision

Shortlist only characters you would actually follow. Rate drawing treatment and weapon/power separately. Reject every character if necessary; liking a style does not obligate liking its face. Notes such as “good face, wrong body,” “want this weapon on another lead,” or “too regal, need a harder fighter” are more useful than an overall score.

After your selections, test a small coherent scene using those actual choices: one chosen lead, a deliberately chosen opponent, one compatible existing location and a visible combat exchange. Establish face, weapon handling and a first power upgrade before expanding monsters, factions or story canon. Another broad random character round is not automatically warranted.
