# Reusable sequence workflow

Use the saved texture-refinement guide unchanged as the finishing baseline. Preserve each source's own drawing treatment. Describe the quiet ground and broad material shapes in the primary prompt, then inspect the actual result; adjectives alone do not reliably protect the surface gains.

For linked encounters, keep an explicit state ledger: anatomical weapon hand, each support limb, one continuous weapon construction, mark location, damage already incurred, current contact, and what the next beat must inherit. Give the generator only the current beat's damage state. Including later injuries in the opening contract caused premature damage here. The corrected prompt preparation removes future-state sentences without changing the frozen plan or historical calls.

Separate recognition references from action requirements. Earlier compositions help identity but can anchor camera position and repeat a pose; Iron Orchard's requested overhead roll stayed close to the opening angle. A stated camera instruction is not evidence that the resulting camera changed.

Before promoting an image, trace the cause through contact and reaction at native size, then read the full sequence at phone width with captions hidden. For redirected magic, follow an uninterrupted source-to-contact-to-target path. For marked abilities, trace the same anatomical limb across frames. For grapples, identify support, lever and displaced center of mass. Record uncertainty rather than inventing an occluded connection.

Repair the most consequential foundational defect within the frozen budget, using one repair per candidate at most. Compare every repair to its source for regressions. Here the opening cape, duplicate spearhead, opening coat and four-legged creature improved; the marked-arm repair failed, and the Nightglass transfer repair remained partial. A returned edit is not automatically a successful edit.

Use a source-only surface finish where actual texture competes with the focal subject. Protect meaningful damage and contact accents explicitly; ordinary scuffs and storytelling damage must be distinguished. Compare source and finish at native size and in matched phone views. This round retained two finishes only as alternatives because the tool weakened a protected impact cue or erased weapon damage. Do not call such a finish fully successful or quietly discard its source.

Keep prompts, actual references, source hashes, call receipts and every attempt. Unknown model/seed parameters remain unknown. Do not retrofit the first four repair receipts with evidence hashes they did not contain at call time; their retained evidence files have inspection-time bindings, clearly distinguished from pre-call bindings. New preferences remain separate from prior exact owner exports and from AI display choices.

For a future approved sequence test, prioritize explicit anticipation and one observable power rule per exchange before adding more world detail. Include enough beats for an opponent to discover and exploit the rule, then show a consequence that changes the next available action. This is a workflow recommendation, not an approved cast, power or next generation batch.
