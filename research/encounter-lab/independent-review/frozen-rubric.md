# Encounter Lab independent rubric — frozen before art

The review prioritizes appealing, consequential encounters in each source style. Correct handling is necessary evidence where it matters; it does not replace felt movement, acting or interesting reversals.

**Appeal and each drawing style.** Faces act, silhouettes carry personality, and the actual line, paint, value and material treatment remain recognizable across the four moments. Judge Black Petal, Iron Orchard, Soft Thunder and Sunbreak in their own systems; do not impose Nightglass or assume an intended style label proves realization.

**Four-beat behavior and felt impact.** Setup establishes a usable relation; commitment exposes a risk; reversal changes who controls the encounter; consequence shows a specific lasting result or cost. Credit body compression, recoil, balance loss, restraint and altered choices. A grip diagram or burst alone is not an exciting action, and repeated airborne targets are not automatically distinct powers.

**Causality and power rules.** Trace actor, weapon or power source, contact/trigger, target response and consequence from actual pixels. Separate a plausible physical hit from proof of the special rule or timing. Horror rules, grappling pressure, elastic response and power counters should change visible behavior rather than only color the effect.

**Anatomy, handling and scale.** Check force-bearing limbs, body connections, planted support, weapon count/continuity, actual grip and joint orientation. Preserve known laterality, creature construction and relative scale. Distinguish hidden detail from a proven error; intentional radical crops are allowed when the decisive relation remains legible.

**Geography, identity and persistent state.** Follow recurring adults, weapons and threat anatomy through camera changes. Track landmarks, contact sides, travel direction, distances, exits and obstacles. Damage, torn clothing, dropped items, severed supports and changed footing must persist or visibly transition; a new angle cannot silently reverse an injury or restore a spent object.

**Framing and reaction variety.** Use actual close, medium, wide, high/low and distance changes to reveal different information and maintain adult recognition. Reward purposeful reactions and consequences as well as peak impacts. Radical views are assessed for their intended composition without pretending multiple changed variables isolate a style effect.

**Quiet surfaces and source-only finish regression.** Ground reflections, cracks, debris and repeated surface marks must stay subordinate to the event; broad bright patterning can still compete without fine grain. Compare each finish to its exact actual parent at native and matched phone size. Preserve faces, silhouettes, contact, damage, material character, depth and purposeful force cues. Mark partial/reject if calming the image damages these; do not recommend another pass for an already calm image.

**Uninterrupted phone reading.** Inspect actual native art, matched phone parent/return views, and each complete four-frame sequence without explanatory captions first. Record where identity, direction, rule or consequence becomes uncertain across adjacent moments. Then compare to the frozen brief; captions and UI integrity cannot stand in for visible comprehension.

Each finding will be bound to actual native and phone evidence, with parent comparisons for edits. Earlier findings and disagreements remain preserved. Unknown relationships stay uncertain. Owner praise endorses the process globally; it supplies no new image ratings, canon or comfort result.

Scope: five four-frame encounters plus four radical peaks, exactly24 primaries; at most6 structural repairs and24 observed-noise source-only finishes, one of each per image. Reviewer makes no generation or selection changes. The exact plan will receive a separate preflight binding when announced.
