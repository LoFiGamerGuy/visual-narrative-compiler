# Impact and clarity results

This round tests whether stronger body mechanics, larger consequences and more deliberate visual hierarchy improve the combat while retaining the cleaner surface treatment. The 16 compositions contain 8 core Nightglass studies, 4 radical Nightglass alternatives, and4 other-style experiments with new adult combat characters. These are independent studies, not a connected battle sequence or selected canon.

The actual generation ledger contains 29 built-in image-generation calls producing 29 retained natives: 16 primary images, 4 structural repairs and 9 source-only texture finishes. There were no service retries. All used the built-in tool; direct paid API, model, cloud and purchase spend was $0. Tool-reported model, snapshot, seed, usage and billing metadata remain null where unavailable. The unused generation allowance was not spent.

## What the pictures suggest

The clearest improvement is visible reaction: M01's airborne opponent, M02's shield-driven launch, A01's bodies hitting a wall, and Y03's lifted, compressed guard. These convey consequences through bodies rather than relying on particles alone. This is an AI review observation, not evidence that the owner likes them.

V01 preserves the movement mechanism better than the extreme X02 view. X02 has stronger lens proximity but its cyan contact attaches to the saber rather than the intended trailing boot. X01's heavy ink and close acting feel louder than M01, while M01 more clearly shows whole-body recoil. X03's broad negative-space gravity field gives an alternative to conventional glow. These comparisons change several pictorial variables; they are not controlled single-variable experiments.

The other directions retain useful differences: Black Petal's restrained horror and severe character appeal; Iron Orchard's broad painted weight; Soft Thunder's elastic gesture and confident cartoon expression; Sunbreak Lancer's warm, crisp illustrative color. Each uses its own original anchor. Nightglass character or finish images were not passed into these other-style primary calls.

The source-only finishing step again provides visible gains, particularly on the ground in Y02–Y04 and the petals/paving in Y01. Initial low-noise prompting alone did not consistently achieve this. The final finishes are still drawn surfaces; they are not a promise of perfectly textureless or entirely matte material.

## Remaining weaknesses

- S01 gains a more visible lowered body and quieter surfaces, but the shield-to-paw load path and intended folded support remain uncertain. Its scale is not the requested extreme ratio. A broad reflection remains at the right of the floor.
- S02 restores the grounded lance and sword-to-ramp connection. Kellan's far hand remains open beside the guard, and the manta's deflection is not fully demonstrated. The complex evacuation remains visually busy despite surface reduction.
- X02's boot-to-plane mechanism is unresolved. X04 conveys enormous falling mass, but the small shield's causal contact is unclear; it remains cinematic illustration rather than the proposed woodcut abstraction.
- Y01's repair separates the shadow-tendon ends and bends the supporting joint, while changing the former claw into a broader fingered support. The finish preserves this actual revised anatomy. Grip laterality is not fully certain.
- Y03 shows a convincing large hit but cannot prove its described delayed second impulse in one frame. Y04's return-fire path remains directionally ambiguous.
- Several solutions still rely on launching an opponent backward or upward. This set does not yet establish a varied long duel, tactics, counters, or a finished sequential fight.

## Review and decisions

Every actual native and corresponding phone presentation is reviewed before final display selection. Independent review records retain their own observations and disclose when lead cues were supplied; this was not uniformly blind review. Repairs and finishes are assessed against their source, including regressions, rather than declared successful merely because one requested change appeared.
