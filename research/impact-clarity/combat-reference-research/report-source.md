# Combat reference study — force, motion and visual restraint

Audience: the project owner choosing a personal combat-art direction. Date: 9 September 2026 UTC. Scope: sixteen specific visual excerpts from eight official or licensed public manhwa readers/previews, with independent blank choices. This is a compact visual study, not a ranking of series or an exhaustive history of manhwa action.

The most promising next step is to let selected peak moments become more graphic. Preserve recognizable faces and equipment, but allow the surrounding world to turn into broad values, a directional smear or a dominant power shape. Do not preserve equal detail everywhere merely because an illustration can render it. This is my inference from the inspected artwork, not a measured audience result.

The Boxer examples are particularly useful for the present problem: a glove visibly compresses the face against a nearly black background, while an overhead evasion separates a committed arm from a folding body against white. The official series credits JH. [The Boxer, official WEBTOON series](https://www.webtoons.com/en/sports/the-boxer/list?title_no=2027).

The God of High School adds a planted throw and a body travelling horizontally; GOSU contrasts a close impact with separated figures, then shows a deliberately extreme rotary smear. These are different possible solutions, not a request to copy their characters or entire drawing treatments. Credits are Yongje Park for the former and Giun Ryu / Mun Jeong Hoo for the latter. [The God of High School](https://www.webtoons.com/en/action/the-god-of-high-school/list?title_no=66), [GOSU](https://www.webtoons.com/en/action/gosu/list?title_no=1099).

Solo Leveling’s public Volume 3 preview offers a creature evasion organized by sharp diagonals and a close tooth/face sequence. The publisher credits Chugong and DUBU (REDICE STUDIO). The selected locators are preview image indices 013 and 015, not guessed printed page numbers. [Yen Press / Ize Press, Solo Leveling Volume 3](https://yenpress.com/titles/9781975336523-solo-leveling-vol-3-comic).

Tower of God supplies two distinct power questions: an immense black sweep dwarfing a fighter, and a recognizable family of large orbital forms. Both come from the verified English Season 2, Episode 304, and are credited to SIU. Neither excerpt by itself proves a complete source-to-target action chain. [Tower of God, official episode](https://www.webtoons.com/en/fantasy/tower-of-god/season-2-ep-304/viewer?title_no=95&episode_no=385).

Jungle Juice adds a rescue release and a steep creature dive. Omniscient Reader adds radial collision and a close blade/face relationship. Doom Breaker adds bodily cost and a large single-axis power event. These deliberately include dense or luminous examples so the owner can reject the texture while valuing the event—or reject the package entirely. Credits are Hyeong Eun / JUDER; singNsong / UMI / Sleepy-C; and Blue-Deep respectively. [Jungle Juice](https://www.webtoons.com/en/action/jungle-juice/list?title_no=2480), [Omniscient Reader](https://www.webtoons.com/en/action/omniscient-reader/list?title_no=2154), [Doom Breaker](https://www.webtoons.com/en/action/doom-breaker/list?title_no=3197).

Compared with actual current M01-P and V02-P, the project has improved displacement and committed poses. M01-P shows a lifted opponent; V02-P makes a leaping figure and large threat readable. The remaining opportunity is unequal visual emphasis: architecture, costume, ice and debris still compete for resolution. The references suggest selective compression, more dangerous proximity, more varied framing and more directional abstraction, not simply bigger effects. This comparison covers two current native images only, not the completed round.
