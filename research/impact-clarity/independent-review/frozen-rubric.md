# ImpactClarity independent rubric — frozen before outputs

The leading question is whether the image makes an action or power feel forceful and worth seeing. Grip correctness alone does not answer it. Quiet ground is a primary requirement, including broad reflection patterns that remain distracting after microtexture is reduced.

**Felt impact and movement.** Lead with the visible body event: acceleration, compression, recoil, loss of balance, redirected weight, interrupted action or a costly landing. Trace source→contact/near-miss→target consequence. Distinguish preparation, sustained pressure and aftermath from an instantaneous blow. A correct grip, touching endpoint or glow alone is insufficient.

**Exciting composition and power identity.** Assess a purposeful action silhouette, line of force, depth/overlap, camera placement and large shape contrast. A power should change a specific physical relationship in an interesting recognizable way, not merely add a generic beam or aura. Extreme camera or exaggerated pose must still make source and target understandable.

**Quiet ground first.** Judge the floor/water as a major value-and-shape field, not just microtexture. Repeated bright reflections, tile seams, puddle bands, sparkles and debris must not compete with faces/contact/trajectory. Broad high-contrast shiny bands can remain noisy. Preserve necessary footing, material and depth; do not reward blank, flat or plastic ground.

**Purposeful sparse effects.** Effects should show direction, speed, pressure, impact or material change with a few clear shapes. A quiet area should let the event read. Do not accept an effect veil over unchanged target posture, or remove force-bearing spray/trails merely to reduce density.

**Adult appeal, anatomy and scale.** Judge expressive adult faces and character charisma separately from mechanical compliance. Verify loaded limbs, believable joints, relevant hands/weapon continuity and no accidental extra people. Minor distortion matters in proportion to its effect on action/identity; stylized foreshortening is not automatically an error. Giant size requires architectural/human anchors and credible mass response.

**Native and matched phone evidence.** Inspect every actual native attempt before root preferred verdict, then actual390px phone rendering. Compare paired core/radical art at equal art width and equivalent framing display; record source IDs/SHA and stage. Report what is visible, uncertain or absent; do not infer motion, comfort or acceptance from captions, labels, metadata or image statistics.

**Fair comparisons and bounded corrections.** Evaluate core and radical variants on the same impact, ground, acting and comprehension questions. Radical difference alone is neither a win nor a failure. If composition, character, effect and treatment all change, describe the combined result without claiming a causal isolation. Keep concrete gains and regressions; source-only F must not be credited with structural fixes.

## Pre-image watchlist

- Static action poster with correct grips but unchanged target torso/center of mass.
- Bright floor/reflection pattern dominating the panel after nominal texture reduction.
- Repeated generic cyan/white beam or ring substituting for distinctive power behavior.
- Overcrowded cast/FX making a contact look dramatic but untraceable.
- Radical camera judged more generously because it is new, or penalized solely for departing from the baseline.
- Source-only finish weakening the very spray, trail or deformation that carried movement, as in prior D05.
