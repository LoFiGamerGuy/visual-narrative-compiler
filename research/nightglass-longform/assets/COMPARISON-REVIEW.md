## Production recommendation

There is no fully successful six-panel method here. C is the strongest demonstrated starting point for contained dialogue, object transfer, and short sequences with simple state changes: it preserves more of the intended sequence with fewer calls and remains inviting to read. That is a limited production choice, not evidence that whole-page generation solves continuity. Its action-critical landing is not ready for use as proof of the skill's mechanics.

Carry forward C's compact grouping for suitable scenes, while restaging the landing before further chapter art. Give braking and the sleeve snag separate readable instants, with the full pommel-to-fixed-post connection unobscured and a close enough view of cloth caught on the tooth. A's repaired landing provides an observed successful contact arrangement; it does not justify silently treating A's whole sequence as corrected. Keep consequential damage visible in a subsequent simple pose instead of relying on a prompt inventory alone.

Do not make B the default based on its extra references: those inputs preserved some geography but did not prevent reversed hands or an impossible extra hand. Continue chapter production with the disclosed limits and the revised storyboard, without another comparison loop. Final longform selection and actual chapter reading review remain the integrator's responsibility.
