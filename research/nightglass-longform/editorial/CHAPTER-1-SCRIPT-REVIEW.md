In 18, freeze the completed state—Pell's harness remains worn, its disconnected safety tether remains with the lift, his hand grips Aren's free left forearm. Aren wants stable paid work and a private room. Pell wants the forgotten community served. Sera wants competent, documentable service but cannot personally override route closure. Ossa wants receipts the upper city must acknowledge. These wants can cooperate without becoming identical. Keep Sera's filed-but-ignored reports: they prevent the gate scene from reducing her to an arbitrarily cruel examiner. Give Ossa concrete postal language and Pell maintenance language; leave Aren's sincere ambition unmocked.

The progression is earned through the rescue overload, chosen for the next actual problem, and demonstrated twice: rough arrival then controlled delivery. Its attention cost and inability to strike while braking create an understandable tradeoff. The badge should remain provisional access before the first paid receipt and should work immediately; do not cancel either that access or his pay at the hook.

The six-beat comparison is a fair story-relevant test after the elbow correction. Keep exact transfer, stow, descent, snag, release and inspection comparable across methods. Also, panel 41's shared bread conveys intimacy but does not itself establish that Pell and Ossa are siblings; treat that relationship as provisional background unless dialogue makes it explicit.
