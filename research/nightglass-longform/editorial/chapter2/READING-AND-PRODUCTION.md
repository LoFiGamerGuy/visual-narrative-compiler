## Voice and charm

- **Aren:** Requests help directly, notices what recipients need, protects entrusted objects and occasionally enjoys an uncomplicated purchase. His most important lines are sincere: “I can take your answer,” “Something I can bring back,” and “I want it.” His limiting choice is his own “Next trip.”
- **Sera:** Records the difference between observation and inference. She is precise without shaming the neighbors or pretending her old reports were adequate proof. She gives Aren permission to take one safe breath, not a taunting test.
- **Ossa:** Names, doors, wages and the next morning's work. She values a corrected living address over the authority of her own old register. Practical generosity comes with understandable terms.
- **Pell:** Latches, straps, shoulders and supply lines. He sees what supports a body or a route. His help does not secretly grant magical lifting strength.
- **Maret:** Specific requests, a clear decision and tangible work. She is allowed to be useful once the record can support it.
- **Neighbors:** Rusk wants his daughter to hear his reply; Hessa wants her actual door recorded and offers food; Dain wants a sleeping household left quiet. They are not three interchangeable information dispensers.
