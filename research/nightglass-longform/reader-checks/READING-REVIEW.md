## Remaining art limitations

These are disclosed visual limitations, not hidden by dialogue or treated as proof of owner preference:

- The amount and exact silhouette of Aren's hem damage vary across new and reused art; the heavily shredded rescue hem becomes smaller notches in later scenes. The later left-elbow tear is much more deliberately tracked.
- The sheathed weapon changes hip in some conversation/reward art, notably panel43. Its rejected repair corrected the hip but erased the elbow tear. The selected action frames preserve the more consequential right-hand weapon/left-hand rescue geometry.
- The hybrid C handoff/stow and A descent/stop change rendering and gate scale. The gate still reads as a blocked route and later an opened entrance, but its architecture is not perfectly stable.
- The silver broken-circle seal varies in gap/shape, and incidental accessories such as Pell's belt ring and Ossa's reading glasses appear. The envelope itself remains recognizable through the job-offer handoff and postal delivery.
- Wide action figures and the shallow aftermath inserts make fine costume details small at 390px. Native inspection supports those details; the phone reading is strongest at the level of contact, action and emotion.

No additional artwork generation was justified by the complete reading. The one subsequent Pell wording correction above improves the causal setup without changing art. A completed illustrated chapter is not a claim of perfect continuity.
