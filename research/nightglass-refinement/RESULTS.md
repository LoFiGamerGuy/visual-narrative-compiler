The clearest advance is a workable surface-redraw approach. In all four matched studies, broad material planes replace much of the crack mosaic, tiny reflection noise and stray marks without losing the recognizable face, silhouette, creature weight or inhabited depth. Root and independent reviewer inspected actual native images and matched phone views. The gate supported expansion technically; it did not establish owner comfort or prove texture alone caused the difference, because brightness and reflection grouping also changed.

S05, the snowy shelter, is the strongest new scene demonstration of that approach: its repaired wind becomes broad flowing shapes while Neris, Dax and their protective moment remain. S03's warm monolith also builds scale from large architectural shapes and light. S02 adds a gentle interaction carried by a face and open hand; S04 stages a readable spear counter above a mouth-source water blast. Neris, Kellan and Elowen have clearer face/costume designs and functional held implements. Their equipment and abilities recur in new actions to test consistency. These are provisional story ingredients, never AI-selected canon.

The main limitation is uneven transfer into fresh compositions. Water, clouds, salt ledges, creature mottling and some ink faces repeatedly regained fine texture despite quieter prompts and a reviewed finish reference. M01's throat vein/crack network, M02's fronds/reflections and C04's scratch treatment remain conspicuous. Several character backgrounds are more elaborate than the quiet-board brief. Good phone silhouettes do not mean the eye-strain concern is solved.

Repairs used the entire bounded allowance, one each on G01/G02/G03/S01/A04/S05. The equipment retries remove unwanted full characters/settings; G02 also removes blade contact. Residuals remain: G01's forearm fades mid-frame, G02's hands overlap closely, and G03's lower string/wrist route is unclear. S01 gains a real lance grip but retains granular salt surfaces. A04-R1 is displayed for reduced spray and mottling only: its shield grip still faces the creature while the arm approaches from the other side, and the crescent is narrower. That construction repair failed; it is not an accepted action. S05 provides substantial texture relief with a more stylized wind treatment.

A02 shows a released arrow at the creature's foot but does not clearly show the anchor or an arrested forward charge. A03 gives a bright source and dim target, but the dagger runs along the beam and can read as interception or emission. S04's forward landing and hidden hindquarters are not fully demonstrated.
