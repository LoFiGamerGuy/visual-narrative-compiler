# New Nightglass refinement — design rationale

Twenty genuinely new images extend the liked city into salt flats, reservoir shores, a bright monolith, a snowy ridge and a broad arena. Four matched surface-only studies are root-owned and separate. The twenty contain 17 Nightglass, two Sunbreak and one Floodline; adding four Nightglass studies gives exactly 21/2/1 across 24 primaries.

Quiet surfaces do not mean empty scenes. Large terrain and architectural masses, atmosphere, deliberate silhouettes, facial acting and a small number of reflections carry richness. Pervasive cracks, facets, cloth grain, hairline etching and glitter are excluded. Adult face appeal, clean tailoring, protective armor and weapon function remain priorities.

Riven carries his same Seam Saber in two new full scenes, S01 and S06. Senn supplies a decisive airborne counter in S04, not another ready pose or a retry of old spear contact. Iria receives a gentle wildlife moment. Dax appears as a protected partner rather than repeating his old punch. Neris and Elowen also anchor complete scenes; all four new adults have a combat power and one signature weapon.

Daystring now leads with combat control: an arrow fixes a real terrain contact point and briefly arrests a charged forefoot. Quiet Harbor has a close shield counter as well as shelter. Four abilities each specify visible source, action, target and consequence, with bounded limits and growth in separate copy. Existing bad action details are not smuggled into the new budget as corrections.

Design references and drawing-treatment references are explicitly separate. WC references preserve subjects and equipment while approved simplified proof guides rendering; no imported old texture or extra cast. Planned actual reference counts are one to four, below the five-reference cap. Dependencies are acyclic and await registered hashes. Root alone owns the proof gate, production freeze and image generation.
