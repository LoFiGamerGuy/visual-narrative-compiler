## What constitutes a material gain

The same focal subject and event should become easier to separate from surroundings because fine chips, cracks, tiny reflections, repeated window specks, stray strands and similar marks compete less. Compare actual mark grouping and visual hierarchy, not just brightness, blur, edge counts or numerical density. A local or barely perceptible cleanup is not a material whole-image gain. Describe exactly which surfaces quiet down and whether the intended focus benefits.

Retain selective sharp contours and drawing where they carry meaning: adult eyes/mouth/hair construction, body silhouette, fabric seams that explain tailoring, plate boundaries, weapon grip/guard/endpoints, creature joints, architectural edges and scale cues. Texture simplification should reorganize broad shapes and highlights, not erase the drawing or smear it. Do not reward empty, flat, sterile, plastic or generic block-like results simply for containing fewer marks.

## Preservation and regression checks

- **Adult face appeal and identity:** preserve recognizable features, age impression, expression and personality. Group hair strands without making the hairstyle a helmet or removing eye/mouth intent. In distant/rear views, preserve the visible identity cues rather than inventing facial proof.
- **Silhouette and anatomy:** preserve the complete attached body, pose, actor/limb counts and contact with its support. Do not lose dark limbs into a single mass or simplify muscles into disconnected armor.
- **Shape and material identity:** distinguish cloth, metal, opaque black glass, pale living throat/jaw, wet stone and atmospheric distance through grouped values, edge behavior and selected reflections. Broad smooth areas may be appropriate; uniform gloss/plastic or unmodeled flat fill is a regression if it removes those distinctions.
- **World depth and scale:** retain foreground/middle/distant separation, route geometry, perspective, atmospheric layering and enough window/floor/street rhythm to support inhabited scale. Fewer tiny windows is allowed; deleting the evidence of a city or its immense vertical/creature scale is not a gain.
- **Action and handling:** preserve the same event, weapon topology and hand relationships. A changed grip, absent seam, broken blade, relocated landing, altered stance or additional effect counts as a redraw regression. An inherited uncertain relationship remains inherited uncertainty, not a new failure or a claimed repair.
- **Focus and light:** reduce competing internal contrast and isolated sparkles while preserving the intentional focal lights, shadows and color relationships. Global brightening/desaturation or depth-of-field blur alone is not evidence of successful texture redesign.
- **Composition:** retain image ratio/framing, silhouette placement, important endpoints and major scene masses. Minor redraw differences need not be exact pixels, but material staging/subject changes undermine the matched comparison.
