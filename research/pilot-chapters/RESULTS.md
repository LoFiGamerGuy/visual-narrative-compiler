# Five pilot chapters: production results

The delivery advances from isolated action moments to five complete, sixteen-panel short stories. Each has new artwork, editable dialogue, a resolved encounter and a next question. The source reader starts with Nightglass and offers direct tabs for the other four chapters. Nightglass Courier, Black Petal, Soft Thunder, Red Current and Floodline Detective remain separate provisional series. The original Nightglass is still its path's leading benchmark. All nine original directions and all69 Anchor Return attempts remain available as78 saved image options; earlier worktrees, including the cover kit, remain preserved separately.

## Art informs the premise; story directs the panels

This hybrid is the active route. An appealing anchor supplies a world promise and drawing language. A compact script supplies motivation, a decision and consequences before panel generation. The80 new compositions serve those beats. Existing art is reference material, not an assembled substitute for sequential storytelling. Exact dialogue stays outside image generation and remains editable.

[Read the pilots](../../docs/pilot-chapters/index.html) before reading detailed production notes if you want the reveals in order. [Series paths](editorial/production-review/SERIES-PATHS.md) describes five possible continuing engines without declaring canon.

## What the images demonstrate

Nightglass combines vertical-city wonder with a costly rescue and a personal delivery hook. Black Petal uses a false familiar voice, a creature rule and a choice to protect a living person. Soft Thunder turns failed strength into observation and partnership. Red Current makes a winning counterattack leave a physical debt. Floodline connects a failed clue-reading attempt to rescue and a larger investigation.

The chapters alternate establishing views, close acting, clues, decisive action and aftermath. Recurring faces and costume silhouettes are generally recognizable. Dialogue was checked in a continuous phone reader, with placement changes where faces, hands or speaker ownership conflicted. These are short pilots, not full commercial-length episodes, and familiar author read times are not independent human comprehension evidence.

Source-only finishing often improves focus by grouping water, paving, clothing and vegetation marks. It does not eliminate all texture or guarantee preservation. RC15's finish enlarges and brightens an existing dim red eye, making the submerged threat more explicit, and regularizes the red wrist damage. The reader retains its original; both attempts remain available. Other finishes retain qualified imperfections, including Black Petal's altered coat-tail notches, Red Current's coarser debris in RC11, and Floodline's shift toward cleaner contours, strongest in FL04.

## What still fails

Distributed continuity is the strongest remaining limitation. The recurring silhouettes are more stable than anatomical hand ownership, exact power states, small objects and damage. Nightglass's tether can originate from the wrong part of the blade; the repaired large-creature panel retains a remote cyan segment with uncertain connectivity. Soft Thunder releases a small puff from the wrong glove after charging the other; its repaired final ride inherits a footwear change. Red Current changes glove state and the key's appearance or position between some panels. Floodline's memory image conflicts with the stated no-ripple rule, and the C implement does not consistently demonstrate two-sided jaw bracing. Attractive action does not establish those mechanics.

The final repairs improve specific story necessities without becoming complete structural successes: Nightglass restores the lift-car roof, Red Current removes a duplicate rival, Soft Thunder restores riding the free ram, and Floodline reduces two stacked hooks to one C. All regressions and adjacent-panel conflicts remain disclosed. Scripts were not rewritten to excuse those defects.

Story limitations also differ. Red Current needs a stronger personal reason to want its key. Soft Thunder's destination is inviting but less urgent than the other hooks. The five leads sometimes share a dry-quip rhythm; relationship-specific dialogue will help distinguish future chapters further. No owner ratings have been inferred from these observations.

## Measured work and boundaries

There are135 new native attempts:80 story primaries,5 cast-sheet primaries,10 structural repairs (including2 sheet repairs), and40 source-only finishes. Each image received at most one structural repair and one finish. All135 returned artwork; no no-art retries were spent. Three outdated queued inputs were administratively withheld before a tool call, preserved in history and later submitted with audited current inputs; they are not image-generation attempts or failures. Prompts for uncalled panels were revised after early evidence, with every input version retained. The frozen story plan and exact copy did not change.

All image creation and editing used the built-in image tool. Direct paid API, model-purchase, cloud and hired-labor spend was$0. Unavailable model snapshot, seed, usage and billing details remain unknown. Editorial and visual reviewers are AI agents, not recruited human readers. Editable geometric controls assisted a few action panels; they are not hand-corrected final art or proof of anatomy.

## Recommended next production decision

Read all five pilots and respond separately to protagonist, story curiosity, drawing treatment and individual panel appeal. Keep all five paths available. Before expanding a chosen path into a longer episode, test a short adjacent action chain using the preceding selected panel alongside the current-state brief and inspected cast reference. Track hands, charge, objects and damage explicitly and inspect the continuous result. That mechanism is a proposed next test, not a demonstrated solution. Preserve the texture method, but require source/result checks for reveals and medium changes as well as calmer surfaces.
