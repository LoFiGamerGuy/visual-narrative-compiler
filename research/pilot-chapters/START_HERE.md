## Art and story

Use both, in a deliberate order: let the artwork establish the appeal of a possible series, write a small complete story, then draw new panels for that story. Here all eighty story panels are new. Older artwork informs taste and drawing treatment; it is not assembled into a comic or automatically appointed as the cast.

The frozen scripts, current-state notes and camera choices are in [the plan](../../production/pilot-chapters/plan.json) and [editorial sources](editorial/). Dialogue remains outside generation so words and placement can be edited without repainting the artwork. [Workflow](WORKFLOW.md) and [lessons from actual production](FIRST-PANEL-LESSONS.md) distinguish demonstrated changes from proposed next tests.

## Reading and responding

Read the five short chapters before browsing their production notes if you want the reveals in story order. Chapter reactions are independent from panel reactions. Notes, shortlist and export/import remain available; new choices start blank. Earlier exported choices are kept intact and separate. Displaying an edited attempt never supplies a rating on your behalf.

The useful next decisions are which protagonist you want to follow, which world invites another chapter, whether the drawing treatment feels right, and where a story confused or lost you. You can like the setting and reject the plot, or like a character without liking their equipment.

## Production limits

The reader and full stories are reviewable pilot material. Distributed continuity issues remain in equipment hands, power states and precise contacts. The review reports preserve those defects even when a structural edit or source-only texture finish improves the displayed panel. Attractive art, readable dialogue and passing code tests are different forms of evidence; none substitutes for your response as a reader.

Exact prompts, actual reference bindings, call records and every returned native attempt are retained under [production](../../production/pilot-chapters/). Original-image and attempt-history links in the reader expose those records. Known direct paid spend is $0; unavailable model snapshot, seed, usage and billing metadata remain unknown. The completed generation ledger contains135 native attempts:80 story panels,5 cast sheets,10 structural repairs and40 texture finishes. See [results and remaining weaknesses](RESULTS.md), [possible series paths](editorial/production-review/SERIES-PATHS.md), and the portable-package receipts for verification.
