# Five pilot chapters — offline reader

Extract the entire ZIP into a new folder, then double-click START_HERE.cmd on Windows or open docs/pilot-chapters/index.html. Keep all folders together. Viewing requires no account, server, installation or network access.

Choose one of five original sixteen-panel stories. Lettering is a separate editable layer over native artwork; switch it off to inspect the art. Panel and chapter reactions start blank and remain independent. Export your choices to keep them. Earlier worlds and all preserved Anchor Return attempts remain separate possibilities, with exact history and source links.

Original art, prompts, calls, scripts, references and prior choices are under production/pilot-chapters. Current reports and editable reader code are under research/pilot-chapters. The package includes the textual texture-refinement guide; it does not include unrelated old galleries or runtime caches.

For hash verification, safe additive restoration and optional offline rebuilding with standard-library Python3, see research/pilot-chapters/asset-bundle/README.md. Final reports describe known story, anatomy, continuity and lettering limitations; displayed images do not imply owner approval or canon.
