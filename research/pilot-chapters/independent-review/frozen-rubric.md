# Frozen independent pilot-comic rubric

Before new scripts or outputs, actual original NG/BP/ST/RC/FL anchors and five representative AR natives were inspected. The review judges a complete readable comic, with art appeal and story causality assessed separately.

**Emotional investment.** An identifiable person wants something specific, encounters a personal obstacle and reveals personality through behavior or a relationship. Attractive design and stated lore alone do not establish investment.

**Choice, change and ending.** The chapter has a legible initiating problem, a consequential character choice, an earned local resolution or meaningful changed situation, and a specific next question. A cliffhanger must not substitute for completing the promised pilot conflict.

**Dialogue and subtext.** Voices differ; lines express intention, tension or relationship rather than repeating visible action. Read the exact final copy in order. Captioned claims cannot rescue invisible contact, damage, escape or rule mechanics.

**Visible action and powers.** Trace source, contact or intervention, target reaction and consequence. Credit felt movement, anticipation, counter and cost, including expressive deformation. Correct grip alone is not exciting action; sparks alone are not contact proof.

**Continuity.** Track recurring face/body/costume, anatomical hand and equipment, actor counts, planted feet/support, screen geography, scale, injury and damaged props through adjacent panels. Record inherited and newly introduced contradictions separately.

**Original medium and appeal.** Compare to actual original five anchor pixels, not labels or latest polished derivative. Preserve face appeal, silhouette, selective ink/brush character, material and world depth. NG leads overall reference; other four retain their own treatments.

**Composition and momentum.** Camera distance, framing, reaction closeups, environment, silence and impact serve a distinct story beat. Avoid repeated full-body confrontation posters, routine scene resets and eight panels of similar poses expanded to sixteen without new information.

**Ground and finishing.** Judge actual native and matched phone source/finish pairs. Quiet pervasive ground/reflection/foliage detail while preserving material, cast shadows, damage and contact deformation. Reject or qualify a calmer finish that changes construction, effect, emotion or defining medium.

**Lettering and phone reading.** Read the actual complete lettered chapter at 390px: speaker/tail attribution, reading order, exact text, contrast, line breaks, balloon collision and scrolling reveal. Faces, hands, contact and important environment remain visible. Full image fit and OCR checks do not prove comfortable reading.

**Evidence and independence.** Bind every actual attempt and before/after finding to image SHA and actual phone capture. Separate observed, ambiguous and absent relationships; preserve disagreement and exposure. No numeric beauty score, invented reader test, individual owner vote, canon or owner approval.

Preflight concerns: Sixteen panels must carry a compact complete story; scripts should not spend most of the budget teaching combat rules before introducing a reason to care. Freeze editable exact lettering and space needs before each image. A late wall of dialogue can obscure the best action even when the raster passes review. New art must stage new story choices and reactions; prior AR compositions may calibrate treatment but cannot silently count as new chapter panels. Inspect every cast-sheet view and insert before propagation. Contradictory hands or duplicate weapons in a supplemental sheet are foundation risks. Reconfirm RC versus FL drawing systems: AR RC became smoother/glossier and lost nib hatching; FL retained stronger coarse brush mass. More quieting is not automatically more faithful. Budget caps are ceilings, not targets. Structural changes and source-only finishing need separate evidence, and no current brief or output is assumed successful.

Global owner praise does not establish cast, canon, individual choices or future acceptance. The frozen rubric will remain unchanged; plan bindings and later observations will be separate.
