## What this round establishes

The strongest scale demonstrations use small, complete figures: the corrected gate arrival, overhead river, high orchard, bridge over a sleeping leviathan, giant reveal and vertical ice crossing. Character recognition at those distances depends on silver hair/petrol clothing/saber, ivory cape/teal/crescent shield, sand jacket/black curls, or copper bob/burgundy/ivory/lance. Faces become useful in the closer shots. The gallery's shared-subject comparisons let you test that continuity without declaring a cast.

The provisional world has more than places to fight. A moving city can change access to homes, crossings and water. T02 explores who pays for that movement; T04 makes a rescue personal; T06 gives the group a moment of exhausted humor. Q01–Q04 tests a threat that may be a captive. These are possible story directions, not a completed chapter or selected canon.

Four combat mechanics receive different applications:

- **Borrowed Horizon — Riven:** a saber-made seam between two visible, supported edges; short displacement can develop toward transporting an ally or redirecting a committed attack. A02 and Q03 imply the traversal more clearly than they prove both endpoints.
- **Quiet Harbor — Neris:** brace a crescent shield against one pressure front, then return it. Broken stance or an open flank releases the stored force. Q02 communicates protection clearly; the direct rigid strike in A03 and outgoing deflection in A07 remain less exact.
- **Deep Winter — Kellan:** blade contact with continuous water forms an ice wedge or route. It cannot freeze distant dry air. A04 and A08 connect the source, implement and result; load-bearing strength remains a proposed rule, not something a still image proves.
- **Downfall Writ — Ilyra:** a planted lance changes weight within a bounded plane, affecting allies as well as enemies. The caster must stay near the planted source. The current images often read as a barrier or rising force ramp rather than a convincing sideways change in gravity. The concept needs a clearer visual consequence before becoming a production mechanic.

## Texture and repair findings

Source-only surface redraws substantially reduce repeated paving speckle, water glitter, tiny hide scales and equally sharp foliage. They preserve richness through large material planes, selective edges, faces, lighting and depth. They are separate from structural corrections. T06 was already sufficiently calm and did not receive a finishing call.

The initial allowance of 12 surface passes underestimated how often new compositions would reintroduce fine texture. A saved, source-bound budget amendment expands only that allowance to 24, while retaining 24 primary compositions and four structural repairs. Every used pass has a recorded visual reason. The original plan and amendment both remain available.

**T03 is an unresolved failure.** Its primary saber visibly kinks. The structural replacement smooths the blade but loses a convincing connection to the gripping hands; its subsequent surface pass inherits that problem. The gallery therefore displays the original with its known flaw and retains both attempted improvements. Cleaner surfaces are not evidence of corrected mechanics.

Most other limitations are narrower: several ensemble shots place the actors much closer than requested; some extra background people appear; T04 uses a handclasp and narrow rail rather than the planned wrist rescue; Q bridge proportions vary across views; the last ankle remnant is less legible than the hanging wall strap. Some finish passes also reinterpret minor background features or lighting, including a moon added in A04 and removed in A06. The source-bound observations describe these rather than marking every edit an unconditional success.

## Decisions to make from the actual gallery

1. Judge whether the cast is recognizable from close acting shots to tiny figures. Rate recognition separately from liking the character.
2. Compare the inhabited moving-city direction with the captive-giant conflict. Identify which situations suggest a story you want to follow; neither is automatically canon.
3. Separate the appeal of a power idea from whether this particular image explains it. The ice-road and shield-defense scenes provide useful clarity benchmarks.
4. Judge the surface finish independently. Broad shapes should feel drawn and rich, without flattening faces, metal, water or organic weight.

## Reuse and provenance

The original native files, every attempt, exact prompts, actual reference paths/hashes and call receipts are in [production/scale-conflict](../../production/scale-conflict/). The original Nightglass image is a review benchmark; the actual image inputs for each call are the ones explicitly recorded in its receipt, usually the recurring character or newly generated scene reference. Do not assume every library reference was supplied to every call.

Use the [saved texture method](../../docs/texture-refinement-kit/GUIDE.md) for future surfaces. A new composition often needs its own finishing pass even when its reference was already quiet. Inspect structural repairs before finishing them; the failed T03 sequence shows why a smoother silhouette alone is insufficient. Use an actual wide scene as a continuity reference when a portrait reference keeps pulling the camera too close.

The portable ZIP includes all new native art and an offline launcher. Git preserves the isolated delivery's source and review evidence; ignored native art is restored from the ZIP. Exact package hashes, extraction/rebuild checks and commit/remote parity belong to the accompanying delivery receipt. No merge or deployment is part of this work.
