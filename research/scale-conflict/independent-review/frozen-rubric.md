## Appeal, staging and variety

Assess the actual drawing’s invitation to keep looking: adult facial appeal and acting, distinctive silhouettes, meaningful composition, lighting, material and world design. A geometrically correct but dull image does not fulfill the request. Record concrete strengths, defects, uncertainty and a strongest counterexample rather than a numerical beauty ranking.

Across the set, check actual camera distance, angle, perspective, group size and composition—not prompt labels. Look for varied close, medium, distant and monumental views rather than repeated foreground pair/background giant arrangements. Recurring adults should remain identifiable through face when visible and through silhouette, posture, hair, costume masses and equipment at distance. Tiny figures need not have portrait detail, but indistinguishable silhouettes or identity swaps must be disclosed.

Huge scale must be visibly grounded by overlap, foreground/middle/background separation, perspective, architectural modules, human landmarks and believable contact with the world. Enlarging a monster against an empty sky is insufficient by itself. Preserve meaningful windows and distant figures where they establish scale; do not misclassify every small mark as noise.

## Action, powers and construction

Trace each intended source → physical action/contact → target → visible consequence from the pixels. Caption or effect glow is not proof of contact. Check connected limbs, actual grip location, continuous equipment, shield front/back, creature limb origins, support, weight, trajectory, clear gaps where pre-contact is intended, and plausible intervening space. Occlusion means uncertainty, not automatically missing anatomy or successful construction.

Powers should have visually specific mechanisms and results, not interchangeable glowing poses. Distinguish what a single still actually shows from inferred movement, causal history or future growth described in copy. Preserve acting, tension and expressive motion while checking mechanics; avoid reducing the review to a checklist of parts.

## Calm surfaces without loss of drawing

Apply the texture-refinement method: rich composition first, then a separate source-only surface redraw when distracting density warrants it. Reward broad intentional value/color planes, selective crisp facial and functional edges, coherent materials and quiet areas. Do not reward blur, plastic skin, erased landmarks, empty cities, bland anatomy or flattened depth. Preserve deliberate stylistic brushwork and meaningful wear.

Compare each texture pass with its actual source at native detail and matched artwork width on a real 390px phone rendering. Check face/expression, body, equipment, camera, scale, color, material and action as carefully as density. Report improvement, partial improvement or regression separately for surfaces and structure. A calmer image with damaged construction is not an overall success. Structural repairs are a separate attempt category; inherited source defects remain recorded.
