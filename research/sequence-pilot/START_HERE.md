# The workflow is implemented; this art route has not earned production

Keep Ember provisionally and replace the weak production stages. The new original 14-panel pilot now exercises a frozen storyboard, source-bound attempts, a strict correction ceiling, independent image observations and editable lettering. The current full-frame generation route still loses important action and state relationships after correction. Stop this route at its spent budget; do not expand it into chapters.

Open the local [research and alternatives report](../../docs/research/sequence-pilot/index.html), [illustrated reader/editor](../../docs/research/sequence-pilot/reader/index.html), or [nine before/after corrections](../../docs/research/sequence-pilot/corrections/index.html). The reader contains rejected draft art deliberately, so the failures remain inspectable. It is not a production-approved comic.

## What was implemented

- One original adult-cast scene, **Three Charges, One Dose**, with 14 frozen beats, exact copy, resource changes, equipment, geography and attempt rules.
- Three original generated reference sheets and 14 editable SVG storyboards. These are new pilot assets; prior work and published comic pages were not uploaded.
- A small Python package that reserves attempts, records exact inputs and hashes, preserves failures, rejects stale evidence, imports hash-bound layout drafts and exports a self-contained local reader.
- A browser editor with separately editable SVG lettering, image-observed protected regions, actual glyph/balloon measurements, phone type checks and synthetic copy-expansion diagnostics.
- Independent AI visual reviews of the original and corrected art. These do not impersonate human reader or owner acceptance.

See the [runbook](../../production/sequence-pilot/RUNBOOK.md), [package commands and schemas](../../src/sequence_pilot/README.md), [frozen plan](../../production/sequence-pilot/plan.json), [initial visual findings](independent-visual-review.md), [corrected findings](corrected-visual-review.md), and [lettering observations](lettering-observations.md).

## What the experiment establishes

The run used 14 primary panel candidates and nine targeted hard-failure retries, plus three reference-sheet calls: **26 in-product image calls, 23 preserved panel candidates, 14 selected drafts, zero beauty variants**. Selection makes a panel visible for review; it does not accept it. No third attempt is permitted for an exhausted panel.

Some corrections repaired concrete mistakes. The handoff now occurs at the west release, the vault has one intact staff, and the aftermath has a physical wall separating the characters from the threat. But the vault still lacks the continuing sweep, Odo moves away from the intended exit, and the right-hand injury becomes a left-hand injury. See the corrected review for the complete disposition, uncertainties and reviewer disagreements. A valid image hash or clean lettering geometry cannot cure those failures.

Definite failures remain in P09, P11, P13 and P14; P07/P08 retain uncertainties. The [machine-readable result](implementation-results.json) includes final selections, 13 logged failed attempts across original and corrected candidates, integrity checks and pending acceptance gates.

This is evidence against scaling this particular supported generation/editing process under the frozen attempt budget. It is not proof that every generative model fails, or a controlled estimate of improvement over the historical Ember art: the new story, boards, references and unknown provider snapshot differ. No numerical claim of premium quality or owner preference was manufactured.

## The next decision

1. **First structural test:** build the gallery, ridge, shutter and cable with explicit room geometry and three cameras, then finish the establishing, contact and escape views. Use original boards and preserve the contact/trajectory decisions through drawing or constrained finishing. Do not begin a complete character-rig system.
2. **First specialized renderer screen:** test a supported NovelAI V5 Full configuration on the four predeclared action beats inside unchanged contextual boards. Record its actual controls; V4.5 Precise Reference is not an established V5 capability. Only a promising screen earns a full 14-panel comparison.
3. **Strongest staffed control:** have a skilled sequential artist finish three difficult action panels with one requested correction and editable sources. No artist has been hired or booked. A layer-decomposition trial is conditional on appealing art whose remaining problem is local repair.

Astra is well suited to editorial planning, implementation and critical review. Its image-input reasoning is distinct from the raster generator.
