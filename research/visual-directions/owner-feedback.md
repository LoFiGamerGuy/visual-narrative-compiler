# Owner direction change

The owner rejected the structural experiment’s new art style, especially its characters, and requested approximately20 radically different character designs across different styles, environments and monsters. This overrides any favorable AI assessment of that art.

The new study reopens visual and character direction. It does not ask the owner to accept the previous cast or preserve its color/costume language. Twenty independent original briefs are frozen in `production/visual-directions/concepts.json`; no previous art is sent as a reference. The names are labels for visual options, not a rewritten serial or new canon.

The owner can prefer a character from one option, drawing language from another, and a world/creature from a third. A shortlist is input to a later coherent design test; it does not certify sequential continuity or production readiness. No direction is the author’s default winner.

Prior generation and correction budgets remain closed. VD-20260907-01 separately authorizes20 primary concept boards and up to6 targeted hard-failure retries, at most one per option. All attempts and unknown provider metadata are retained.
