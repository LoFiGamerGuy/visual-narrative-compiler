The first18/19 pairs failed to transfer enough of their original drawing systems. Four targeted redraws materially recovered bold cartoon contour and refined modeling. They used the actual primary board plus original style reference, replacing the neutral-sheet inputs. That changed conditioning is disclosed in every repair comparison. Cropped creatures and18-B's missing wheel contact remain visible.

These are technical observations, not reports of owner preference or measured human visual comfort. Your discomfort can reject a treatment regardless of its technical strengths.
