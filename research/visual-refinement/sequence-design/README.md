# Frozen sequence: I loosened it.

This is the internally approved six-panel story for the three provisional rendering treatments. It uses the same cast, quiet landing, panel compositions and exact dialogue in every treatment. Provisional style selection is pending; owner art acceptance remains unset.

The irreversible event is a single wooden crank breaking into a mounted stub and Iven’s right-hand remnant. The closed gate does not move when Iven pulls the crank. Mara then turns the iron rim, the gate rises, and the creature passes. The final line claims credit for an action the pictures show Mara completing.

The framing changes from establishment to medium effort, tight break detail, full-body action, wide consequence and medium comic reaction. All cameras stay south of the canal, preserving left-to-right passage. The schematic SVG records blocking and camera sides only; it is not final artwork or proof of generated geometry.
