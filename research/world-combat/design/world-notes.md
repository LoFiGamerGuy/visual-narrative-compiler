# The Verge Districts — exploratory design vocabulary

A sleek vertical city surrounds black-glass seams, with abandoned transit cuts leading into basalt wilderness and buried halls. Intact seams carry useful charge and attract harmless wildlife; broken seams leak predatory pressure. This makes routes, creatures, combat and progression part of the same visual promise without declaring a canonical plot or compulsory party.

Nightglass dominates 18 of 24 images. Three Sunbreak and three Floodline images test the same connected vocabulary under warm terrace light and graphic flooded-depth treatment. This allocation is an experiment, not an owner-selected style winner. Scenes are complete inhabited compositions, not portrait/component boards.

Six adults have distinct faces, silhouettes, expressions and one simple signature weapon apiece. Dax uses the approved older salt-and-pepper/bearded identity; Vale protects through Quiet Oath rather than summoning defeated shadows. Solene has the approved light warm-tan complexion. Complexion is never a review score.

The six scenes separately explore traversal, discovery, confrontation, direct combat, one controlled power impact and a much larger organic threat. S05 contains Dax alone. The Hollow Regent is a living four-legged animal, never a generic robot or upright armored person. S06 keeps both Riven and lance-bearing Senn readable on the foreground high walk.
