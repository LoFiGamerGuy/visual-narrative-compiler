# Conditioning decisions

Before tools-danger prompts were frozen, the lead inspected all nine E1 tool-return images in full. All nine preserve a usable material vocabulary and distinct principal place; none was treated as a full mechanical or owner-acceptance pass. Original style references remain the first inputs and govern drawing treatment. E1 supplies materials and context only.

Visible caveats:01 night floor has wet-looking reflection despite a dry-route brief;02's rope and mill assembly suggests its function without proving a working wind transmission;05's scattered wear/leaf/fruit texture creates a greater detail burden;06 has two counter drying bundles instead of three and a wet-looking everyday floor;13 shows a wider multi-wheel cart than the simple two-wheel brief;15's round handwheel does not establish a square-stem connection for the later key;17's adjustment socket protrudes into a tread;18 includes incidental kitchen items beyond the three main masses;19 adds fine reeds, wet red cloth and small counter shears. No such incidental object is an identity constraint for later cards.

The frozen tools-danger prompts already require independent E2 layout, clear hazards, and exactly three isolated G1 objects. They do not require reproducing every E1 object, mechanism or decorative mark. Independent reviews provide separate evidence and may add limitations. No E1 retry is selected at this stage.

Before people-creatures freeze the lead inspected all18 actual E2/G1 returns. All nine gear cards contain three separated complete objects with recognizable primary grips; they are suitable visual identity references, not engineering validation. Differences retained:15 key has a closed square socket rather than the proposed open jaw;17 level is opened into a triangle;19 shears are slightly open and the jar wax is a broad dripping seam. Character prompts explicitly give inspected gear construction precedence and permit ordinary opening/closing for the carry pose. This is a disclosed interpretation of actual kit assets, not a claim that the preliminary prose was perfectly followed.

E2 caveats remain:06 floating pages contain printed-looking marks;15 is dense at phone scale;18 mill rotor extends beyond the environment framing;19 repeats fine reed texture despite the simplified creature brief. M1 uses each E2 for material/ecological context only, and its isolated-body prompt prohibits scenery, lettering, extra appendages and effect clutter. No E2 or G1 retry is selected before the final primary review.
